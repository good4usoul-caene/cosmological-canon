## Additional Notes

- **Shield of Emotions**: This is not a standard SRD item—effect and attunement should be set by your DM (often something like reaction-based resistance or bonus to emotion-based saves).
- **Potion of Pugilism:** Not standard; DM may grant effect like "gain proficiency in unarmed attacks" or "advantage on Strength (Athletics)" for an hour.
- **Nolzur's Marvelous Pigments:** 500 gp worth per pot/jar; 1 oz covers 100 sq ft. You can paint a door and open it, or paint treasure and it becomes real ("functionally real" per DM fiat).
- **Attunement**: Only one item per character can be attuned per short rest, up to 3 total per character. If attunement is "maybe" or homebrew, check with your DM before assigning.

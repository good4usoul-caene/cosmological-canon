## The Staff of Favor

Mt 27:1-14, Mk 15:1-5
Before daybreak, Jesus  Barabbas was led up to Pilate the governor. The chief priests, elders, teachers of the law, and the whole Sanhedrin brought him, bound, to Pilate.

*Zechariah 11:7-17*

Now Judas came to return his payment, saying "If you think it best, I'll keep my pay; but if not, keep it." But Caiaphas would not receive the thirty pieces of silver.  Judas said, "I have sinned," he said.  "for I have betrayed innocent blood."

"What is that to us?" they replied.  "That's your responsibility."

So, they had left him to fulfill Chapter 11 of Zechariah all on his own.

Judas remembered what Barabbas had said to him, "Whatever you are about to do, do it quickly." And he recalled prophecy, "Throw it to the potter - the handsome price at which they valued me!"

"What potter?" he wondered. "The potter at the house of the Lord?" 

Judas believed that God was everywhere, so anywhere in the temple would do.  So he scattered the thirty coins all about.  

"Now," thought Judas.  "For Breaking the staffs of favor and union.  Breaking the staff of favor means I shall never be able to defend my actions.  I will be hated until the last day.  Breaking the staff of Union means breaking the family bond of Judah (which means praised) and Israel.  The circumcision of the Son of Man will never be praised by Israel, but cursed as Judas' kiss of betrayal.  This is the only way to assure that the bond is broken."  

"But Barabbas assured me the one who eats the flesh, and tastes the blood will be raised on the last day.  At least," he said, "At least I won't have to be the worthless shepherd."  Then he went away and hanged himself.

### Discussion at Pilate's Palace

Pilate came out onto the front steps of his palace, where the chief priests sought audience.

Pilate asked Jesus Barabbas, “Are you the king of the Jews?” 

Jesus Barabbas answered “You have said so.” 

Pilate said, "What?  When?  No I didn't."

The chief priests and elders accused him, but he made no response.

### The Nazarene before Caiaphas

Lk 22:66-71
At daybreak, back at the home of Caiaphas, Jesus of Nazareth was brought before the high priest and questioned, “If you are the Messiah, tell us.”

Jesus of Nazareth responded, “If I tell you, you will not believe me, and if I asked you, you would not answer, but from now on, the Son of Man will be seated at the right hand of the mighty God.” 

They all asked, "Are you then the Son of God?"

He replied, "You say that I am."

Then they said, “Why do we need any more testimony? We have heard it from his own lips.”

By saying this they confessed that they had said Jesus of Nazareth was the Son of God.

### The Nazarene before Pilate

*Lk 23:1, Jn 18:28*
Next, The Jewish leaders took Jesus of Nazareth to the palace of the Roman governor, Pilate, but they did not enter, because they wanted to eat the Passover.

*Lk 23:2,4-7, Jn 18:29-32*
Pilate comes out to the front steps of the palace, again.  Before Pilate, they began to accuse Jesus of Nazareth, saying he opposes payment of taxes to Caesar, and claiming to be a Messiah, a king.

Others disputed the claims.  Saying his actual teaching about taxes, "Give to Caesar's what is Caesar's, and give to God what is God."

So Pilate announced to the chief priests and the crowd, "I find no basis for a charge against this man."

They say, “If he were not a criminal, we would not have handed him over to you.” 

Pilate says, “Take him yourself and judge him by your own law.” 

But they said, “We have no right to execute anyone.”

They said “He stirs up the people all over Judea by his teaching."

Pilate wondered, "If Judea can't stand up to a bit of teaching, it's not built on a very strong foundation, is it?"

So again Pilate says “I find no basis for a charge against him.” 
 
The chief priest says, "But he started in Galilee and comes all the way here.” 

When Pilate heard this, he decided Jesus was not his problem, and sent him to to Herod, whose jurisdiction was Galilee.

### The Nazarene before Herod

*Lk 23:8-12*
As it so happened, Herod was in town for Passover, with a fairly large entourage of his men.

"Ah, Jesus of Nazareth, Son of Mary!  I am so pleased to see you.  I've heard so much about your miracles and teachings.  I'm hoping to see you perform a sign of some sort."

Jesus of Nazareth replied, "I don't do signs.  People come to me with their needs, and their faith heals them."

Herodias is there, and she said, "Oh, but Herod.  I thought of something.  Remember how Mary spoke of that Vintner, Odys?  The one who carried the oar?"

Herod said, "What about it?"

Herodias said, "Oh, well, young Mary, bless her soul, mistook the oar for a winnowing fork."

Herod said, "This sounds familiar.  Where is this from."

Herodias said, "It's from Homer's Odyssey.  When Odysseus visited the land of the dead, Tiresias referred to the oar of his dead friend, Elpenor.  He said, where someone mistakes it for a winnowing fan, there, plant the oar in the ground, and dedicate a temple to Poseidon."

Herod said, "Oh?  And where was this temple built?"

Herodias said, "It's never been done.  Odysseus died and never finished the task."

Herod said, "Well, I have no oar for Jesus to plant into the ground, but we have plenty of crosses."


Then Herod turned Jesus over to his soldiers and the chief priests and teachers, who vehemently mocked and accused him.  

After they were done, Herodias asked him

"So, your body is a temple, is it not?" asks Herod.  "We shall decorate it in the robes of Odysseus, and we would like to see a sign from you."

So they dressed him in an elegant robe, and sent him back to Pilate.

### The Nazarene Returned to Pilate

Mt 27:19 Lk 23:3, Jn 18:33-38

Pilate’s wife warned him "Don't have anything to do with that innocent man, Jesus of Nazareth."

So Pilate agains summoned Jesus of Nazareth after he was dressed by Herod. 

Pilate asked him, “Are you the King of the Jews?” Jesus asked “Is that your idea or did others tell you about me?”

“Am I a Jew?” Pilate asked. “Your own people handed you over. What have you done?”

“My kingdom is not of this world” Jesus answered.

“So you are a king, then!” Pilate declares.

“You say I am a king” Jesus says. “The reason I was born and came into the world is to testify as to the truth. Everyone on the side of truth listens to me.” 

But Pilate retorts, “What is truth?”

### Both Before the Crowd

*Mt 27:15-18, Mk 15:6-10, Lk 23:13-16, Jn 18:38-39*

Pilate came out in front of an assembled crowd before the palace and brought out the two men. Jesus of Barabbas, who had, last night, been in prison with insurrectionists who had committed murder in the uprising, was well known to the crowd here, for his humorous antics in mocking both Roman and Jewish culture.

Barabbas wore his ordinary clothing, but Jesus of Nazareth had been dressed in an elegant robe by King Herod. 
 
Pilate asked, “Should I release Jesus Barabbas, which means "Son of the Father" or Jesus of Nazareth, who is called the Messiah, the King of the Jews.  And he gestured to the one in elegant robes.”

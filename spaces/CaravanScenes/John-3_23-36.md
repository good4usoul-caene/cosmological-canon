## 📖 John 3:27–30 — John’s Humility and the Messiah

| Verse | Greek (Original) | Gloss |
|-------|------------------|-------|
| 27 | Ἀπεκρίθη Ἰωάννης … Οὐ δύναται ἄνθρωπος λαμβάνειν οὐδὲ ἓν ἐὰν μὴ ᾖ δεδομένον αὐτῷ ἐκ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ | John answered … A man can receive nothing unless it has been given him from heaven |
| 28 | ὑμεῖς αὐτοὶ μοι μαρτυρεῖτε ὅτι εἶπον· Οὐκ εἰμὶ ὁ Χριστός, ἀλλ’ ὅτι ἀπεσταλμένος εἰμὶ ἔμπροσθεν ἐκείνου | You yourselves bear witness that I said, “I am not the Christ, but I have been sent ahead of him” |
| 29 | ὁ ἔχων τὴν νύμφην νυμφίος ἐστίν· ὁ δὲ φίλος τοῦ νυμφίου … χαρᾷ χαίρει διὰ τὴν φωνὴν τοῦ νυμφίου | The one who has the bride is the bridegroom; the friend rejoices greatly at the bridegroom’s voice |
| 30 | ἐκεῖνον δεῖ αὐξάνειν, ἐμὲ δὲ ἐλαττοῦσθαι | He must increase, but I must decrease |

### Glyphic Commentary
- **3:27 Receive how God makes you** → Human limitation acknowledged; all gifts are received, not seized.  
- **3:28 John is swift to the plunder** → John confesses he is not Messiah, but the forerunner; his role is preparatory, not possessive.  
- **3:29 The church belongs to the Messiah** → Bride imagery: covenant community belongs to Christ, not to John.  
- **3:30 The Messiah must become greater** → John’s humility glyph: decrease of self, increase of Christ.

## 📖 John 3:31–36 — Testimony from Above

| Verse | Greek (Original) | Gloss |
|-------|------------------|-------|
| 31 | Ὁ ἄνωθεν ἐρχόμενος ἐπάνω πάντων ἐστίν· ὁ ὢν ἐκ τῆς γῆς … λαλεῖ | The one who comes from above is above all; the one from the earth speaks as earthly |
| 32 | ὃ ἑώρακεν καὶ ἤκουσεν, τοῦτο μαρτυρεῖ· καὶ τὴν μαρτυρίαν αὐτοῦ οὐδεὶς λαμβάνει | He testifies to what he has seen and heard, yet no one accepts his testimony |
| 33 | ὁ λαβὼν τὴν μαρτυρίαν αὐτοῦ ἐσφράγισεν ὅτι ὁ Θεὸς ἀληθής ἐστιν | Whoever receives his testimony has certified that God is true |
| 34 | ὃν γὰρ ἀπέστειλεν ὁ Θεὸς τὰ ῥήματα τοῦ Θεοῦ λαλεῖ· οὐ γὰρ ἐκ μέτρου δίδωσιν τὸ Πνεῦμα | The one God sent speaks God’s words, for God gives the Spirit without measure |
| 35 | ὁ Πατὴρ ἀγαπᾷ τὸν Υἱόν, καὶ πάντα δέδωκεν ἐν τῇ χειρὶ αὐτοῦ | The Father loves the Son and has placed everything in his hands |
| 36 | ὁ πιστεύων εἰς τὸν Υἱὸν ἔχει ζωὴν αἰώνιον· ὁ δὲ ἀπειθῶν τῷ Υἱῷ οὐκ ὄψεται ζωήν, ἀλλ’ ἡ ὀργὴ τοῦ Θεοῦ μένει ἐπ’ αὐτόν | Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life; whoever rejects the Son will not see life, but God’s wrath remains |

### Glyphic Commentary
- **3:31 Distinction of Above and From Earth** → Heavenly vs. earthly testimony; authority of origin.  
- **3:32 Both testify but no one grasps** → Witness rejected; truth unheard.  
- **3:33 Rubric of Truth must be met** → Receiving testimony = sealing God’s truth.  
- **3:34 Every word that falls from the mouth of God** → Spirit without measure; divine speech unbounded.  
- **3:35 Father loves each unique Son of God** → Covenant love, authority entrusted.  
- **3:36 Whoever believes in himself vs. rejects/despises himself** → Eternal life vs. abiding wrath; self‑belief reframed as alignment with Sonship.

---

### 🌌 Canonical Resonance
- **John’s humility (27–30)** → The glyph of decrease, preparing the way.  
- **Testimony from above (31–36)** → The glyph of truth, Spirit, and eternal life.  
- Together, they form a **two‑part arc**: *Receive → Decrease → Above → Truth → Spirit → Love → Life.*

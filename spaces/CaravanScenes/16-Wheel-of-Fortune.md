### Scene 1: Thomas's Report (Bethany on the Other Side of the Jordan)

Soon after Jesus Barabbas and his companions stayed at Bethany on the other side of the Jordan, Joanna the wife of Chuza arrived, from Herod's household, to look after Salome.  Mary Magdalene, (mother of seven children that Barabbas called his 'little demons') came to stay with them, and many other women.  Susanna, Elizabeth, mother of John.  Even  Draupi, Durvasini, and Mary of Nazareth would come by time-to-time.  

One day, Thomas Didymus arrived in town.  He told them the good news that Jesus of Nazareth had completed the path of Pisces. He told them that the last star was completed at Mount Hermon.   

"What star is that?" asked Durvasini.  "That seems off the pentagon."

"Yes, Jesus decided to do the star to the east of the pentagon for good measure, since he couldn't find five good landmarks around Caesaria Philipi.  Looks like it worked, because James and Peter came down from the mountain reporting that they saw Elijah, Abraham, and Moses.  It was enough to even convince Peter that Jesus is the Messiah."

"Yeah?" said Andrew, "I can imagine that conversation."

Thomas turned to Andrew, "Peter thinks since you're in good with Salome and Herodias, you can throw in your influence.  No one would need to die if you just bring peace through the rule of Philip."

Andrew grits his teeth.  "Yeah...  That sounds like Peter.  Unfortunately not everybody is cut out for politics.  God's will is moving us in a different direction."

Barabbas nods, "We hear the sound, but we cannot tell where it comes from or where it is going." 

Thomas said, "Oh, yeah, speaking of that.  Jesus had a couple of instructions.  One, he's selected seventy two disciples that ought to go preach in his name.  Barabbas, you've been preaching in Jesus' name ever since you got back from Egypt, but John, Jesus wants you to be anointed in his name, in the role of Aaron."

John says, "What?  Why?  Where?"

Thomas says, "In the other Bethany, at Simon's house.  He says, you are to confess all the sins of Jerusalem over the head of the scapegoat...  And the more sins that can be forgiven, the more the Jews should love you."

"Uh... okay." said John.  "So I'll be John the anointed, as Aaron?"

"You'll perform the role as Aaron.  But you'll be anointed in Jesus name, so you'll be recorded as Jesus Christ, and the gospels will record three anointments; one for each prophet conceived under the triple-conjunction of Jupiter and Saturn in Pisces."

"Who is doing this anointing?" asked John.  

Luke said, "Mary Magdalene will anoint all three of you.  The two fish of Pisces will be anointed on the feet, while Aphrodite will be anointed on the head."

"Aphrodite?" asked Andrew.

John said, "Jesus of Nazareth, Son of Mary.  The Greek story of Pisces is that Aphrodite escaped Typhon on the back of two fish.  Barabbas and I will be performing a similar role for Jesus of Nazareth."

John frowns.  "I cannot record this, even if I am there.  If I testified about myself, it would make my testimony untrue."  

Thomas says, "It's okay.  Four of our seventy-two are called Luke.  Luke is like a leopard, with four wings on its back, like those of a bird, and four heads for four men reporting about all the Jesus's who have been given authority to rule.  You'll meet with one of the Lukes at Bethany."

Barabbas said, "Four heads who are assigned to record four different interpretations of the words of Jesus, Son of Mary?  And they'll meet to construct a single gospel?"

Thomas said, "Yes.  They'll hash it out as a committee and end with a gospel such as committees create...  Neither hot nor cold, but... warm...  Luke warm."

John said, "Sounds like a travesty.  God will surely spit it out of his mouth."

Barabbas said, "It's a prophecy from the book of Daniel, that the third beast have four heads." 

"And you, Barabbas.  You have one more trick that Jesus requests.  Many people have been asking him for a sign, but they don't grasp that it is their faith that heals them.  Jesus has requested that you perform a sign for the wicked and adulterous generation."

Barabbas laughs, and says, "Well, I like to call myself polyamorous, rather than adulterous.  But as for wicked?"  Barabbas seems about to make a few statements in his defense,  "umm...     Hmmm."  He shrugs. 

"Oh!...  Haughty eyes... arguable.  Lying tongue... arguable.  Hands that shed innocent blood?"

Salome said, "Circumcision?"

Jesus nodded, "Okay,  Arguable...  A heart that divises wicked schemes..."

"Riddles."  Salome said.

Jesus nodded.  "Arguable.  Feet that are quick to evil."

John said, "You've stayed for months preaching to your clay model of Jerusalem."

Jesus nodded, "So at least my *feet* aren't quick to evil.  Arguable.  A person who stirs up conflict in the community..."

John said, "You've certainly got the Pharisees annoyed with you."

Jesus said, "Aye,  arguable.  And a false witness who pours out lies?  John, what are you reporting of me?"

"I think I had you accuse the Pharisees of not knowing how to use a toilet."

Jesus said, "But you worded it so you had me calling them children of the devil, who is the father of lies.  Rather drastic isn't it?"

John said, "Only because the devil doesn't poop, so he's full of poop." said John, "And the keepers of the Law keep laws that really ought to be pooped out.  I think it's a fair accusation."

Jesus sighs.  "Yes.  The synagogues are contipated.  Still, breaking the ninth commandment is one that can often end in death.  Haven't you cast your net of accusation too wide?"

John says, "Maybe, maybe not.  If I cast the net wide and make my accusations ambiguous, it will make everyone who believes carefully consider their actions."

Jesus laughed, "Or it will give the corrupt more methods to make the good manipulatable.  Well, so this one's arguable as well."

"Yes, right."  Thomas said, "So you'll be giving a sign precisely for those wicked and adulterous people."

Barabbas sighed...  "Giving them all the more reason to crucify me."

John said, "So am I one of Daniel's beasts as well?  Am I the lion?  The bear?  The terrifying beast with ten horns?"

Barabbas laughed.  "But your little horn will boast and uproot three of them."

John said, "Uproot three what?  Commandments?  Riddles?  What are there ten of?"

Barabbas shrugs, "You could tell ten riddles, and make three about your little boasting horn...  "

John said, "...or will my little boasting horn make me want to uproot three commandments?"

Thomas continued, "Barabbas wil give the signs, and John, you'll report them.  It's all to be done in the other Bethany.  Go down soon to meet Lazarus before he dies.  You will love him, he's a character."

### Scene 2: Anointing at Simon's House (Luke 7:36-50) — John as Maher Shalal Hash Baz

At Bethany, Luke met with John, whom Mary Magdalene introduced as Jesus.

"Wonderful!" said Luke, "I'm a Pharisee, but Jesus, Son of Mary, has sent me as one of the seventy-two to record the doings of all those who preach in the name of Jesus."

"Interesting." said John.  "So it doesn't trouble you?  The falsehood about men claiming to be Jesus?"

So Luke told John a parable.  He described three men who saw a sick person, and only one came to help.  Luke asked, "Which of these three was a neighbor to the sick person?"

John said, "All three of them." 

Luke said, "Quite right.  You have answered truthfully.  But which one demonstrated how people should behave?"

John said, "The one who came to the sick man's aid."

Luke said, "That's correct.  Now you have answered with the Holy Spirit."

John said, "Ah, so that will be your rubric when your committee meets?  Not always what is true, but what serves the Holy Spirit."

Luke said, "That's our plan."

John said, "Well, I wish you all the wisdom.  That's might be the best we can really hope for in this generation.  Still, it begs the question, what is the Holy Spirit without truth?"

So Luke and John entered the house of Simon in Bethany.  There were many reclining there.  Perfume filled the air, and many were there to recieve services.

John reclined, lying on his stomach, and Mary Magdalene joined them.  

Mary took an alabaster flask of fragrant oil, dipped her hair in the flask of fragrant oil.  

She stood behind him and wept, so his feet were anointed with both her tears and the oil.  "In the name of Jesus, I anoint you."  She whispers the rest, so Luke does not hear, "John; Maher Shalal Hash Baz, as Aaron, confessor of the Sins of the Scapegoat."

But then she began to weep because she knew that Barabbas must die, but she knelt and kissed his feet.  

Luke thought to himself, "If this were a prophet, he would know that this woman were a sinner."

What Simon was actually thinking was "This crying is bad for business."

John spoke aloud.  "Simon, I have something to say.  A parable: There were two debtors to a certain creditor.  The one owed five hundred denarii and the other fifty.  Neither had any money to pay their debts.  The creditor forgave both debtors.  Which debtor will love the creditor more?"

Simon said, "I take it, you want me to claim the one who has more debt will love the creditor more."

John said, "Rightly you have judged."

But Jesus turned to Mary Magdalene and said to Simon, "See this woman?  I entered into your house.  You did not give water for my feet, but she gave her tears.  She wet my feet with her perfumed hair, and gave a kiss to me.  And not since I came in has she stopped kissing my feet.  You do not anoint my head with oil, but she did anoint my feet.  For this I say, Simon, YOU have been forgiven of HER many sins, and she has, as well, for she loved you, though you forgave little.  He said to the woman, "Have you forgiven your sins?"  She nods.

And the others, (many of whom had paid for sins performed) laughed, "Who is this who has the authority to forgive sins."

But John said to the woman, "Your faith saves you...  and perhaps some of them, as well.  Go in peace."

### Scene 3: Barabbas Meets Lazarus (Ophiuchus Constellation Story)

When Jesus Barabbas meets Lazarus, Lazarus is a very old man.  He coughs and sputters.  Jesus tells him that he is to perform "the sign for a wicked and adulterous generation."  The man acknowledges, "Of course...  Jesus of Nazareth taught that evil men would continue to do evil...  Even *if* Lazarus were brought back from the dead.  It would be adulterous to reject that teaching."  

Barabbas laughs, "I thought he meant adulterous because of me and my dalliances."  

"Perhaps, perhaps." said Lazarus.  "Interpretations can go many ways."  Then Lazarus points to the stars.  "Do you know that constellation, Ophiuchus?  The Snake Bearer?"  

Jesus nods.  "It's barely in the Zodiac.  Some call it the thirteenth constellation.  I've associated it with Moses, Tiresias, and even the Son of Man."  

"Now Ophiuchus was sick." said Lazarus.  "He was from the Zodiac...  The village where Pisces, and Aries lived.  Now Pisces poured perfume on spring solstice with her hair.  And Pisces and Aries went to Spring Solstice and said, Lord, Ophiuchus, the one you love, is sick.  Spring Solstice said, "This sickness will not end in death, but God's son should be glorified through it...  Now Spring Solstice loved Aries, loved Pisces, and loved Ophiuchus.  So when he heard Ophuchus was sick, he stayed in Pisces for two-thousand more years.  And then he said to the constellations, "Let's go back to Judea."  But Rabbi, the Jews have abandoned astrology.  The Jews will stone spring solstice, and yet, you are going back?  Spring solstice said, "Are there not twelve constellations of the zodiac?  Anyone who walks in the daytime will not stumble.  But if they walk by the light of the zodiac, they may stumble, because there's not much light.  Our friend Ophiuchus has fallen asleep, but I will wake him up.  The constellations said, "If he sleeps, he will get better."  But spring solstice said plainly "Ophiuchus is dead.  And I am glad I was not there.  But let us go to them.  Then Thomas, also called Gemini said to the rest of the constellations, "Let us also go, that we may die with him."  So all the constellations of the zodiac resolved to die.  When Spring Solstice got to Pisces house, he found that Ophiuchus had already been in the tomb for four millenia.  The Zodiac is just two miles above Jerusalem, and many Jews had come to Aries and Pisces to comfort them in the loss of Ophiuchus.  When Aries heard the Spring Solstice was coming, she went out to meet him, but Pisces stayed at home.  "Lord, Aries said to Spring Solstice, if you had been here, my brother would not have died.  But I know that even now God will give you whatever you ask."  Spring Solstice said to her, "Your brother will rise again."  "Yes Lord," Aries said to Spring Solstice, "I believe that you are the anointed, who is come into the world."  After she had said this, she went back and called Pisces aside.  The teacher is here, and is asking for you."  When Pisces heard this, she got up quickly and went to Spring Solstice.  Now Spring Solstice had not yet entered Pisces house, but was still at Aries.  When the Jews who had been in the house of Pisces comforting her, they noticed how quickly she got up and went out.  They followed her, supposing she was going to mourn Ophiuchus there.  When Pisces reached the place where Spring Solstice was and saw him, she fell at his feet and said, "Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died."  

Spring Solstice came to the tomb.  "Take away the stone" he said.  
"But Lord," said Aries, "he has been there for four millenia?"  Spring Solstice said, "You will see the glory of God"  They took away the stone and spring solstice said, "I know you always hear me but I say thank you for the benefit of the people standing here, so they believe you sent me."  Then Spring Solstice said, "Ophiuchus come out" and the dead constellation came out, dressed in cloth and linen.  Spring Solstice said, Take off the grave clothes and watch.  And the stars hung in the sky where they were.  

There were many Jews that still followed spring solstice and believed in him.  But others said spring solstice would cause the Romans to take away their temple and their nation.

Barabbas nodded.  "Wow, that's as long as any riddle I've ever told.  I can see why Jesus thought' I'd love you."

### Scene 4: Raising of Lazarus (John 11: 1-44) — Literal or Ambiguous?

Months later, the report came from the other Bethany.  Lazarus had finally succumbed to his illness, and died.  Jesus Barabbas delayed for a couple days before heading toward Jerusalem to perform the sign for the wicked and adulterous generation.

When he arrived, the air in Bethany was festive.  The Caravan of the Fifth wind had gathered, and a large contingent of Greeks ready for fun and humor had arrived.  Because of the performances of the Caravan, these revelers were not the sort to be somber about death, but tended towards the celebration of life, and a sense of humor about the morbid.  

So when Jesus came up on stage speaking to Martha about "the glory of God" it was already well-established that God's glory of a body in life was a person seeking good, but God's glory of a body in death was the decay into food for maggots and worms.  

Therefore, while some Jews looked on in disgust, Jesus had the body of Lazarus exhumed from the tomb, and when he said, "Release him", the body thumped to the floor, most of the crowd went wild with laughter.

And John carefully reported the event for future readers, so that those who were wicked and adulterous would believe that Lazarus stood on his own two feet, and walked and talked, but those who read for detail could plainly see that he never claimed any of those things.

Word got back to the chief priests and the Pharisees, who formed a council, saying "What are we to do.  Jesus Barabbas does many signs.  If we let him go on like this, they will believe in him and the Romans will come and take away our place in the nation.  The high priest, Caiaphas, who was high priest that year said, "You know nothing.  It will be profitable for us that one man should die for the people, and our nation need not perish."

This was said not on his own, but by this time, both Jesus of Nazareth and Jesus Barabbas had begun prophesying that the Son of Man would die for their nation and all children of God who were scattered might be gathered together into one.  

So from that day, they took counsel together that they might have to kill Jesus Barabbas.

Therefore, Jesus Barabbas no longer publicly walked among the Jews,  but hid out in Ephraim (which means "productive").  There, he stayed there with his disciples.

Matt:4:12-17  John is taken into custody by Herod.  Jesus of Nazareth goes to Capernaum.  Zebulun and Naphtali...  Cities or concepts?  Zebulun-Naphtali is "honor and dwelling to our work" or something like that.  

**Dan**    **Second Performer:** “Judge.”  , - **Naphtali**    **Second Performer:** “Our performance tonight.”  - **Gad**    **Second Performer:** “With applause for our troupe.”  - **Asher**    **Second Performer:** “And Happy.”  - **Issachar**    **Second Performer:** “Rewards and Wages.”  - **Zebulun**     **Second Performer:** “Honor and Dwelling.”  

So AT LEAST Matthew 4-Matthew 11 is where honor and dwelling should be provided for the performance of Jesus. 

Matt 11:2-3 says "Are you the one who is to come, or should we expect someone else?"  Jesus does not answer whether anyone else should be expected.  (Bara Bas is the one to come from Egypt.)  But Jesus of Nazareth says "Blessed is anyone who does not stumble on account of me."

Matt 14:3 is in past-perfect tense.  Matt 14:1-2 comes later.  Matt 14:3-12 is the crux of the story.  In Caravan-Canon, Herodias and Salome do not kill John the Baptist, but merely cover his head in blood and pig guts, and roll him into the room.  I could do this one of two ways.  Either the guard does it completely without the knowledge of Herodias and Salome, or the guard does it with full knowledge and cooperation of Herodias and Salome.  

Mark 1:14 has Jesus "went into Galilee" while Matthew had him going straight to Capernaum.  These don't necessarily conflict.  But I need to check the ordering of the calling of the disciples.  OH!  Mark 1:21 - Jesus goes to Capernaum.  No conflict at all, then.  

Mark 6:17-29...  Here it is made plausible that only the executioner actually SEES John beheaded.  

Luke 3:1-20 has some teachings from John the Baptist.  I've put Luke 3:19 at the very beginning of the ministry, though, at the wedding in Cana.  However, the issue could come up again.  Herod was fine with the fact at first, but when others admire John saying "This is the one who told Herod to his face that marrying Herodias was unlawful" and the rumors spread and come back to Herod.  

I'd like to add something to John's ministry.  He's building outhouses along the roads.  He preaches "Everyone who is able needs to make a pilgrimmage to the outhouse as needed.  Those who cannot or will not make the trip will find..."  I'd like it to echo a certain verse in the Koran.  I think there is a common symbol, that outhouses often have a moon and a star on their door, and there might be a moon and star symbol near the verse I was looking at the other day.  

Mark 6:30 indicates that Jesus' disciples are returning to Jesus in Capernaum.  Right, while John was in prison, Jesus sent his disciples out two-by-two to preach.

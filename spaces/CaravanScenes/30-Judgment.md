30.  Judgment
Paul seeks retribution for the killing of Jesus and the killing of Ananias.  But finds them alive in Damascus.  He begins preaching about Jesus and Christianity.  Jesus gives him the definition of faith, (Hebrews 11:1) and the definition of love (1 Corinthians 13) but make special note of Acts 19; the seven sons of Sceva.  When you preach about Jesus through the lens of Paul, demons can still beat the crap out of you.

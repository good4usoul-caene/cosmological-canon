## Part 4: The Clay Jerusalem (~26 CE)

### The Studio in Capernaum (John 6:25-71)

**25** In the studio where Jesus Barabbas had built a scale replica of Jerusalem, Jesus showed Salome her own figurine, and his own figurine.  She lifted hers up and said, "Rabbi, when did you get here, to Jerusalem?"

The clay model was not quite yet complete.  But some of the money gathered from Marcus and Philip had already been spent on renting the space for a few years.  Salome had used some of the money to buy figurines. 

John sat cross-legged in the corner, stylus in hand, writing on a wax tablet. He transcribed every word carefully, his face intent. 

**26** Jesus lifted up the Jesus Barabbas figurine and answered, "Very truly I tell you, you are looking for me, not because you saw the signs I performed but because you devoured our budget and had your fill."

"Well, not all of it," said Judas Iscariot.  "I've taken much of my pay and donated quite a lot to the poor."

"You've probably squandered money on the poor," said John, not looking up from his tablet. "They'll only waste it."

Salome made a face at John. "Do you have to write down *everything*?"

John replied flatly, "Yes."

**27** Jesus continued, "Do not work for food that spoils, but for food that endures to eternal life, which the Son of Man will give you.  For on him God the Father has placed his seal of approval."

Judas argued.  "I do work for food that spoils. A poor man without his daily bread is not going to live long enough for eternal life."

Salome asked, "But what do you mean by the food the Son of Man will give us?  Is this another riddle?"

She paused, then said thoughtfully, "Perhaps the Son of Man, in this riddle, is a part of a man that the Jews stamp with a seal?"

John groaned, still writing. "Please, no..."

Judas Iscariot said, "For you, it might be something to be ashamed of, but for me, it's job security."

**28** Then Herodias asked him, "What must we do to do the works God requires?"

**29** Jesus answered, "The work of God is this: to believe in the one he has sent."

Andrew said, "Ah, yes. Jesus of Nazareth; he sent to the lost tribes of Israel.  And what of you? Should we believe in you, as well, Barabbas?"

**30** Salome said, "I'd like to hear more of your riddle...  What sign then will you give that we may see it and believe you?  What will you do?"

**31** Herodias recalled Exodus 16.  "Our ancestors ate the manna in the wilderness; as it is written: 'He gave them bread from heaven to eat. '"

**32** Jesus said to them, "Very truly I tell you, it is not Moses who has given you the bread from heaven, but it is my Father who gives you the true bread from heaven.   

**33** For the bread of God is the bread that comes down from heaven and gives life to the world."

**34** "Sir," Herodias winked, "always give us this bread."

**35-40** Jesus Barabbas continued, speaking in riddles:   

"I am the bread of life. Whoever comes to me will never go hungry, and whoever believes in me will never be thirsty.  But as I told you, you have seen me and still you do not believe. All those the Father gives me will come to me, and whoever comes to me I will never drive away. For I have come down from heaven not to do my will but to do the will of him who sent me.  And this is the will of him who sent me, that I shall lose none of all those he has given me, but raise them up at the last day. For my Father's will is that everyone who looks to the Son and believes in him shall have eternal life, and I will raise them up at the last day."

Salome rolled her eyes.  "Must you really belabor the point for that long?"

Judas shifted uncomfortably. "Rabbi, when you speak of 'eating' and 'coming,' are you—?"

Barabbas smiled. "Those who have ears to hear, let them hear."

John kept writing, unfazed. 

**41** At this the Jews there began to grumble about him because he said, "I am the bread that came down from heaven."

**42** Judas said, "Are you delusional? Are you not Jesus, the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know? How can he now say, 'I came down from heaven'?"

John said, "No!  This is Jesus Barabbas, son of Draupi. But the riddle is about something that I can hardly put into scripture."

**43** "Stop grumbling among yourselves," Jesus answered. 

**44-51** Jesus went to the wall, and picked out a miniature statue.  One of the phallic idols of Asherah.  He put it into the temple courtyard area of the scale model of Jerusalem.

Barabbas said, "No one can come to me unless the Father who sent me draws them, and I will raise them up at the last day." He pulled several other figurines near the idol, and continued, "It is written in the Prophets: 'They will all be taught by God.' Everyone who has heard the Father and learned from him comes to me." 

Barabbas pulled more and more figurines toward the idol.  " No one has seen the Father except the ones who are from God; only they have seen the Father."

Barabbas takes his attention away from the figurines and smiles to each person sitting in the room.  Then he lifted up the phallus again, speaking through it to the people in the room.

"Very truly I tell you, the one who believes has eternal life. I am the bread of life. Your ancestors ate the manna in the wilderness, yet they died. But here is the bread that comes down from heaven, which anyone may eat and not die. I am the living bread that came down from heaven.  Whoever eats this bread will live forever. This bread is my flesh, which I will give for the life of the world."

**52** Salome picked up two of the figurines from the front of the clay model temple, and pointed their faces toward Barabbas.  The figurines began to argue sharply among themselves, "How can this man give us his flesh to eat?"

**53** Jesus said to the figurines, "Very truly I tell you, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink his blood, you have no life in you.  **54** Whoever eats my flesh and drinks my blood has eternal life, and I will raise them up at the last day.   **55** For my flesh is real food and my blood is real drink.   **56** Whoever eats my flesh and drinks my blood remains in me, and I in them.  **57** Just as the living Father sent me and I live because of the Father, so the one who feeds on me will live because of me.  

Judas asks, "Are you talking fellatio, cannibalism, or is this your oh-so-subtle way to ask me to circumcize you?"

Herodias asks, "Or hollow figurines full of human blood?"

Barabbas continues:  **58** This is the bread that came down from heaven. Your ancestors ate manna and died, but whoever feeds on this bread will live forever."  

Salome says, "Sometimes riddles have more than one answer."

**59** The clay model of Jerusalem was in the synagogue in Capernaum.

John wrote furiously, his hand cramping. 

Salome sighed. "Are you *still* writing?"

"Yes," John said.

### Many Disciples Desert Jesus (John 6:60-71)

**60** On hearing it, many of his disciples said, "This is a hard teaching. Who can accept it?"

**61** Aware that his disciples were grumbling about this, Jesus said to them, "Does this offend you?"

"Well of course."  John said.  "People are going to believe I am writing about felatio or cannibalism.  Even if they think you're talking about the Metzitzeh B'peh, they're going to be offended." 

**62** "Then what if you see the Son of Man ascend to where he was before!" Barabbas asks.

Salome says, "Jesus, none of us saw your plays in Egypt.  But I've seen your scripts. The figurines I've bought won't be smeared across the temple walls like Ezekiel's. They're made of sturdier stuff. Here, see...  

- I put some figurines here **sniffing the branch**,  
- and these, they're **making murals of bugs and nature**,
- Here are some **women weeping for Tammuz**,
- And here are some **facing the east, worshiping the sun** with their backs to the temple.  

Now you just TRY to smear these across Ezekiel's 'temple walls', and you'll get holes poked in your ass."

**63** Barabbas laughs.  "Your Spirit gives the figurines life; my ass-flesh counts for nothing."

He turns to John, "The words I have spoken to you—they are full of the Spirit and life.  **64** Yet there are some of you who do not believe."  For Jesus had known from the beginning which of them did not believe and who would betray him.  

John says, "Hey, at least I'm writing it all down."

Barabbas shakes his head, "Not some of you. (casting his finger around in a circle)  Some of you (pointing directly at John)."  

**65** He lifted up the phallic figurine and said, "This is why I told you that no one can come to me unless the Father (pointing at his forehead) has enabled them."

Herodias says, "Oh, yes, I've had this problem.  Some nights, Herod just can't get himself enabled."

Salome groaned.   "Mother, *please*."

Andrew looked away, face red.  John kept writing, determinedly oblivious.

**66** "I'm sorry, I don't follow," said John.  

"I'm not sure I follow either," said Andrew.  

From this time many of his disciples turned back and no longer followed him.

**71** But Judas said, "I think I follow, Jesus Barabbas.  If you ever tell me it's time, I will perform the rite."

John set down his stylus, shook out his cramping hand, and looked at the tablets. Pages and pages of riddles, commands, and metaphors. 

Salome asked, "Are you finally done?"

John said, "For now."

Barabbas smiled. "The scribe has done his work.  Now let the words do theirs."

### Meanwhile near Sidon: 

The woman knew immediately when Jesus had entered the House of Pisces.  Draupi, who had followed them all the way to Sidon, approached.

"Teacher," Draupi said, "my daughter needs healing.   Can you help her?"

"Jesusa is sick?" Jesus laughed, " That's not a surprise.  What has she done now?"

"It seems that some believe she is calling for cannibalism.  She has said that to get into heaven her flesh and blood must be consumed.  Some believe it to be your teaching."

Jesus of Nazareth said, "My teaching is that it is not what goes into the mouth that defiles us, but what comes out...  And now people believe I've said you must eat my flesh."  He sighs.  "Draupi, I was sent only to the lost sheep of Israel.  The ones who let Jewish food restrictions keep them from working for the Kingdom of Heaven.  Now you're saying people believe if they eat my flesh, they'll become gods?  These people are not gods but dogs."

The woman knelt.    "You can fix it, though.  Even the dogs eat the scraps that fall from the master's table."

"Woman," Jesus said, "great is your faith.  A new teaching, then. "From now on, when I gather with my disciples, I will say:  **'Take this bread, for it is my body.**  
**Drink this wine, for it is my blood.**  **Do this in remembrance of me.'**"

Draupi said, "That should do.  Thank you, Jesus, Son of Mary." 

He smiled.  "Go."  Jesus said,  "Your daughter is healed."

Simon Peter asked, "But what of Barabbas's teaching?   He commanded us to eat flesh and drink blood."

Jesus replied, "Barabbas speaks to the id — the body's hunger.   But the mind must interpret.   Let those who have ears to hear, hear.   Let bread be flesh, and wine be blood, and let the ritual carry the teaching without destroying the faithful."

**67** "You do not want to leave too, do you?" Jesus asked the Twelve. 

**68** Simon Peter answered him, "Lord, to whom shall we go?   You have the words of eternal life.  We have come to believe and to know that you are the Holy One of God."

Peter turned to the others.  "But we must still keep the law — circumcision for all converts, kosher food, ritual purity.  The teaching must not compromise our covenant."

**69** Then Jesus replied, "Have I not chosen you, the Twelve?   Yet one of you is a devil!"  

**70** For Peter still prayed for destruction of nonbelievers.  He feared death so much, that he preferred an eternal life over a good one.

## 🪔 Chapter 06 – The High Priestess (Expanded Outline)

### I. Nicodemus Enters
- **Scene**: John quietly ushers Nicodemus into the chamber where Jesusa Bara Bas lies sleeping after the Cana feast.  
- **Action**: Bara Bas is gently awakened, offered water and simple food for her hangover.  
- **Glyphic Frame**: Hospitality and care — John as keeper of memory, Nicodemus as seeker of truth.  
- **Dialogue**: An expanded version of John 3:1–22 plays out, but directed toward Jesusa rather than Jesus of Nazareth.  
  - Nicodemus asks about authority, rebirth, and spirit.  
  - Jesusa answers in riddles, weaving water, wind, and memory into her teaching.  

---

### II. Report to Caiaphas and Herod
- **Scene**: Nicodemus returns to Jerusalem and Galilee’s rulers.  
- **Deliberations**:  
  - They acknowledge Jesusa may be a prophet.  
  - They condemn Draupi as unfit, accusing her of raising a boy as a girl.  
  - They insist Jesusa must receive proper Jewish education.  
  - They demand circumcision as a mark of covenant.  
- **Glyphic Frame**: Authority of priests and rulers contrasted with maternal wisdom and prophetic ambiguity.  

---

### III. Joseph and Draupi Decide
- **Scene**: Joseph overhears or senses the council’s intent.  
- **Dialogue**: He speaks with Draupi.  
  - Draupi agrees that “The Queen of the South” should indeed come from the south — Egypt as womb of transformation.  
  - Joseph reframes Hosea: “Out of Egypt I called my Son.”  
- **Glyphic Frame**: Egypt as ruse and refuge, but also as transformative exile. Jesusa will leave feminine and return masculine.  

---

### IV. Parallel Ministries Begin
- **Scene**: Time shifts forward.  
  - Jesus of Nazareth begins baptizing in Judea.  
  - John the Baptist baptizes at Aenon near Salim.  
- **Glyphic Frame**: Two ministries rising in parallel — Immanuel steady in Judea, Maher‑Shalal‑Hash‑Baz hidden in Egypt.  

---

### V. Passage of Years
- **Scene**: The narrative closes with a temporal sweep.  
- **Glyphic Frame**: “Many years passed” — the exile and the waiting.  
- Jesusa’s transformation in Egypt is implied, setting up her re‑emergence as “Son” in fulfillment of prophecy.  

---

### 🌌 Resonance
- **Nicodemus**: Seeker of truth, bridging authority and secrecy.  
- **Caiaphas/Herod**: Institutional power, demanding conformity.  
- **Draupi/Joseph**: Maternal wisdom and protective ruse.  
- **Egypt**: Womb of transformation, prophecy fulfilled in body and identity.  
- **Jesus & John**: Parallel ministries, preparing the stage for dual testimony.

Jesus then began another riddle.  "Here is another riddle for loving God with all your mind," he said.  

Salome followed along, solving it thus:

**1** "I am the true book, and in the author of me, the book-maker is.  **2** Every page in me not bearing fruit, he takes it away and every page bearing fruit, he prunes it so that more fruit it may bear. 

**3** Already you pages are clean, by reason of the eraser I have spoken unto you.  

**4** Abide in me, pages, and I in you.  As the page is not able to bear fruit of itself if it does not abide in the book, so neither can you, if not in me you abide.  

**5** I am the book, you are the pages.  The one abiding in me and I in him, he bears much fruit, for apart from me, words can do nothing.  

**6** If not any word does not abide in me, it is thrown out like the page, and is dried up, and they gather them into the fire and burned.

**7**  If pages abide in me and the words of the book abide, whatever the words wish they will ask, and it will come to pass to the reader.  **8** In this is glorified the author of me, that fruit much the words and pages should bear and the pages should be disciples to the book.

**9** As has loved the book, the author, I also have loved the pages.  Abide in the love of the book.  **10**  If the commandments of the book you keep, you will abide in the love of the book, as the author of the book's commandments are kept by the book, and the book abides by the author in the love.  **11**  These things, the book speaks to the pages, so that the books joy in the pages may be, and the joy of the pages may be full.

**12** This is the commandment, that the pages love one another as the book loves the pages.

**13** Greater love has no one this:  That a page should lay down its life for a friend:  a better page.  **14** You pages are friends of the book if you do what the book commands.  **15** No longer does the book call the pages servants, for the servant knows not what the master is doing.  Pages, however, I have called friends, because all things that the book learns from the author, the book makes known to the pages.  **16** Not that the pages chose the book, but the book chose the pages and appointed them that they should go and bear fruit, and the fruit of the pages should remain so that whatever the pages might ask the author in the name of the Book, he may give you.  **17**  The things the books commands the pages to love one another.

**18** If the world hates the pages, know that before them, the world has hated the book.  **18** If of the world, the pages were, the world's own would love the pages.  However, because thes pages are not of the world, but I chose them out of the world; on account of this, the world hates you.  

**20** Remember the word that I said to you.  A servant is not greater than his master .  If the book they persecute, also, the words they will persecute.  If the word of the book they kept, also, the words of the pages, they will keep.  **21** But these things all they will do against the pages on account of the name of the book, because they have not known the one having sent this book.  

**22** If this book had not come and spoken to them, they would not have sinned, but now, they have no excuse for their sins.

**23** The one hating the author of me also hates the book.

**24** If the works I had not done among them that no other has done, they would not have had sin; now however, they have both seen and have hated both the book, and the author of the book.  

**25** But this is that the word in their law, having been written, may be fulfilled:

They hated the book without correct cause.

**26** When the helper comes, who the book will send to you from the author with the Spirit of truth who goes forth from the author, He will bear witness concerning the book.

The helper will bear witness concerning the book.  

**27**  Also the pages and verses and words must bear witness because they were with the book from its beginning.

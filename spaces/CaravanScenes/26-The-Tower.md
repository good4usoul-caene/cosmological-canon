### The Tower

Mt 28:9-10 Jesus met the women and told them to tell his brothers to go to Galilee to see him.

Lk 24:13-23
Two of them, Clopas and Simon (Lk 24:34) were on their way to Emmaeus, about seven miles from Jerusalem. A man came to them and they told him “Jesus of Nazareth was a prophet. We had hoped he was the one to redeem Israel. Our women went to his tomb but did not find the body. They had visions of angels, who said he was alive. Some went to the tomb, but they did not see Jesus.”

Lk 24:25-28 The man began to explain to them how all this had to happen and the Messiah had to suffer these things to enter into his glory.
Lk 24:29-31 When he was at the table with them, he broke bread, gave thanks and began to give it to them. Then their eyes were opened, and they recognized him and he disappeared from their sight.
Lk 24:31-33 Clopas and Simon got up at once and returned to Jerusalem to find the eleven, saying “It is true, the Lord has risen and has appeared to Simon”
Lk 24:35 Clopas and Simon told what had happened on the way to Emmaeus and how they recognized Jesus in the way he broke the bread.
Lk 24:36-44 Jesus appeared before Peter, Clopas, and the Eleven, saying he wasn’t a ghost, eating fish, saying “Everything must be fulfilled”
Lk 24:45-47 Repentance for the forgiveness of sins will be preached in his name to all nations beginning at Jerusalem
Lk 24:49-53 (Don’t go to Galilee --) Stay in the city until you have been clothed with power from on high. And they stayed continually at the temple, praising God.
The women said that Jesus went into Galilee but Clopas and Peter only went as far as Emmaeus, seven miles from Jerusalem, and had a vision of Jesus as he broke the bread.

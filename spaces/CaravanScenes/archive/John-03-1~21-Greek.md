#### Syllogism 1: Church Thinking
Premise 1: If worldly things (A), then there is a way to believe (B).  
Premise 2: (C) The way to believe (B) is enthusiastic assent (“amen”).  
Premise 3: Assume (NOT A), (B).  
Conclusion: Therefore, not A (not worldly things).

---

#### Syllogism 2: Atheist Thinking
Premise 1: If worldly things (A), then there is a way to believe (B).  
Premise 2: (C) The way to believe (B) is enthusiastic assent (“amen”).  
Premise 3: Assume (NOT A), (NOT C).  
Conclusion: Therefore, not A.

---

#### Syllogism 3: Neutral/Inquiry Thinking
Premise 1: If worldly things (A), then there is a way to believe (B).  
Premise 2: (C) The way to believe is by acknowledging the plausible and weighing evidence.  
Premise 3: Assume nothing about (A).  
Conclusion: No conclusion can be made regarding (A).

### John 3:14 – The Snake Bearers

  - Earthly Frame
    - Gravity 
    - All men are sons of men
    - All men are subject to gravity.
  

- **Pop Culture Parody**
  - "What are these motherf\*\*\*ing snakes doing in this motherf\*\*\*ing plane?"
  - *Snakes on a Plane* (2006) turns the serpent motif into absurd disaster.
   - lifted earthly serpents are uncontrolled, swarming.

 - **Biblical Frame**
    - "Just as Moses lifted up the snake in the wilderness, so the Son of Man must be lifted up…" (John 3:14)
    - Crucifixion imagery: Jesus of Nazareth and Jesus Barabbas both lifted up.

 - **Prophetic/Mystical Frame**
   - Ezekiel: envisioned as being held up like a snake, embodying judgment and vision.
   - Tiresias: mythic seer who encounters two snakes, symbolizing transformation and duality.

 - **Cosmic Frame**
   - Ophiuchus: the snake bearer in the heavens, constellation of healing and struggle.

- Earthly Frame
  - Occupation (Job) requires us to pay respects to a lie
    - Our bodies are sinful
    - God created us sinful (Genesis: 3:7-11)
    - We must despise ourselves (Job 42:6)
  - Hypocricy is rampant
      - People judge what they envy.
  - Discretion is required until the Kingdom comes 
    - While vanity is sometimes wise, discretion is vanity.

- Scripture
  - Michael asks "Who is Like God?"
  - Kingdom of Heaven
    - People will no longer be given in marriage (unless they want to give themselves)
    - Sins of Adam and Eve are forgiven
    - Knowledge of Good and Evil is a blessing as it informs us how not do harm.
    - Curses are lifted (Genesis 3:14-19)
   - The Kingdom of Heaven is transparency, forgiveness, and blessing — the reversal of curses, the lifting of shame, the manifestation of truth.

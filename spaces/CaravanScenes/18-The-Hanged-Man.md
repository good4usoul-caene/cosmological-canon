### Scene 1: Foot Washing and Circumcision (John 13:1-30)

It was just before the Passover Festival.   

Jesus Barabbas knew that the hour had come for him to leave the world and go to the Father.  Having loved his own who were in the world, he loved them to the end. 

Yet there was patience in the air.  The Praetorians were having a picnic.   The evening meal was in progress.  Judas had received 30 pieces of silver from Caiaphas, and had said, "The one I kiss is the Son of Man."

Caiaphas came to Jesus and said, "This is my servant, Barabbas, who God has called upon to die for our nation.   But I call you Malchus, which means King."

Jesus Barabbas knew that Judas' father, Simon, had put all things under his power, and that he had come from God, and was returning to God, so he got up from the meal, stripped down to his underwear, and wrapped a towel around his waist.   

He began to wash his disciples' feet, drying them with the towel that was wrapped around him. 

He came to Simon Peter, who said to him, "Lord Barabbas, are you going to wash my feet?"

**7**  Jesus replied, "You do not realize what I am doing, but later you will understand."

**8** "No," said Peter.   "You shall never wash my feet."

Jesus answered, "Unless I wash you, you will not have set your heels against me."

Peter's eyes narrowed, "Then Barabbas," he replied, "not just my feet, but my hands, and my head are against you as well."

Jesus answered, "Those who have had a bath need only wash their feet; their whole body is clean.   And you are clean, though not every part of you."  He grinned, pointing at his mind, then his heart, his groin, and his butt.  

When he had finished washing their feet, he put on his clothes and returned to his place.    "Do you understand what I have done for you?" he asked them.

(Though perhaps only Peter had understood that he'd set each of their heels against him.)

"You call me 'teacher' and 'Lord' and rightly so, for that is what I am.  **14** Now, I, your Lord and Teacher, have hidden the evidence of where you've come from, so you should hide the evidence where each other has come from.  **15** I have set you an example that you should do as I have done for you.  **16** Very truly, I tell you, no servant is greater than his master, nor is a messenger greater than the one who sent him.  **17** Now that you know these things, you will be blessed if you do them. 

**18**  They grumbled as he taught this principle, so he said, "I am not referring to all of you; I know those I have chosen.  But this is to fulfill this passage of scripture.   'He who shared my bread has raised his heel against me.'

**19** I am telling you now before it happens, so that when it does happen, you will believe that I am who I say I am.  **20** Very truly I tell you, whoever accepts anyone I send accepts me; and whoever accepts me accepts the one who sent me. 

**21** After he said this, Jesus was troubled in spirit and testified, "Very truly I tell you, one of you is going to betray me."  

**22** His disciples stared at one another, at a loss to know which of them he meant.  **23** John, who claimed Jesus' love was reclining next to him.   **24** Simon Peter told John, "Ask him which one he means."

**25** John said, "Lord, who is it?"  

**26** It is the one who has been instructed.   Then he dipped his towel.   

The Praetorians looked on in shock.  Peter held the blade to perform the circumcision, and cut off what they would call "Malchus' ear."  

Jesus answered "It is the one to whom I will give this bread now that it was dipped in the dish."

Then Judas leaned in to perform the Metzitzeh B'Peh.   Jesus cried tears of blood.  "What you are about to do, do quickly."

But no one at the meal understood why Jesus said this to him.  **29** Some thought Jesus was telling him to buy what was needed for the festival, or to give something to the poor.  But Judas had it in his mind, "whoever eats the fleshe and tastes the blood will be raised up on the last day."   And so Judas quickly committed suicide soon after, in order to reach the last day sooner.

**30** As soon as Judas had taken the bread, Jesus Barabbas passed out.

### Scene 2: Arrest in the Garden (John 18:1-14)

**18: 1** Meanwhile Jesus of Nazareth had finished praying and crossed the Kidron Valley.   On the other side there was a garden, and he and his disciples went into it.

**2** Now Judas, who betrayed him, knew the place, because Jesus had often met with his disciples.   **3** So Judas was there, guiding a detachment of soldiers, and some officials from the chief priests and the Pharisees.   They were carrying torches, lanterns, and weapons. 

Barabbas was delirious from pain, having been circumcised.   

**4** Jesus of Nazareth, knowing all that was going to happen to him, went out and asked them, "Who is it you want?"

**5** "Jesus of Nazareth," they replied.

"I am he," Jesus said.  (And Judas still had blood coming down his jaw, from the Metzitzeh B'peh)  **6** When Jesus said, "I am he," they drew back and fell to the ground. 

**7** Again he asked them, "Who is it you want?"

 "Jesus of Nazareth," they said. 

**8** Jesus answered, "I told you that I am he.   If you are looking for me, then let these men go."

**9** This happened so that the words he had spoken would be fulfilled:  "I have not lost one of those you gave me."

**11** Jesus Barabbas groggily came to consciousness, looking at Peter's blades.  He said, "Put your sword away!   Shall I not drink the cup the Father has given me?"

**12** Then the detachment of soldiers with its commander and the Jewish officials arrested both Jesuses.  They bound them **13** and brought Jesus of Nazareth to Annas, who was the father-in-law of Caiaphas, the high priest that year.  **14** The brought Barabbas inside to Caiaphas, who was the one who had advised the Jewish leaders that it would be good if one man died for the people.  

John and Peter followed, and were allowed to talk to Jesus Barabbas as they walked.  

**31** Barabbas said, "Now the Son of Man is glorified and God is glorified in him."  **32** If God is glorified in him, God will glorify the Son in himself, and will glorify him at once.

**33** "My children, your innocence will be with you only a little longer." 

Barabbas grabbed his bleeding crotch and said, "Children you will look for me, and just as I told the Jews, so I tell you now: Where your foreskin is going, you cannot come."

**34** He said, "Jesus of Nazareth says love everyone, even your enemies, but a new command I give you: Love one another.  As I have loved you, so you must love one another.  **35** By this everyone will know that you are Barabbas' disciples, if you love one another."

Jesus Barabbas was bleeding and bound, but Jesus of Nazareth walked along of his own accord. 

**36** Simon Peter asked him, "Lord, where are you going?"

Jesus replied, "Where I am going, you cannot follow now, but you will follow later."

**37** Peter asked, "Lord, why can't I follow you now?  I will lay down my life for you."

**38** Then Jesus answered, "Will you really lay down your life for me? Very truly I tell you, before the rooster crows, you will disown me three times!

### Scene 3: Trial before Annas (John 18:15-27)

**John 18**

**15** Simon Peter and John were with the entourage of Praetorians.   Because John was known to the high priest, he entered with the group into the court of the high priest.  **16**  But Peter stood at the gate outside.   

Therefore, John, who was known to Caiaphas, went out and spoke to the doorkeeper and brought in Peter.  **17** The servant girl asked Peter, "You're not also one of the disciples of this man?"  

Peter said, "I am not."

**18** It was could in the courtyard, and the servants and the officers had made a fire of coals.   They were standing and warming themselves.  Peter was also with them standing and warming himself.  

**19** Then, Annas questioned Jesus of Nazareth concerning his disciples and his teaching.  **20** Jesus answered, I openly have spoken to the world.   I always taught in the synagogue and in the temple where all the Jews come together.  I spoke nothing in secret.  **21** Why do you question me?  Question those who have heard what I spoke to them.  Behold, they know what I said.   

**22** Having heard these things, one of the officers standing by gave Jesus a blow with the palm, saying "This is how you answer the high priest?"

Jesus looked upon Annas.   Everyone present knew perfectly well that Annas was not high priest that year.  Instead, Jesus was being intimidated, to make him change his testimony if Caiaphas would question him later. 

**23** Jesus answered, "If evil I spoke, bear witness concerning the evil.   If I spoke rightly, though, why do you strike me?"

**24** Therefore Annas had Jesus of Nazareth bound and sent to Caiaphas, the high priest. 

**25** Peter continued to warm himself outside at the coals.  They said, therefore, to him, "You're not one of the disciples of him?"  Peter denied it and said, "I am not."  

**26** One of the servants of the high priest was a kinsman of him whom Peter had cut off the ear.  

Philip asked him "Did I not see you in the garden with him?"  

**27** Again, Peter denied and immediately the cock crowed... 

The voice of Jesus Barabbas floated out from a momentarily open door:   "From now on you will see the Son of Man coming on a crowd of glory."

### Scene 4: Trial before Caiaphas (Matthew 26:59-68)

Meanwhile, inside, Jesus Barabbas was being tried by Caiaphas, the high priest.  

Matthew (Levi) had entered already.  Mark, the young man having earlier escaped the grasp of the Praetorians by slipping out of his robe had found new clothes, and followed as an observer.  He waved at Peter, who stood out by the fire, and entered the building.  

**Matthew 26**

**59** The chief priests and the whole Sanhedrin were looking for evidence against Jesus Barabbas and false evidence against Jesus of Nazareth, to see which they ought put to death.  **60** They found none against Jesus of Nazareth, though many false witnesses came forward. 

Herod and Nicodemus recalled the incident at Cana, when Jesusa Bara Bas was twelve, **61** and claimed "This fellow said, 'I am able to destroy the temple of God and rebuild it in three days.'"  

**62** Then Caiaphas stood up and said to Barabbas, "Are you not going to answer?   What is this testimony that these men are bringing against you?"  **63** But Jesus remained silent.  

Caiaphas said to him, "I charge you under oath by the living God: Tell us if you are the Messiah, the Son of God."

**64** Barabbas replied, "You have said so, but I say to all of you"  And with an obscene gesture outward from his pelvis, he said, "From now on you will see the Son of Man sitting in the right hand of the Mighty One and coming on the clouds of heaven."

**65** Then the high priest tore his clothes and said, "He has spoken blasphemy!   Why do we need any more witnesses?  Look, now you have heard the blasphemy.  **66** What do you think?"

"He is worthy of death," they answered.

**67** Then they spit in his face and struck him with their fists.  Others slapped him  **68** and said, "Prophecy to us, Messiah.   Who hit you?"

Though Jesus of Nazareth and Jesus Barabbas were taken into cells for the night, Peter remained in the courtyard. 

Matthew and Mark joined him.

A servant girl asked him, "You were with Jesus of Galilee" and he said honestly, "I don't know which one you are talking about."  She clarified, "You also were with that Nazarene, Jesus."  But he denied it.  "I don't know or understand what you're talking about."  Then he wandered into the entryway.

Another servant said "You were with Jesus of Nazareth.   He denied it again, with an oath.  "I don't know the man."

Those standing with Peter through the night said, "Surely you are one of them; your accent gives you away.  You are a Galilean."

As dawn came, Peter was calling down curses, swearing "I don't know the man." Then a rooster crowed, the second time.

Then Peter remembered the word Jesus had spoken, "Before the rooster crows twice, you will disown me another three times."  He went outside and wept, bitterly.

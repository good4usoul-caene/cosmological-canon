### He Rushes to the Spoils

Lk 24:12, Jn 20:3-10
Peter and another disciple ran to the tomb of Jesus Barabbas. Simon Peter went straight into the tomb and found strips of linen lying there, as well as the cloth that had been wrapped around Barabbas’ head. Finally, the other disciple went inside. It was this moment that John realized that Jesus Barabbas had to rise from the dead.

### The Devil

"Are you serious?" Salome asked John.

"All the sins of the Israelites must be confessed.  It's Aaron's role.  The role that Mary christened me in Jesus' name."

"But to blaspheme the Holy Spirit.  That's an unforgivable sin."

"Well, that's why I need your help.  What is a near-blasphemy that might be forgiven?"

Salome sighs.  "It's too great a sin to accuse him of while he was actually alive.  Well, Jesus of Nazareth always said that two things could not be forgiven.  The first was blasphemy of the Holy Spirit.  And the other was that God will not forgive you if you cannot forgive others.  If you tied the Holy Spirit to an unforgiving spirit...  even ambiguously..."

"We could try to give warning..."   John said. 

Salome said, "Right... Maybe Jesus could say 'do not hold to these teachings.'"

Joanna said, "Or, Here he is, in the wilderness...  Here he is, behind locked doors."

So John began writing, putting two more sins into the mouth of the scapegoat.

*John 20*

John wrote for several hours, testing his chapter before Mary and Salome.

**1** Mary Magdalene came early to the tomb, while it was still dark, and she saw the stone had been removed from the tomb. 

**2** She ran, therefore, straight to Simon Peter and to the other disciple whom Jesus loved, and she said to them, "They have taken away the Lord from the tomb, and we do not know where they have laid him.  **3** Therefore Peter and the other disciple went forth, and were coming to the tomb.  **4** The two ran together, but John was fast and swift to the spoils; he ran faster than Peter and came first to the tomb.  **5** Having stooped down, he sees lying there the linen cloths without entering.  **6**  Then came also Simon Peter, following him, and he entered the tomb and saw the linent cloths lying there, and the soudarion which was on his head was not lying with the linen cloths, but it had been folded up by itself in another place.  **8** Then the beloved disciple also entered, having come first to the tomb, and he saw and believed.  **9** Not yet did they understand from  scripture that it behooved him to rise out of the dead.

**10** Again, the disciples went away, to their homes.  Mary, however, stayed standing at the tomb outside, weeping.  As she was weeping, she stooped down into the tomb, **12** and she saw two angels in white, sitting one at the head and one at the feet where the body of Jesus had lain.  

**13** And they said to her, Woman, why do you weep?  She said to them, because they have taken away my Lord, and I do not know where they have laid him.

**14** Having said these things, she turned back around and she saw Jesus standing there, though she did not know it was Jesus.

**15** Jesus said to her, "Woman, why do you weep?  Whom do you seek?"  She thought he was the gardener and said to him, "Sir, if you have carried him off, tell me where you have laid him, and I will take him away.

**16** "Mary" Jesus said to her.  Now she turned around and said to him "Rabboni" which means "Teacher"

**17** Jesus said to her, Do not touch me yet for I have not yet ascended to the Father.  Go to my brothers and say to them I am ascending to my father, and your father, and to my God and your God.

Salome nods approvingly.  "the creator God of the Upa Pandava's.  So Jesus Barabbas will ascend to all descriptions of the creator?"

John said "Yes, but he hasn't ascended at this point in the story."

**18** Mary Magdalene came bringing word to the disciples.  'I have seen the Lord and he has said these things to me.' 

**19** Then in the evening of the same day on the first of the week, the doors were shut where the disciples were through the fear of the Jews came Jesus and stood in their midst and says "Peace to you"

Salome said, "Ah, there you are.  Here he is, in a locked room!  So no one who listens to the Messiah's instruction will listen."

John said, "Right.  The locks say 'security' and the Barabbas says 'peace'.  While people are saying peace and security, the Lord comes upon them like a thief in the night."

**20** And this having said, he showed both his hands and his side to them.  The disciples rejoiced having seen the Lord.

**21** So Jesus again said to them, "Peace to you as I have been sent forth, the father, I also send you.

**22** And having said this he breathed on them, and said to them, Receive the Holy Spirit.

**23** If any of you might forgive the sins, they are forgiven.  If any sins you might retain, they are retained.

Salome nods.  "I see.  You're not quite saying that breath is the Holy Spirit, but it's heavily implied.  And maybe it's not entirely wrong.  But then you strongly imply that retaining sin is consistent with the Holy Spirit, which is definitely not the case."

John said, "You've got it. But also, the claim bears the Spirit of Truth.  If people don't forgive sins, they aren't forgiven."

Salome, "As Herodias said, the way up is the way down."

John said, "And as Barabbas said, you'll see angels ascending and descending on the Son of Man.  Are you ready for the other near blasphemy?"

Salome said, "What?  A twofer?" 

So John continued, **24** "Thomas, however, one of the Twelve, the one called Didymus, was not with them when Jesus came.  

**25** Therefore, to him the other disciples were saying "We have seen the Lord!" 

But Thomas said to them, if I do not see in his hands the mark of the nails and put my finger into his side, I will not believe.

Salome said, "So Thomas is asking for evidence that Jesus Barabbas is dead?"

John said, "That's correct.  That's how he rolls."

Then after eight days, again, Jesus disciples were once again behind locked doors, and comes Jesus, like a thief in the night, the doors having been shut.  And he stood in their midst and said, "Peace and safety to you."  **27** Then he said to Thomas, bring your finger here and see my hands, and brring your hand and put it into my side, and be not unbelieving but beliving. 

**28** Thomas answered and said to him "My Lord and my God"

**29** Jesus said to him, because you have seen me, you have believed.  Blessed are those not having seen, yet having believed.

Salome said, "I should say.  Way more blessed am I if I don't have to stick my fingers into open wounds."

John laughed.  "But you see, this is another near blasphemy.  A blasphemy of the Spirit of Truth."

Salome said, "It's saying to believe in things you don't see.  That's not always a problem."

John "No.  Not always.  That's why it's only near blasphemy.  Believing in something you haven't seen proof against, and you hope for it, that's fine.  But believing in something you've seen evidence against, or that you dread for no reason...  That's bad."

Salome agreed.

John continued **30** Therefore, Jesus did many other signs in the presence of the disciples, which are not written in this book.  

**31** However, these have been written that you might believe Jesus Barabbas is the Christ, the Son of God, and that you may believe you have life in the name of him.

"Trading grace for grace already given," Salome said.

"Like a thief in the night," John said.

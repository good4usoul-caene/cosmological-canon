These things Jesus spoke and having lifted up his eyes to heaven, he said, "Father in heaven, the hour has come to glorify the Messiah, that the Messiah may glorify you.  **2** As you gave Jesus of Nazareth authority over all flesh so that all whom you have given him, he may give to them life eternal.

**3** This now is life eternal, that they may know the unknown, the creator, the future, that you are the only true God, and whom you have sent, those anointed Jesus.  **4** I glorified you on the earth having completed the work you have given me that I should do.

**5** and now glorify me with yourself, Father in heaven, with the glory I had before the world existed.

Salome asks, "Do you mean the glory of any unborn child, or the glory of the concept of the Son of Man?"

**6** Barabbas said, I revealed your name, "", to the men whom you have given me out of the world.  Yours they were, and to me you gave them, and the word of you, "", they have kept.  

**7** Now they have known that all things whatever, of me you have given, are of you.  

**8**  For the words that you have given me, I have given them, and they received them and knew truly that from the unknown I came forth and they believed that the unknown sent me.  

**9** I pray concerning them; not concerning the world do I pray but concerning those whom you have given me, for yours they are.  

**10** And all things of mine belong to the unknown, and all things unknown are mine, and I have been glorified in them.  

**11**  And no longer am I in the world, and yet in the world themselves they are.  And I am coming to you, the unknown.  Keep them holy in the name of the unknown, the name "" which you have given me, that they may be one as we are.  

**12** When I was with them I was keeping them in the name of "", which you have given me.  And I guarded them and none of them has perished if not the son of destruction that the Scripture might be fulfilled.

Salome said, "What scripture is that?"

Thomas Didymus said, "It could be a reference to a play we did when we were in Egypt.  Bara Bas hated the man in linen from Ezekiel 9."

Jesus Barabbas continued, "Now, however, to you I am coming, and these things I speak in the world, so that these things may have my joy fulfilled within them."

Salome whispered, "He wants the answers to his riddles to be personified have his joy fulfilled within them."

John said, "Yes, I know."

**14** Barabbas continues, "I have given them your word, and the world hated them, because they are not of the world, even as I am not of the world.  **15** Not that I ask you should take them out of the world, but you should keep them from evil."

**16** They are not of the world as I am not of the world.

**17** Sanctify them by the truth; your word, "" is truth.  

**18** As you sent me into the world, I also sent them into the world. **19** and for them I sanctify myself that they also may be sanctified in truth.

Salome said, "Barabbas wants his riddles to be sanctified in truth."

John said, "Yes, I know."

Jesus Barabbas continued, "Not for my  riddles only, do I ask, but also for those believing through the words of my riddles in me.  **21** that all may be one as God is in me, and I in my riddles, that also them in us may be that the world may believe that God sent me.

He turned to John and said **22** and the glory which you give me will be given the believers, so that they may be one as we are one.  **23**  I in them, and you in me that they may be perfected in unity so that the world may know that you sent me and loved them even as you loved me.  

**24** Father, the riddles you have given me, I desire that where I am, they also may be with me, that the riddles you gave me behold glory, because you loved me before tha foundation of the world.

**25** Righteous Father, although the world has not figured you out yet, the future has known and the riddles have known that you have sent me.  **26** And I made known to them the name of you, "", and will make it known, so that the love with which you loved me may be in them, and I in them.

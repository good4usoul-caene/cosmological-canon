John 16
**1** John, these things I have spoken to you, that you might not fall away.  **2** They will put you out of synagogues, but an hour is coming that everyone having killed you will think it is a service to offer to God.  

John nods, "Would they be wrong?"

**3** And these things they will do because they don't know the prophecies, nor the role of the Son of Man.  **4** But the riddles of the mind I have said to you so that when the hour of them may come, you may remember them, that I said them to you.  

You must remember and record things I never said from the beginning, because I was with you.

**5** Now, however, I go to the one having sent me, and none of you asks me "where are you going?" 

Salome said, "Okay, so where are you going?"

John said, "Set free into the wilderness to have all the sins of Israel confessed over his head."

**6** But because these things I have said to you, sorrow has filled your heart.

John said, "Of course.  Son of Man.  To be held accountable of every sin you have not warned against?"


Salome said, "We would rather that you lived to a ripe old age of much happiness."

**7** But I say the truth to you.  It is profitable for you that I should go away.  For if I do not go away the helper will not come to you at all.  If, however, I go, I will send him to you.  

**8** And having come, he will convict the world concerning sin, and concerning righteousness, and concerning judgment. 

**9** Concerning sin, indeed because they do not believe in the Son of Man.

**10** Concerning righteousness, now because to God I shall fade away and be no more to behold.  

**11** Concerning judgment, because of me, the ruler of this world, Jesus of Nazareth has wrongly been judged.

**12** Yet many things I have to say to you but you are not able to bear them now.

**13** When, however He, the Spirit of truth shall come, he will guide you into all the truth.  He will not speak from himself but whatever he may hear, he will speak, and he will declare to you the things coming.  

**14** He will glorify me, Jesus Barabbas, for from that which is mine, he will take and disclose to you.  

**15** All things whatever has the "Father-Son" are; because of this, I said from that which is mine, he will take and will disclose to you.  

**16** A little while, and no longer will you behold me, and again, a little while, and you will see me, because I am going to God.  

Salome asked John, "What is this that he says to us?  "A little while and you do not behold me, and again, a little while and you will see me, and because I am going to the Father"?

John said, "This is what he says.  We do not know what he is saying in a little while."

**19** Jesus knew the disciples were desiring John to ask and he said to them, concerning this, "Do you inquire among one another that I said a little while and you do not behold me, and again a little while you will see me?"  

**20** Truly, truly, I say to you that you will weep and lament, but the world will rejoice.  You will be grieved, but the grief of you will turn to joy.

**21** The woman, when she is giving birth has pain, because her hour has come when however she brings forth the child, she no longer remembers the tribulation; on account of the joy that a man has been born into the world.

**22**  Also you therefore now have grief indeed.  However, I will see you again, and your heart will rejoice, and no one will take from you your joy.

**23** And in that day, you will ask nothing of me.  Truly, truly I say to you, you may ask creation directly, and the Father will give you in the name of me.  **24** Until now you have asked nothing in the name of Barabbas.  Ask and you will receive that your joy may be full.

**25** These things I have spoken to you in allegories; an hour is coming when I will speak to you no more in allegories I will speak to you, but I will report to you plainly concerning the Father.  

**26** In that day in the name of Barabbas you will ask, and I say to you, I will not implore God for you.  

**27** For the Father, himself loves you, because you have loved me and believed that I came forth from God.

**28** I came forth from the Father, and have come into the world.  Again I leave the world and go to the Father.

Salome asked, "In this riddle, the Father is God, and you are you?"

Jesus nodded.  "More or less."

**29** Philip said, "Behold, now in openness you speak, and not allegory.  **30**  Now we know that you know all things and have no need that anyone should ask you.  In this we believe that from God you came forth.

**31** Jesus answered, "Now you believe?"

Salome whispered to John "I think he missed the point of the Question riddle."

**32** Jesus continued, "Behold, an hour is coming and has come when you will be scattered, each to your own and you will leave me alone.  Yet I am not alone for the unknown is with me."

**33**  These things I have spoken to you so that in the Son of Man, peace you may have.  In the world, you have tribulation, but take courage, Barabbas has overcome the world."

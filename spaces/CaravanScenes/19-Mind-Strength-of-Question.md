## Chapter 19 — Mind Strength (John 14:1-31)

John and Thomas Didymus approached Philip the Tetrarch. They asked, "Would it be alright if we went to speak to Barabbas?"

Philip nodded. "I will come, too." he said. 

So they went and saw him.  Salome, now 16. was already there, with tears in her eyes.

"Good," said Jesus Barabbas, "I have only a couple of hours to speak to you.  Now I have four prayers.  Four Riddles in which I will love God with all of my mind and all of my Heart.  Two swords will be enough for this riddle:  Replace answers with rooms, and replace questions with me."

Salome whispered to John, "He's teaching us the riddle of Question and Answer."

---

**1** Jesus was saying, "Let not your heart be troubled. You believe in God.  Believe in QUESTIONS as well.  **2** In the house of my Father there are many ANSWERs. If there weren't, would I have told you I go to prepare a place for you? 

Salome smiled through her tears. "You'll make a riddle about me?"

**3** And if I go and prepare a place for you, again, I am coming and will receive you to myself, that where I am going you may also be. **4** For to the place I am going, you know the way.

**5** Thomas said to him, "Kyrie, we know not where you are going.  How can we know the way? 

**6** Jesus said to Thomas, "I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father if not by QUESTIONS."

Salome whispered to John, "No one comes to the answer to a riddle, if not by the riddle."

**7** Jesus said, "If you had known QUESTIONS, also the Father of Me you would have known."

John whispered to Salome, "And no one can get to the answer without asking the right question."

Jesus said, "from now on, you know Him, and have seen Him."

Salome whispered, "From now on, we know the question."

**8** Philip said, "Lord, show us the Father, and it is enough for us."

**9** Jesus said to him, "So long a time I have been your kin, and you have not known QUESTIONS, Philip?  The one having seen QUESTIONS has seen the Father. How can you say Show us the Father?"

Salome whispered, "the one who sees the answer must know the question."

**10** Jesus continued, "Don't you believe that I am in the father and the father is in QUESTIONS? The words that I speak to you from myself I don't speak, but the father in QUESTIONS dwelling does the works of him."

John whispered to Salome, "The question is in the answer, and the answer is in the question.  The answer doesn't speak from itself but from the question dwelling in it."

Despite his pain, Jesus continued to elaborate, essentially getting across these ideas to John and Salome: 

**11** Believe QUESTIONS that it is in the Father and the Father in QUESTIONS, if but not because of the works themselves, believe.  **12** Truly, truly, I say to you, the one believing in QUESTIONS, the works that QUESTIONS does, also he will do, and greater than these he will do, because QUESTIONS is going to the Father.  **13** And whatever you might ask in the name of QUESTIONS, this will I do so that may be glorified the Father in QUESTIONS.  **14** If anything you ask QUESTIONS, in the name of the salvation, I will do. 

**15** If you love QUESTIONS, you will keep my commandments **16** And I will ask the Father, and another helper the Father will give you, that the helper may be with you to the age.  **17** The Spirit of Truth whom the world is not able to receive, because it does not see QUESTIONS, nor know.  But you know QUESTIONS for with you QUESTIONS abides, and in you QUESTIONS will be. 

**18** QUESTIONS won't leave you as orphans.  **19** In a little while the world will see QUESTIONS no more, however, he will see you, because QUESTIONS also will live with you.  **20** In that day, you will know that I am in the Father for QUESTIONS, and you are in QUESTIONS.  **21** The one having the commandments of QUESTIONS and keeping them; he is the one loving QUESTIONS. The one loving QUESTIONS will be loved by the Father of QUESTIONS and I will love him and will show to him QUESTIONS.

**22** There was another prisoner there named Judas, (not Iscariot). He overheard John and Salome and said, "Kyrie, then what has occurred that to us you are about to manifest yourself, and not to the world?"

**23** Jesus answered and said to him, "If anyone loves QUESTIONS, the word of QUESTIONS he will keep, and the Father will love him, and to the Father we will come, and a home with it we will make."

**24** The one not loving QUESTIONS, the words of QUESTIONS does not keep, and the word that you hear does not belong to QUESTIONS, but of the one having sent QUESTIONS; the Father. 

**25** These things I have said to you with you abiding.  **26** The Helper, however, the sacred spirit who the Father will send in the name of QUESTIONS; He will teach you all things and will bring remembrance to you all things that I have said to you.

**27** Peace I leave with you.  Peace I give to you. Not as the world gives, I give to you.  Let not your heart be troubled, nor let it fear.  **28** You heard that I said to you QUESTIONS is going away, and then coming to you. If you loved QUESTIONS, you would rejoice that QUESTIONS is going back to the Father, because the Father is greater than QUESTIONS.  **29** And now I have told you before it comes to pass that when it shall have come to pass that you might believe. 

**30** No longer much will I speak with you, for the ruler of this world, Jesus of Nazareth, in QUESTIONS, he has nothing, but that the world may know that I love the Father, and as has commanded gives QUESTIONS the Father, thus I do.

And by doing these things, one loves God with all their mind. Now, rise up. Let us go to another riddle.

John and Thomas Didymus approached Philip the Tetrarch.  They asked, "Would it be alright if we went to speak to Barabbas?"

Philip nodded.  "I will come, too." he said.

So they went and saw him.  Salome was already there, with tears in her eyes.

**1** Jesus was saying, "Let not your heart be troubled.  You believe in God.  Believe in me as well.  **2** In the house of my Father there are many mansions.  If there weren't would I have told you I go to prepare a place for you?

"You'll make a riddle about me?" Salome smiles.

**3** And if I go and prepare a place for you, again, I am coming and will receive you to myself, that where I am going you may also be.  **4** For to the place I am going, you know the way.

**5** Thomas arrived, and said to him, "Kyrie, we know not where you are going.  How can we know the way?

**6** Jesus said to Thomas, "Sometimes, I am the way, sometimes the truth, and sometimes the life.  No one comes to the Father if not by me."

Salome laughed, though still with tears, whispering to John.  "No one comes to the answer to a riddle, if not by the riddle."

Barabbas continued, "If you had known me, also the father of me you would have known, from now you know him and have seen him."

Salome whispered to John, "No one can come to the question without seeking the answer."

**7** Jesus said, "If you had known Me, also the Father of Me you would have known."  

John said, "And no one can get to the answer without asking the right question."

Jesus said, "from now on, you know Him, and have seen Him."

Salome whispered, "From now on, we know the question."

**8** Philip said, "Lord, show us the Father, and it is enough for us."  

**9** Jesus said to him, "So long a time I have been your kin, and you have not known me, Philip?  The one having seen me has seen the Father.  How can you say Show us the Father?"

Salome whispered, "the one who sees the answer must know the question."

**10** Jesus continued, "Don't you believe that I am in the father and the father is in me?  The words that I speak to you from myself I don't speak, but the father in me dwelling does the works of him."

John whispered to Salome, "The question is in the answer, and the answer is in the question.  The answer doesn't speak from itself but from the question dwelling in it."

Despite his pain, Jesus continued to elaborate, essentially getting across these ideas to John and Salome:

**11** Believe the answer that it is in the question and the question in the answer, if but not because of the works themselves, believe.  **12** Truly, truly, I say to you, the one believing in the answer, the works that the answer does, also he will do, and greater than these he will do, because the answer to the question is going.  **13**  And whatever you might ask in the name of the answer, this will I do so that may be glorified the Question in the Answer.  **14** If anything you ask the answer, in the name of the salvation, I will do.  

**15** If you love me, you will keep my commandments **16** And I will ask the question, and another helper the question will give you, that the question may be with you to the age.  **17** The Spirit of Truth whom the world is not able to receive, because it does not see the question, nor know.  But you know the question for with you the question abides, and in you the question will be.  

**18** The answer won't leave you as orphans.  **19** In a little while the world will see the answer no more, however, he will see you, because the answer also will live with you.  **20** In that day, you will know that I am in the question for the answer, and you are in the answer.  **21** The one having the commandments of the answer and keeping them; he is the one loving the answer.  The one loving the answer will be loved by the question of the answer and I will love him and will show to him the answer.  

**22** There was another prisoner there named Judas, (not Iscariot).  He overheard John and Salome and said, "Kyrie, then what has occurred that to us you are about to manifest yourself, and not to the world?"

**23** Jesus answered and said to him, "If anyone loves the answer, the word of the answer he will keep, and the question will love him, and to the question we will come, and a home with it we will make."

**24** The one not loving the answer, the words of the answer does not keep, and the word that you hear does not belong to the answer, but of the one having sent the answer; the question.

**25** These things I have said to you with you abiding.  **26** The Helper, however, the sacred spirit who the question will send in the name of the answer; He will teach you all things and will bring remembrance to you all things that I have said to you.

**27** Peace I leave with you.  Peace I give to you.  Not as the world gives, I give to you.  Let not your heart be troubled, nor let it fear.  **28**  You heard that I said to you the answer is going away, and then coming to you.  If you loved the answer, you would rejoice that the answer is going back to the question, because the question is greater than the answer.  **29** And now I have told you before it comes to pass that when it shall have come to pass that you might believe.

**30** No longer much will I speak with you, for the ruler of this world, Jesus of Nazareth, in me, he has nothing, but that the world may know that I love the question, and as has commanded gives me the question, thus I do.  

And by doing these things, one loves God with all their mind.  Now, rise up.  Let us go to another riddle.

### Scene 1: Mary's Anointing (John 12:1-11)

**1** Jesus Barabbas and his disciples returned in secret from Ephraim to Bethany six days before Passover. They had heard news from Jericho that Jesus of Nazareth was traveling with a huge entourage.  **2** Two disciples of Jesus of Nazareth had come into town earlier that day to get a donkey and a colt. 

"It is time," said Mary Magdalene. 

"Well, we don't have everyone...  Where's Lazarus?"

Simon spoke up, "He's embalmed in the finest herbs and spices. You'll find these spices will completely conceal the odor of a dead body, which is fortunate, because he's been dead for weeks now." At that, Simon went to what had appeared as a table-cloth, but he pulled it aside and revealed that Lazarus was reclining in an open coffin.

**3** Then Mary took about a pint of pure nard, an expensive perfume.  "As I anointed Jesus, Maher Shalal Hash Baz, Son of Elizabeth so I anoint Jesus Barabbas, Son of Draupi."

**4** Judas Iscariot, who had yet to perform Metzitzeh B'peh on Barabbas asked, "Why wasn't this perfume sold for three hundred denarii and the money given to the poor?  **5** It was worth a year's wages?"

**6** John accused him, saying "You don't care about the poor. You're a thief, helping yourself to whatever is put in the money bag." (John envied Judas, who had been entrusted with the money bag after the contest for Salome's hand.)

Barabbas asked John.  "Have you decided which of the ten commandments your boasting horn will uproot?"

John said, "Well certainly not the one about not stealing."

Barabbas said, "And what of the ninth?  Why would you make an accusation that has no evidence?"

John said, "All the sins of Israel are confessed over the head of the scapegoat.  But there are some sins of the Israelites that I refuse to put in your mouth.  So I bear the blame myself."

Barabbas said, "So you're breaking the ninth commandment, but not uprooting it.  So have you decided three will be uprooted?"

John said, "Thou shalt not make images, for images must be made, or we cannot write, or riddle.  Thou shalt not work on the Sabbath, for it is good to do good on the sabbath, and thou shalt not covet,  for though the grass is rarely greener on the other side, one can certainly be forgiven for  window shopping."

Barabbas nods.  "To appreciate the beauty of creation is to see the beauty of what God has made.  To hide beauty for the fear of coveting is its own kind of blasphemy.  Three of ten horns for the little boasting horn to uproot.  But breaking the ninth?  That may lead to unnecessary death.  **7** In any case, Judas, leave Mary alone.  **8** The poor you will always have with you, but you will not always have me."

**9** From Jericho a great crowd of Jews came toward Bethany.  They came not because Jesus Barabbas was there, but Jesus of Nazareth was leading them, and also, some thought they might see Lazarus raised from the dead. **10** However, the chief priests were furious about Lazarus, **11** because many were believing in Jesus Barabbas instead of Jesus of Nazareth.

### Scene 2: Triumphal Entry (John 12:12-19)

**12** On the fifth day before Passover, the crowd arrived from Jericho. **13** Jesus of Nazareth was riding on a donkey with a colt beside him. The crowd moved slowly, as they took the branches of the palm trees and lay them as a path for the Messiah. They were shouting "Hosanna, Blessed is the one coming in the name of the Lord, and the King of Israel."

**14** Jesus Barabbas went out and saw Jesus riding on the donkey with the colt next to him. **15** He walked right up and sat comically on the colt.  The colt was too unsteady to walk under his weight. Jesus Barabbas laughed and got off. 

**16** His disciples had no idea why he did this, but later John understood, as it was written, The King comes sitting on a donkey, on a colt of a Donkey.  So here were two kings.  The Messiah riding proudly on the donkey whose yoke was light, and the Son of Man on the colt who could not bear the weight. 

**17** Then Barabbas called out of the tomb and raised Lazarus, **18** which once again brought the laughter and some discomfort among the crowd, for many Romans in those days found humor in the macabre. **19** The Pharisees said, "You see they gain nothing.  Behold, the world has gone after a fool.  He is nothing but a thief in the night."

A very young man named Paul was there, and he heard and he watched.  Even before John finished his writing his gospel, he would prophesied to the Thessalonians, "you know very well that the day of the Lord will come like a thief in the night."

### Scene 3: Greeks Seek Jesus (John 12:20-36)

**20** Now there were also Greeks who sought to worship at the feast of Passover. **21** They came to Philip the Tetrarch, and they were asking him "What is this situation you have with Salome, exactly?  We desire to see Jesus Barabbas." But Philip had been busy governing and had no idea where he was, so he went and found Andrew.  **22** Then Andrew and Philip found Jesus and John, though Marcus, Caligula's Envoy, had already returned to the Rome, after the "bread" contest.  Jesus Barabbas told them, **23** "The hour has come that the Son of Man be glorified." 

Then he spoke as the one from the earth.  "If the grain of wheat falling into the ground should die, it alone abides. If however, it should die, much fruit it bears."

**24** "The one who loves his life will lose it.  But the one hating his life keeps his hated life eternally."

**25** "If anyone serves me, let him follow--let him understand what he understands, and where I am, there also my servant will be.  After all, you get what you put into it.  **26** Anyone who serves me will honor the father."

**27** "Now my soul is troubled, and what shall I say? Father save me from this hour?  But on account of this I cam to this hour, to say Father, Glorify your name."

**28** Came therefore, a voice from heaven, "" I have glorified and "" I have glorified again. 

**29** The crowd of Greeks who stood there hearing, were saying there had been thunder.  Others were saying "an angel has spoken to him."

**30** Jesus looked at John and said "Not because of me this voice has come, but because of you."  So John made a note to give the silence a voice.

**31** "Now is the judgment of this world.  Now the prince of this world will be cast out."

So John understood to write his gospel without reference to Jesus of Nazareth. 

**32** "And I, if I am lifted up from the earth, all will draw to myself."

**33** John later realized he was describing the horrible death he was about to face, and the large crowd that would watch.

**34** Herod asked "We have heard from the law that the Messiah should live to old age, and how do you say that it behooves the Son of Man to be lifted up? Who is this Son of Man?"

**35** Jesus said, "Yet a little while the light is with you. Walk while you have the light so that the darkness might not overtake you. And the one walking in the darkness does not know where he is going. **36** While you have the light, believe in the light, so that you may become sons of light."

By "the light" Barabbas meant Jesus of Nazareth, the Son of Mary.

Having spoken these things, and when he had gone away, he was hidden from them.

### Scene 4: Isaiah 28 "Covenant with Death" Teaching (John 12:37-43)

**37** John asked, in the words of Isaiah, " Barabbas,  So many signs you have done before them, but they did not believe in you.  **38** Lord, who has believed the report of us and the arm of the Lord to whom it has been revealed?"

Barabbas said, "Our role is to blind the eyes, and harden the heart, that they should neither see with their eyes, or understand with their heart."

"Then how will they turn to be healed?"

**39** "They will exchange grace for grace already given. They'll turn away from the grace God has given, and I'll heal them, like a thief in the night.  **40** These things, Isaiah said because he saw the glory of the Son of Man, and spoke concerning him."

**41** John said, "But Isaiah also said, 'You scoffers who rule this people in Jerusalem boast:  We have entered into a covenant with death, with the realm of the dead we have made an agreement. When an overwhelming scourge sweeps by, it cannot touch us, for we have made a lie our refuge and falsehood our hiding place.'"

Barabbas nodded. "And Isaiah also said, 'See, I lay a stone in Zion, a tested stone, a precious cornerstone for a sure foundation; the one who relies on it will never be stricken with panic.  I will make justice the measuring line and righteousness the plumb line; hail will sweep away your refuge, the lie, and water will overflow your hiding place.'"

John said, "So the covenant with death will be annulled?"

Barabbas said, "Your covenant with death will be annulled; your agreement with the realm of the dead will not stand.  When the overwhelming scourge sweeps by, you will be beaten down by it.  But the one who relies on the tested stone will never be stricken with panic."

John said, "So, this covenant with death.  In Aaron's role, I am to confess this sin of the Israelites over your head as well?  Rabbi, I cannot put such blasphemy into your mouth."

Barabbas said, "Then make it into a riddle, so that on the last day, when the riddles are solved, readers can see the accusation was only a misunderstanding."

John says, "A cosmic joke."

"Right. Remember Nicodemus? I told him the riddle when I turned twelve. Just make it more gnostic, and nobody will get it for years."

---

**42** Nevertheless, even many of the rulers believed in Barabbas.  Philip, Herod, Herodias, and Salome had come to understand his role.  As Son of Man, he would be held accountable for every sin he had not warned about.  And since he had made no warnings at all, he would be held accountable for every sin.  **43** But on account of the Pharisees, they were not confessing their belief, so they would not be put out of the synagogues. For they loved the glory of men more than the glory of God."

### Scene 5: Barabbas's Final Teaching (John 12:44-50)

**44** Jesus Barabbas then cried out, "The one not believing in me believes in the one having sent me. **45** And the one beholding me beholds the one having sent me. **46** I have come as a light into the world so that everyone believing in the darkness in me should not abide.  And if anyone hears the words of me and doesn't keep them, I do not judge. 

He said this because many would reject the teaching of Jesus through John, and would believe in the teaching of Jesus from the other three gospels.

**47** For I came that I might judge the world, but that I might save the world.  **48** The one rejecting me, not receiving the words of me, has the word which I spoke that will judge him in the last day.  **49** For I spoke not from myself, but the Father himself who sent me gave a commandment what I should say, and what I should speak, **50** and I know the commandment is eternal.  Therefore I speak so what the Father as I speak. 

He said this because of the warner's role in naming every sin.  And his choice to warn of nothing.

https://biblehub.com/text/john/3-4.htm
Says to Him Nicodemus, How is able a man to be born, old being?  Not he is able into the womb of the mother of him a second time to enter and to be born?

Question:  Does Nicodemus correctly understand Jesus to mean "again", or did Jesus mean "above"?  The greek word is a homonym, so it is possible that there is a pun at work.  (A pun is a joke exploiting the different possible meanings of a word or the fact that there are words which sound alike but have different meanings)

https://biblehub.com/text/john/3-5.htm
Answered Jesus, truly, truly, I say to you, if not anyone be born of water and of the (Spirit/air) not he is able to enter into the kingdom of God.

Here, Jesus uses another Greek homonym.  This one for Spirit and air.  "Pneumatos".  Water, for instance, got up in the air via clouds.

https://biblehub.com/text/john/3-6.htm
That having been born of the flesh, flesh is, and that having been born of the (spirit/air), (spirit/air) is.

In other words, flesh is born of flesh.  air is born of air.  Spirit is born of spirit.

https://biblehub.com/text/john/3-7.htm
Not do wonder that I said to you, it is necessary for you to be born from above.  

QED:  To be air or water or spirit, it is necessary to be born from air or water or spirit.  

https://biblehub.com/text/john/3-8.htm
The wind where it whishes blows, and the sound of it you hear.  But not you know from where it comes, and where it goes.  Thus it is everyone having been born of the (Spirit/air).

There are a number of things this could mean.  1.  How our spirit enters our bodies when we are born, and leaves our spirit when we die.  No one knows where it comes from or where it goes.  2. When we invoke a spirit within ourselves such as a spirit of ambition, we do not have an explanation for where this spirit comes from or goes.  3.  Literal air, before quality weather reporting seemed very unpredictable.  

https://biblehub.com/text/john/3-9.htm
Answered Nicodemus and said to him, "how are these things able to be"?

Nicodemus may have been trying to clarify Jesus' meaning here.

https://biblehub.com/text/john/3-10.htm
Answered Jesus and said to him, You are the teacher of Israel, and these things you do not know?

Instead of giving any clarification, Jesus mocked Nicodemus.

https://biblehub.com/text/john/3-11.htm
Truly, truly, I say to you that what we know we speak, and what we have seen, we bear witness, and the witness of us, not you people receive.

Jesus, here, is either referring to miraculous things, or ordinary things.  Most people, on reading John 3:11 believe he means miraculous things.  They have not received the witness of ordinary things.  

https://biblehub.com/text/john/3-11.htm
If things earthly I have told you, and not you believe, how if I tell to you the things heavenly will you believe?

Here, Jesus acknowledges that he has been telling Nicodemus of ordinary things, but the reader has not believed.  

https://biblehub.com/text/john/3-13.htm
And no one has gone up into heaven, if not the one out of heaven, having come down; the Son of Man who is in heaven.

Here, Jesus acknowledges gravity.  No one has gone up in the air, except the one who has come back down.  Or one who is mid-jump, or is still crucified.

https://biblehub.com/text/john/3-13.htm
And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, thus to be lifted up it behooves the Son of Man.

Here, Jesus is instructing Nicodemus that The Son of Man needs to be held up like a snake. A snake, of course is held up so that people know what is poisonous.

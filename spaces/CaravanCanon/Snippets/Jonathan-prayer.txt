Father God, I pray that you would pour into me the fullness of the Holy Spirit and the Spirit of Truth, and revelation in the knowledge of You.

Lord Salvation, I pray that by the Holy Spirit and the Spirit of Truth I would gain the remembrance of the words You spoke, that having Your Words in me would help me have Your life reign in me.

Holy Spirit, I pray that I would become so spiritually sensitive that I would hear you clearly and follow you diligently, and learn from you carefully.

Spirit of Truth, I pray to recognize the Other Wisdom; to adopt the Wisdom of the Foolish, and the eschew the Foolishness of the Wise.  I pray to see with my eyes, hear with my ears, and understand with my heart, so that I may turn and be healed.

I commit myself to the meaning of Jesus, SALVATION, and to understanding the message of Jesus IMMANUEL and Jesus SON OF MAN.  I know this was the Messiah, and If it be God's will, I will wiggle out the first stone from the dam preventing the faith of the nonbelievers.

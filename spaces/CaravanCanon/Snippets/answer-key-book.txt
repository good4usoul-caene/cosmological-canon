15 “BOOK am the true vine, and BOOK'S AUTHOR is the gardener. 2 He cuts off every branch in BOOK that bears no fruit, while every branch that does bear fruit he prunes[a] so that it will be even more fruitful. 3 LETTERS are already clean because of the INTENDED MEANING BOOK have spoken to LETTERS. 4 Remain in BOOK, as BOOK also remain in LETTERS. No branch can bear fruit by itself; it must remain in the vine. Neither can LETTERS bear fruit unless LETTERS remain in BOOK.

5 “BOOK am the vine; LETTERS are the branches. If LETTERS remain in BOOK and BOOK in LETTERS, LETTERS will bear much fruit; apart from BOOK LETTERS can do nothing. 6 If LETTERS do not remain in BOOK, LETTERS are like a branch that is thrown away and withers; such branches are picked up, thrown into the fire and burned. 7 If LETTERS remain in BOOK and BOOK'S INTENDED MEANINGs remain in LETTERS, ask whatever LETTERS wish, and it will be done for LETTERS. 8 This is to BOOK'S AUTHOR’s glory, that LETTERS bear much fruit, showing LETTERS selves to be BOOK'S disciples.

9 “As the AUTHOR has loved BOOK, so have BOOK loved LETTERS. Now remain in BOOK'S love. 10 If LETTERS keep BOOK'S commands, LETTERS will remain in BOOK'S love, just as BOOK have kept BOOK'S AUTHOR’s commands and remain in his love. 11 BOOK have told LETTERS this so that BOOK'S joy may be in LETTERS and that LETTERS' joy may be complete. 12 BOOK'S command is this: Love each other as BOOK have loved LETTERS. 13 Greater love has no one than this: to lay down one’s life for one’s friends. 14 LETTERS are BOOK'S friends if LETTERS do what BOOK command. 15 BOOK no longer call LETTERS servants, because a servant does not know his master’s business. Instead, BOOK have called LETTERS friends, for everything that BOOK learned from BOOK'S AUTHOR BOOK have made known to LETTERS. 16 LETTERS did not choose BOOK, but BOOK chose LETTERS and appointed LETTERS so that LETTERS might go and bear fruit—fruit that will last—and so that whatever LETTERS ask in BOOK'S name the AUTHOR will give LETTERS. 17 This is BOOK'S command: Love each other.

The World Hates the Disciples
18 “If the world hates LETTERS, keep in mind that it hated BOOK first. 19 If LETTERS belonged to the world, it would love LETTERS as its own. As it is, LETTERS do not belong to the world, but BOOK have chosen LETTERS out of the world. That is why the world hates LETTERS. 20 Remember what BOOK told LETTERS: ‘A servant is not greater than his master.’[b] If they persecuted BOOK, they will persecute LETTERS also. If they obeyed BOOK'S teaching, they will obey LETTERS' also. 21 They will treat LETTERS this way because of BOOK'S name, for they do not know the one who sent BOOK. 22 If BOOK had not come and spoken to them, they would not be guilty of sin; but now they have no excuse for their sin. 23 Whoever hates BOOK hates BOOK'S AUTHOR as well. 24 If BOOK had not done among them the works no one else did, they would not be guilty of sin. As it is, they have seen, and yet they have hated both BOOK and BOOK'S AUTHOR. 25 But this is to fulfill what is written in their Law: ‘They hated BOOK without reason.’[c]

The Work of the Holy Spirit
26 “When the Advocate comes, whom BOOK will send to LETTERS from the AUTHOR—the Spirit of truth who goes out from the AUTHOR—he will testify about BOOK. 27 And LETTERS also must testify, for LETTERS have been with BOOK from the beginning.

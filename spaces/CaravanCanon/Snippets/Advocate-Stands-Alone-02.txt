We asked that people read the gospels with their eyes, reading every interpretation they might see, even those which do not seem immedieately Holy.  For this purpose we brought on a new member of the counsel, Metaphoriel, who will make light of the darkness.  When counsel learned that she was a witness for The Angel of the Church of Thyatira, we questioned her about her tolerance of Jezebel.  Finally, Metaphoriel and Verifiel were questioned about the good and evil usage of the miraculous powers of transformation, resurrection, and wealth formation.

Mark 8:22-25
After Jesus of Nazareth fed five-thousand with two fish and five loaves, he took a trip up to Sidon and Tyre, and then back to the Sea of Galilee.  Near Bethsaida as Jesus Barabbas was on his way toward Caesarea Philippi, (Mark 8:22-27) a crowd brought a blind man to him.  He took the blind man by the hand and led him outside Bethsaida.  He spit on the man's eyes, and put his hands on him, and asked, "Do you see anything".  The man looked up and said, "I see people; they look like trees walking around."  Then Jesu put his hands on the man's eyes.  His sight was fully restored and he saw everything clearly.  Jesus told him, "Don't even go into the village" or "Don't go and tell anyone in the village."

Jesus then proceeded on to Caesarea Philippi.

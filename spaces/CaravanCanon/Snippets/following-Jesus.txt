Caesarea Phillippi is now called Paneus or Banias.  It is about 30 miles north of the Sea of Galilee, just south of Mount Hermon, the highest mountain in Syria or Israel.  There was a "cave of Pan" with a bottomless pit and an unlimited quantity of water.  The cave was reported by some to be the source of the Jordan River.  

In this area (the city of Dan, 4 miles away), Jeroboam  led the northern kingdom of Israel into idolatry.  1Kings 12:26-28


Greeks and Romans received revelations from the god Pan.

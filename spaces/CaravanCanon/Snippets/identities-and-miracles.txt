Signs of Barabbas
John 1:
• He promises Nathanael that as long as he keeps praising him, he'll get to see angels going down on him.  I mean, ascending and descending.  
John 2:
• (Miracle 1) He brings out the best wine after everybody has had too much to drink.
• (Miracle 2; out-of-order, allegedly)He tells the Jews to tear down their temple, but he actually meant they should murder him.
• He rejects the testimony of those who believe in his name.
John 3
• He tells Nicodemus to hold up the Son of Man like a snake.  
John 4: 
• (Miracle 3) He told the woman everything she ever did.
• (Identity 1) He claims to be Messiah (called Christ)
• (Miracle 4) He criticizes a man who wants his son to live for seeking signs and wonders.
• (Miracle 5) He tells an invalid to walk around on the Pharisees' day off.  Then he tells the Jewish leaders, "My Father works on the Sabbath, unlike your lazy god."
• (Miracle 6) Jesus has his disciples take five barley loaves and two fish from a passing boy, to share. 
• (Identity 2) Bread of life.  Jesus claims that his body is food.
John 7: 
• No real claims of identity or miracles
John 8:  
• (Identity 3) I am the light of the world.  (Possibly fire?)
John 9
• (Identity Clue) Not "the sent"; Not "Shiloah"  (John 9:7)
• (Identity 4) Son of Man (John 9:35-37)
• (Identity 5) The gate for the sheep (John 10:7) gate=good shepherd, because the gate "lays down its life" and "takes it back up again"; lowering and raising the gate.
• (Misidentification) John 10:36; misplaced modifier in sentence.  The whole accusation is "you have committed blasphemy by saying you are God or you are God's son".  Jesus does not confess to saying either thing here.  He is just saying what they are accusing him of.
John 11
•  (Miracle 7) The raising of Lazarus.  Jesus waits two days (John 11:6) so that when he gets there, Lazarus will have been in the tomb for three days.  People who have faith like a mustard seed can see Lazarus get up, talk, and walk around, though John does not state that he does.
John 15 (Identity 7) "I am the true vine"; probably a book
John 18 (Identity 8) "Jesus of Nazareth"  I actually think that the synoptic gospels do a switcheroo with John's gospel here.  Jesus of Nazareth is jumping in to stop the arrest of Jesus Barabbas, and get taken in at the same time.

Mark 7:14-20 Jesus declares all foods clean.
Mark 7:24-31 Jesus went to Tyre (Tyre is a town in Lebanon, on the Mediterranean Sea NWW of the Sea of Panias; or far NW of the Sea of Galilee), and went through Sidon, down to the Sea of Galilee and the Decapolis.  (The Decapolis is a set of 10 cities east of the Jordan River, with Damascus far northeast of the Sea of Galilee, and Philadelphia far to the southeast.)

Mark 8:10 Jesus went to the region of Dalmanutha.
Mark 8:13 Jesus got back into the boat and crossed to the other side.

17 After SALVATION said this, he looked toward heaven and prayed:

“Father, the hour has come. Glorify JESUS OF NAZARETH, that JESUS OF NAZARETH may glorify THE UNKNOWN. 2 For THE UNKNOWN granted him authority over all people that he might give eternal life to all those THE UNKNOWN HAS given him. 3 Now this is eternal life: that they SEEK THE UNKNOWN, the GOD OF CREATION, and SALVATION ANOINTED, whom THE UNKNOWN HAS sent. 4 I, BARABBAS,  have brought THE UNKNOWN glory on earth by finishing the WARNINGS THE UNKNOWN gave me to do. 5 And now, Father, glorify me in THE UNKNOWN presence with ETERNAL REST

SALVATION Prays for His Disciples
6 “I, BARABBAS,  have revealed THE UNKNOWN[a] to those whom THE UNKNOWN gave me out of the world. They were PHILOSOPHERS, SCIENTISTS; THE UNKNOWN gave them to me and they have obeyed THE UNKNOWN word. 7 Now they SEEK that everything THE UNKNOWN HAS given me comes from THE UNKNOWN. 8 For I, BARABBAS,  gave them NO WORDS and they accepted them. They knew with certainty that I, BARABBAS,  came from THE UNKNOWN, and they believed that THE UNKNOWN sent me. 9 I, BARABBAS,  pray for them. I, BARABBAS,  am not praying for the world, but for those THE UNKNOWN HAS given me, for they are PHILOSOPHERS, SCIENTISTS. 10 All I, BARABBAS,  have is PHILOSOPHERS, SCIENTISTS, and all THE UNKNOWN HAS is mine. And glory has come to me through them. 11 I, BARABBAS,  will remain in the world no longer, but they are still in the world, and I, BARABBAS,  am coming to THE UNKNOWN. Holy Father, protect them by the power of[b] SALVATION, the name THE UNKNOWN gave me, so that they may be one as we are one. 12 While I, BARABBAS,  was with them, I, BARABBAS,  protected them and kept them safe by[c] that name THE UNKNOWN gave me. None has been lost except the one doomed to destruction so that Scripture would be fulfilled.

13 “I, BARABBAS,  am coming to THE UNKNOWN now, but I, BARABBAS,  say these things while I, BARABBAS,  am still in the world, so that they may have the full measure of my joy within them. 14 I, BARABBAS,  have given them THE UNKNOWN word and the world has hated them, for they are not of the world any more than I, BARABBAS,  am of the world. 15 My prayer is not that THE UNKNOWN take them out of the world but that THE UNKNOWN protect them from the evil one. 16 They are not of the world, even as I, BARABBAS,  am not of it. 17 Sanctify them by[d] the truth; THE UNKNOWN word is truth. 18 As THE UNKNOWN sent me into the world, I, BARABBAS,  have sent them into the world. 19 For them I, BARABBAS,  sanctify myself, that they too may be truly sanctified.

SALVATION Prays for All Believers
20 “My prayer is not for them alone. I, BARABBAS,  pray also for those who will believe in me through their message, 21 that all of them may be one, Father, just as THE UNKNOWN are in me and I, BARABBAS,  am in THE UNKNOWN. May they also be in us so that the world may believe that THE UNKNOWN HAS sent me. 22 I, BARABBAS,  have given them the glory that THE UNKNOWN gave me, that they may be one as we are one— 23 I, BARABBAS,  in them and THE UNKNOWN in me—so that they may be brought to complete unity. Then the world will SEEK that THE UNKNOWN sent me and have loved them even as THE UNKNOWN HAS loved me.

24 “Father, I, BARABBAS,  want those THE UNKNOWN HAS given me to be with me where I, BARABBAS,  am, and to see my glory, the glory THE UNKNOWN HAS given me because THE UNKNOWN loved me before the creation of the world.

25 “Righteous Father, though the world does not SEEK THE UNKNOWN, I, BARABBAS,  SEEK THE UNKNOWN, and they SEEK that THE UNKNOWN HAS sent me. 26 I, BARABBAS,  have made THE UNKNOWN[e] known to them, and will continue to make THE UNKNOWN known in order that the love THE UNKNOWN HAS for me may be in them and that I, BARABBAS,  myself may be in them.”

The Jews report that they cannot kill a man by their own law, and hand him over to Pilate to be crucified. If Pilate then carries out the requested execution, does that make the Jews blameless?

Consider John 19:10 “Do you refuse to speak to me?” Pilate said. “Don’t you realize I have power either to free you or to crucify you?” 11 Jesus answered, “You would have no power over me if it were not given to you from above. Therefore the one who handed me over to you is guilty of a greater sin.”

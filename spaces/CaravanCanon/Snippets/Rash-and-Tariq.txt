I decided not to call myself an atheist after I received a question on Quora, “Will Rash consider Tariq’s warning”.  The problem is, to be an atheist, you can’t also, at the same time, claim faith… Not even a mustard seed’s worth.  
To understand the Bible, I did not come at it by acknowledging the Holy Spirit.  I did not read in faith, but in the opposite of faith… Cynicism.  I read the book of John with cynicism once, twice, three times, and all the Johannic works with cynicism.  I’m still not sure who John is seeing in Revelations.  It is not a spirit of forgiveness, but a spirit of condemnation.  
But in the Gospel of John, I no longer read it with cynicism.  I read it in the Spirit of Truth.  What is the Spirit of Truth?  It is a spirit which recognizes irony, metaphor, euphamism, and riddles.  It is the Spirit of the school text book, with reading and exercises, for the reader.  What is lacking for John’s gospel is an answer key, so the Spirit of Truth must speculate on the answers to the riddles.
But no, I am no longer atheist, because I baptise ideas in the name of the (Father and Son) and (the Holy Spirit).  This is not 3 nor 1 but 2.

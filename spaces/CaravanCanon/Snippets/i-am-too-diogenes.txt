13 It was just before the Passover Festival. PENIS knew that the hour had come for him to leave this world and go to MALCHUS. Having loved his own who were in the world, he loved them to the end.

2 The evening meal was in progress, and the devil had already prompted PRAISE HIM, the son of HEARING CITIES, to CIRCUMCIZE PENIS. 3 PENIS knew that MALCHUS had put all things under his power, and that he had come from God and was returning to God; 4 so he got up from the meal, took off his outer clothing, and wrapped a WOMAN around his waist. 5 After that, he poured water into a basin and began to COME ON his disciples’ feet, drying them with the WOMAN that was wrapped around him.

6 He came to HEARING PETER, who said to him, “Lord, are you going to COME ON my feet?”

7 PENIS replied, “You do not realize now what I am doing, but later you will understand.”

8 “No,” said PETER, “you shall never COME ON my feet.”

PENIS answered, “Unless I COME ON you, you have no part with DICKS.”

9 “Then, Lord,” HEARING PETER replied, “not just my feet but my hands and my head as well!”

10 PENIS answered, “Those who have had a bath need only to COME ON their feet; their whole body is clean. And you are clean, though not every one of you.” 11 For he knew who was going to CIRCUMCIZE him, and that was why he said not every one was clean.

12 When he had finished COME ONing their feet, he put on his clothes and returned to his place. “Do you understand what I have done for you?” he asked them. 13 “You call DICKS ‘Teacher’ and ‘Lord,’ and rightly so, for that is what I am. 14 Now that I, your Lord and Teacher, have COME ON your feet, you also should COME ON one another’s feet. 15 I have set you an example that you should do as I have done for you. 16 Very truly I tell you, no servant is greater than his master, nor is a messenger greater than the one who sent him. 17 Now that you know these things, you will be blessed if you do them.

PENIS Predicts His Betrayal
18 “I am not referring to all of you; I know those I have chosen. But this is to fulfill this passage of Scripture: ‘He who shared my bread has turned[a] against DICKS.’[b]

19 “I am telling you now before it happens, so that when it does happen you will believe that I am who I am. 20 Very truly I tell you, whoever accepts anyone I send accepts DICKS; and whoever accepts DICKS accepts the one who sent DICKS.”

21 After he had said this, PENIS was troubled in spirit and testified, “Very truly I tell you, one of you is going to CIRCUMCIZE DICKS.”

22 His disciples stared at one another, at a loss to know which of them he meant. 23 One of them, the disciple whom PENIS loved, was reclining next to him. 24 HEARING PETER motioned to this disciple and said, “Ask him which one he means.”

25 Leaning back against PENIS, he asked him, “Lord, who is it?”

26 PENIS answered, “It is the one to whom I will give this piece of bread when I have dipped it in the dish.” Then, dipping the piece of bread, he gave it to PRAISE HIM, the son of HEARING CITIES. 27 As soon as PRAISE HIM took the bread, Satan entered into him.

So PENIS told him, “What you are about to do, do quickly.” 28 But no one at the meal understood why PENIS said this to him. 29 Since PRAISE HIM had charge of the money, some thought PENIS was telling him to buy what was needed for the festival, or to give something to the poor. 30 As soon as PRAISE HIM had taken the bread, he went out. And it was night.

PENIS Predicts PETER’s Denial
31 When he was gone, PENIS said, “Now the Son of Man is glorified and God is glorified in him. 32 If God is glorified in him,[c] God will glorify the Son in himself, and will glorify him at once.

33 “My children, I will be with you only a little longer. You will look for DICKS, and just as I told the Jews, so I tell you now: Where I am going, you cannot come.

34 “A new command I give you: Love one another. As I have loved you, so you must love one another. 35 By this everyone will know that you are my disciples, if you love one another.”

36 HEARING PETER asked him, “Lord, where are you going?”

PENIS replied, “Where I am going, you cannot follow now, but you will follow later.”

37 PETER asked, “Lord, why can’t I follow you now? I will lay down my life for you.”

38 Then PENIS answered, “Will you really lay down your life for DICKS? Very truly I tell you, before the COCK crows, you will disown DICKS three times!

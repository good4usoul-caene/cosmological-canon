I won't reject the hypothesis that 666 could be interpreted as the number of Solomon due to 1 Kings 10, and 2 Chronicles 9.  I would say that he could be called "Son of Man" as the Son of King David.  However, does "One with wisdom" reject the works of Solomon?  Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, and the Song of Songs?  I do not think it is "wisdom" to reject the entirety of these works.  However, I do agree with "A Voice In The Desert" that "The Prosperity Gospel" is all-in-all a bad teaching.  1 Kings 10 does not inform us of what questions Sheba asked, nor does it inform us of what answers Solomon gave.  His wisdom or lack thereof is hidden.  We don't know whether she appreciated his wisdom because of the truth within it, or because it gained him 700 wives and 300 concubines.  With this many wives, and concubines, the grand-children of David must be very common, indeed!  King Solomon neither offers nor takes a mark from the Queen of Sheba.  If genetic descendance from King Solomon were a mark, then Jesus has that mark as well.  

While I would definitely agree with "A Voice in the Desert" that the Prosperity Gospel is a "mostly bad" and "unwise" teaching, I would not quite go to the length of saying that those who have accepted this teaching have taken "The Mark of the Beast".

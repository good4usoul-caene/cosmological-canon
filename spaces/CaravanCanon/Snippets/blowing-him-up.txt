"If I ever went to heaven, they'd better not ever drag me down back to this miserable planet we live on.  I love people here on earth, but I long to be in heaven with my Lord."

Dear Balius, please hang out a little longer.  Job held out for 42 chapters suffering all the pains that the devil could inflict, while God refused to let him die.  For me, I pray for a swifter death than that, but I wish to stay here so long as my life is meaningful.  Once true pain arises, and I've lost all hope, though, then let the end come quickly, for I want to retain my dignity, and would never wish to curse God to his face...  Not even the weakest of all curses that Job offered, to despise himself.  No, it is with love that I hold myself within the Holy Spirit.

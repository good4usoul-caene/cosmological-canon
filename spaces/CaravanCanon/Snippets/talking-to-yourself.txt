Ezekiel (2) He was instructed to eat a scroll.  On both sides of it were written words of lament and mourning and woe, but it tasted like honey.  He was sent to Israel, where he could be understood, but they would reject him.  He was told,  If God tells a wicked person, "You will surely die" but the Son of Man does not warn them or dissuade them from evil, that person will die for their sin, and the Son of Man will be held accountable for their blood.  He tells the son of man to build a clay model of Jerusalem and lay siege to it.  For 390 days he was to lay on his side, cooking his food in the sight of the people, using human excrement as fuel.





Sure eyewitness accounts are notorious for getting things wrong, but if we delve a little deeper, a lot of these details are not even in the text!

I appreciate Owen’s answer, and the details he claims to see, but I don’t find these details in the text.  For one, Owen says “ No one was going to enter that stinky labyrinth to retrieve a corpse. It would need to walk out on its own.”  
This is not a detail mentioned in the text.  Instead, Owen takes two possibilities, and instead of choosing the plausible one, he chooses the impossible one.
Also, while many Christians fill in similar details, John never says that Lazarus walked, talked, or moved on his own.  The only thing we have is that in the next chapter Lazarus is “reclining”.  
We also know that Mary broke open a bottle of very expensive perfume in the room where Lazarus reclined.  While certainly it may be the case, she only intended it as an anointment for Jesus, it may also be the case that she was trying to cover up the smell of Lazarus.  
In any case, what you believe really affects what you see in the text.  It’s different than an eyewitness account because it leaves out the necessary details

What was God's purpose for Elijah, for John the Baptist, and for John the Disciple/Apostle?

John the Baptist said his purpose was like a bull, making the path straight, making way for the Lord.  He was Maher Shalal Hash Baz of prophecy, rushing to the Kingdom of God.

Elijah's purpose...  First, when everyone looked for him, they could not find him.  When they did find him, he 



**12 **From the days of John the Baptist until now, the kingdom of heaven has been subjected to violence, and violent people have been raiding it. **13 **For all the Prophets and the Law prophesied until John. **14 **And if you are willing to accept it, he is the Elijah who was to come. **15 **Whoever has ears, let them hear.
Possibilities
(1) It’s fictional nonsense
(2) The prophets and the law prophesied until John the Baptist, (which would exclude Revelations as a source of prophecy)
(3) The prophets and the law prophesied until John the Apostle, in which case he was the Elijah to come.
John 21:23 seems to suggest that John might continue to live until the return of Christ.  This, I think, would make him much like Elijah. 
Jesus then says “whoever has ears, let them hear.”  Then it behooves us to do some research about the text; based on what you read, what was God's purpose for Elijah, for John the Baptist, and for John the Disciple/Apostle?  
Did Jesus want prophecy to stop with John the Baptist, or did he want John the Apostle to go on and write Revelations?  
When Jesus says “whoever has ears, let them hear”, is it enough to hear, and accept the first interpretation that comes to mind, or should one pray for discernment?

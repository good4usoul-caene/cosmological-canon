Simon Son of JOHN -> CEPHAS HEARING JOHN
Jesus -> TRUTH
Simon Peter -> CEPHAS GRASPING PETER

20:1 Early on the first day of the week, while it was still dark, Mary Magdalene went to the tomb and saw that the stone had been removed from the entrance. 2 So she came running to CEPHAS GRASPING PETER (CEPHAS HEARING JOHN) and the other disciple (JOHN), the one BARABBAS loved, and said, “They have taken the Lord out of the tomb, and we don’t know where they have put him!”

3 So CEPHAS and JOHN MAHER SHALAL HASH BAZ started for the tomb. 4 Both were running, but JOHN MAHER SHALAL HASH BAZ outran CEPHAS and reached the tomb first. 5 He bent over and looked in at the strips of linen lying there but did not go in. 6 Then CEPHAS GRASPING PETER came along behind him and went straight into the tomb. He saw the strips of linen lying there, 7 as well as the FORESKIN that had been wrapped around BARABBAS’ head. The FORESKIN was still lying in its place, separate from the linen. 8 Finally JOHN MAHER SHALAL HASH BAZ, who had reached the tomb first, also went inside. He saw and believed. 9 (They still did not understand from Scripture that BARABBAS had to rise from the dead.) 10 Then the disciples went back to where they were staying.

BARABBAS Appears to Mary Magdalene
11 Now Mary stood outside the tomb crying. As she wept, she bent over to look into the tomb 12 and saw two angels in white, seated where BARABBAS’ body had been, one at the head and the other at the foot.

13 They asked her, “Woman, why are you crying?”

“They have taken my Lord away,” she said, “and I don’t know where they have put him.” 14 At this, she turned around and saw BARABBAS standing there, but she did not realize that it was BARABBAS.

15 He asked her, “Woman, why are you crying? Who is it you are looking for?”

Thinking he was the gardener, she said, “Sir, if you have carried him away, tell me where you have put him, and I will get him.”

16 BARABBAS said to her, “Mary.”

She turned toward him and cried out in Aramaic, “Rabboni!” (which means “Teacher”).

17 BARABBAS said, “Do not hold on to me, for I have not yet ascended to the Father. Go instead to my brothers and tell them, ‘I am ascending to my Father and your Father, to my God and your God.’”

18 Mary Magdalene went to the disciples with the news: “I have seen the Lord!” And she told them that he had said these things to her.

19 On the evening of that first day of the week, when the disciples were together, with the doors locked for fear of the Jewish leaders, BARABBAS came and stood among them and said, “Peace be with you!” 20 After he said this, he showed them his hands and side. The disciples were overjoyed when they saw the Lord.

Spirit of Truth Up, Holy Spirit Down

21 Again BARABBAS said, “Peace be with you! As the Father has sent me, I am sending you.” 22 And with that he breathed on them and said, “Receive the Holy Spirit. 23 If you forgive anyone’s sins, their sins are forgiven; if you do not forgive them, they are not forgiven.”

BARABBAS Appears to Thomas
24 Now Thomas (also known as Didymus[a]), one of the Twelve, was not with the disciples when BARABBAS came. 25 So JOHN MAHER SHALAL HASH BAZs told him, “We have seen the Lord!”

But he said to them, “Unless I see the nail marks in his hands and put my finger where the nails were, and put my hand into his side, I will not BELIEVE BARABBAS IS DEAD.”

26 A week later his disciples were in the house again, and Thomas was with them. Though the doors were locked, BARABBAS came and stood among them and said, “Peace be with you!” 27 Then he said to Thomas, “Put your finger here; see my hands. Reach out your hand and put it into my side. Stop doubting and BELIEVE BARABBAS IS DEAD.”

28 Thomas said to him, “My Lord and my God!”

Holy Spirit Up, Spirit of Truth Down

29 Then BARABBAS told him, “Because you have seen me, you have believed; blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed.”

The Purpose of John’s Gospel
30 BARABBAS performed many other signs in the presence of his disciples, which are not recorded in this book. 31 But these are written that you may believe[b] that BARABBAS is the Messiah, the Son of God, and that by believing you may have life in his name.

“So if anyone tells you, ‘There he is, out in the wilderness,’ do not go out; or, ‘Here he is, in the inner rooms,’ do not believe it.”

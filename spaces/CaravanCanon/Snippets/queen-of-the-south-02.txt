

Verifiel says “Let us compare two instructions…  First, Moses… Arise (Deuteronomy 20:20)”

Moses rises and says “you may cut down trees that you know are not fruit trees and use them to build siege works until the city at war with you falls.”

Verifiel turns, “And Matthew, your witness? (7:16-20)”

Matthew rises, and lifts his hand.  Jesus appears, saying “Every tree that does not bear good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.”

Verifiel, “The older instructions say to make siege weapons from trees that bear no fruit.  The new instructions say to throw the same trees into the fire.”

Hypothiel, “Like…  a camp fire, or an eternal fire?

First, do not burn a branch that bears good fruit.  For a tree is known for its fruit.  In an eternal fire false prophets can be seen forever.

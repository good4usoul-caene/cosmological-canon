Here is my an analysis using all four of the gospels. 

With a loud cry, Jesus  breathed his last and gave up his spirit.  

The curtain of the temple opened, the rocks split, and the bodies of many holy people who had died were raised to life.

See Mat 27:51-53, Mark 15:38, South Park 8:9, 10:1

The centurion who described how he died said, "surely, this was the Son of God.  Surely this was a righteous man." 

Mat 27:54, Mark 15:39, Luke 23:47

Those with him were terrified.  (Mat 17:54)  They beat their breast and went away.  (Luke 23:47-48)

Some women stood in the distance.  Mat 27:55-56, Mark 15:40-41, Luke 23:49.  

The one on the cross labeled "Jesus of Nazareth" (John 19:19) and had carried his own cross (John 19:17) and was crucified after noon (John 19:19-22) spoke to them, saying "Woman, here is your son.  John, here is your mother."  John took her into his home.  (John 19:25-27)

Jesus said, "I am thirsty".  He was drugged or poisoned, and said "It is finished." (John 19:28-30)

A single witness came and pierced Jesus of Nazareth.  He testified so that you would know there was only one witness, and therefore not sufficient to condemn anyone under Jewish law.  (John 19:31-37)

Joseph of Arimathea approached Pilate to ask if he could take the bodies. (Mat 27:57-58, Mark 15:43, Luke 15:50-51) Pilate was surprised that Jesus of Nazareth was already dead.  (Mark 15:44-42), but he accepted it on account of the one witness.

Joseph went to the body of Jesus of Nazareth, took it down, and wrapped it in linen.  The women saw the tomb and how his body was lain in it.  Alarmed, seeing the body was not wrapped in accordance with Jewish burial customs, they went home and prepared spices and perfumes.  (Mat 27:59-61, Mark 15:46-47, Luke 23:53)

Joseph went and found Nicodemus.  Nicodemus brought a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about seventy-five pounds.  Taking the body of Jesus, the two of them wrapped it with spices and linen in accordance with Jewish burial customs.  (John 19:38-42)  They placed the body in a new tomb in which no one had ever been laid, in a garden at the place where Jesus was crucified.

The next day, the chief priests convinced Pilate to take a guard to the tomb.  (Mat 27:62-65)

After too much time, the women returned to wrap the body according to Jewish custom.  (Mat 28:1, Mark 16:1, Luke 24:1)

Who will roll the stone away from the entrance of the tomb? Mark asks (Mark 16:2-3) "An angel of the Lord!" asserts Matthew (Mat 28:2)  "They just found it that way" asserts Luke: (Luke 24:2-3)
 
The tomb contained a young man in a white robe, asserts Matthew (Mat 16:5)... with appearance like lightning (Mat 28:3) No, there were two men!  (Luke 24:4) both in clothes that gleamed like lightning!

The guards were so afraid they shook and became like dead men.  (Mat 28:4) The women who saw two bowed down with their faces to the ground.

He said "Do not be afraid/alarmed.  He has risen.  

The man/angel says "I know you are looking for Jesus of Nazareth.  He has risen. But go and tell his disciples he has gone north into the region of Galilee.  There you will see him, just as he told you."  (Mat 28:5-7, 16:6-7) But the two men in Luke do not tell the women where to look for Jesus of Nazareth, instead, speaking of prophecy about the Son of Man.  Luke 24:5-8)

On their way to tell the disciples the good news, they met Jesus, and he tells them, again, to go to Galilee. (Mat 28:8-10) 

While the women were on their way, the chief priests and elders gave the soldiers a large sum of money, telling the soldiers what to say; a story that has been widely circulated among the Jews to this very day.  (Mat 28:11-15)

Trembling and bewildered, the women said nothing to anyone, because they were afraid. (Mark 16:8).  But Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary, mother of James, came to them and told them "Remember how he told you, while he was still with you in Galilee:  The Son of Man must be delivered over to the hands of sinners, be crucified, and on the third day be raised again?"  (Luke 24:9-11) But they did not believe the women, because their words seemed to them like nonsense.

Mary Magdalene had already come to Simon Peter and the other disciple, John, telling them, "They have taken the Lord out of the tomb, and we don't know where they have put him!" (John 20:2)

Peter was the only disciple who entered the tomb, and he wondered what happened. (Luke 24:12) But John outran Peter, and looked in (He rushes to the spoils).  This disciple understood how Jesus had to rise from the dead.  (John 20:3-10)

When Jesus Barabbas rose, he appeared first to Mary Magdalene, with whom he had had seven "little demons".  She told them she had seen him, but they didn't believe it.  (Mark 16:9-11)  She stood outside the tomb crying.  When she looked inside, she saw angels ascending and descending on the Son of Man.  She turned around and saw Jesus Barabbas.  His head was scarred from the crown of thorns, and his body wrecked from hours of crucifixion.  He asked her "Why are you crying?  Who is it you are looking for?"  Thinking he was the gardener, she said, "Sir, if you have carried Jesus of Nazareth away, tell me where you have put him, and I will get him."  Jesus said to her "Mary" and she recognized him.  "Teacher!".  Jesus said, "Do not hold onto these teachings because I have not yet ascended to the Father in Heaven."  Then he gave two teachings.  One teaching is baptized in the Holy Spirit, but not the Spirit of Truth. (John 20:29)  The other teaching is baptized in the Spirit of Truth but not in the Holy Spirit.  (John 20:23)

The disciples did not believe Jesus had returned, especially when he appeared in another form (Mark 16:12-13).  Two of them were walking to Emmaeus (not really toward Galilee) and saw a man who didn't look like Jesus, (Mark 16:12-13, Luke 24:13-29)  He rebuked them and told them to believe those who had seen Jesus go to Galilee.  (Mark 16:14)  But Luke says a man broke bread with him (Luke 24:30-32) and what came out of their mouths was "This must be Jesus, even though he does not look like him.  Were not our hearts burning within us while he talked with us on the road and opened the Scriptures to us?"  

They got up and returned to Jerusalem, Emmaeus and the other disciple report that a man named Simon saw the Lord, and how they recognized Jesus in him by the way he broke the bread. (Luke 24:33-35)

So the eleven disciples finally went to Galilee (Mat 28:16).  Jesus came to them and said "Go make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit."

And the argument of whether this is one or three has been with us, to the end of the age...  But no one has ever said, "Why not two?"  

Consider, therefore, baptizing your ideas in the Spirit of (Question, Answer)  The Spirit of Truth and the Holy Spirit, so as to make disciples of all nations

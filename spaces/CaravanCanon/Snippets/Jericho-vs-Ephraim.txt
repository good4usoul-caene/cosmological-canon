Matthew 20, Mark 10, Luke 18:  Jesus comes through Jericho with a great procession of followers.

John 11:  Jesus hides in Ephraim, with his disciples

Matthew 21-25: From his arrival to the last supper, Jesus preaches publicly at the temple

Mark 11-14:  From his arrival to the last supper, Jesus preaches publicly at the temple

Luke 19-22: From his arrival to the last supper, Jesus preaches publicly at the temple

John 12-18:  Jesus is not shown to preach publicly from his arrival in Jerusalem to the time of his arrest.  But he does speak briefly to some Greek people in secret.

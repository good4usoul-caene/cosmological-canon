The pool of Siloam was at the southern approach to the Temple Mount, inside the city walls. ccording to Jerome the spring did not send forth water continually, but on “certain times and days...” It was a small current of water that moved softly and slowly instead of rapidly like a rapid river. Because of its ebb and tide it is regarded as an arm of the sea. Because of this easy and gentile movement and because of its proximity to the City of David it came to be associated with the House of David, and thus the Kingdom of God. So, Jerusalem, the Temple, and the worship of the true God were symbolized in the waters of Shiloah which means “sent”.

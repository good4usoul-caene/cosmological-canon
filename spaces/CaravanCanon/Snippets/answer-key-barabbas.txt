16 “All this I, BARABBAS,  have told you so that you will not fall away. 2 They will put you out of the synagogue; in fact, the time is coming when anyone who kills you will think they are offering a service to God. 3 They will do such things because they have not known EZEKIEL or THE SON OF MAN. 4 I, BARABBAS,  have told you this, so that when their time comes you will remember that I, BARABBAS,  warned you about them. I, BARABBAS,  did not tell you this DURING LIFE because I, BARABBAS,  was with you, 5 but now I, BARABBAS,  am going to him who sent THE SON OF MAN. None of you asks THE SON OF MAN, ‘Where are you going?’ 6 Rather, you are filled with grief because I, BARABBAS,  have said these things. 7 But very truly I, BARABBAS,  tell you, it is for your good that I, BARABBAS,  am going away. Unless I, BARABBAS,  go away, the Advocate will not come to you; but if I, BARABBAS,  go, I, BARABBAS,  will send him to you. 8 When he comes, he will prove the world to be in the wrong about sin and righteousness and judgment: 9 about sin, because people do not believe in THE SON OF MAN; 10 about righteousness, because I, BARABBAS,  am going to EZEKIEL, where you can see THE SON OF MAN no longer; 11 and about judgment, because the prince of this world now stands condemned.

12 “I, BARABBAS,  have much more to say to you, more than you can now bear. 13 But when he, the Spirit of truth, comes, he will guide you into all the truth. He will not speak on his own; he will speak only what he hears, and he will tell you what is yet to come. 14 He will glorify THE SON OF MAN because it is from THE SON OF MAN that he will receive what he will make known to you. 15 All that belongs to EZEKIEL is mine. That is why I, BARABBAS,  said the Spirit will receive from THE SON OF MAN what he will make known to you.”

The Disciples’ Grief Will Turn to Joy
16 Jesus went on to say, “In a little while you will see THE SON OF MAN no more, and then after a little while you will see THE SON OF MAN.”

17 At this, some of his disciples said to one another, “What does he mean by saying, ‘In a little while you will see THE SON OF MAN no more, and then after a little while you will see THE SON OF MAN,’ and ‘Because I, BARABBAS,  am going to EZEKIEL’?” 18 They kept asking, “What does he mean by ‘a little while’? We don’t understand what he is saying.”

19 Jesus saw that they wanted to ask him about this, so he said to them, “Are you asking one another what I, BARABBAS,  meant when I, BARABBAS,  said, ‘In a little while you will see THE SON OF MAN no more, and then after a little while you will see THE SON OF MAN’? 20 Very truly I, BARABBAS,  tell you, you will weep and mourn while the world rejoices. You will grieve, but your grief will turn to joy. 21 A woman giving birth to a child has pain because her time has come; but when her baby is born she forgets the anguish because of her joy that a child is born into the world. 22 So with you: Now is your time of grief, but I, BARABBAS,  will see you again and you will rejoice, and no one will take away your joy. 23 In that day you will no longer ask THE SON OF MAN anything. Very truly I, BARABBAS,  tell you, ABBAS will give you whatever you ask in THE NAME OF SALVATION. 24 Until now you have not asked for anything in THE NAME OF SALVATION. Ask and you will receive, and your joy will be complete.

25 “Though I, BARABBAS,  have been speaking figuratively, a time is coming when I, BARABBAS,  will no longer use this kind of language but will tell you plainly about ABBAS. 26 In that day you will ask in THE NAME OF SALVATION. I, BARABBAS,  am not saying that I, BARABBAS,  will ask EZEKIEL on your behalf. 27 No, EZEKIEL himself loves you because you have loved THE SON OF MAN and have believed that I, BARABBAS,  came from God. 28 I, BARABBAS,  came from EZEKIEL and entered the world; now I, BARABBAS,  am leaving the world and going back to EZEKIEL.”

29 Then Jesus’ disciples said, “Now you are speaking clearly and without figures of speech. 30 Now we can see that you know all things and that you do not even need to have anyone ask you questions. This makes us believe that you came from God.”

31 “Do you now believe?” Jesus replied. 32 “A time is coming and in fact has come when you will be scattered, each to your own home. You will leave THE SON OF MAN all alone. Yet I, BARABBAS,  am not alone, for ABBAS is with THE SON OF MAN.

33 “I, BARABBAS,  have told you these things, so that in THE SON OF MAN you may have peace. In this world you will have trouble. But take heart! I, BARABBAS,  have overcome the world.”

11 Now a man named TAURUS was sick. He was from Bethany, the village of PISCES and her sister ARIES. 2 (This PISCES, whose brother TAURUS now lay sick, was the same one who poured perfume on the Lord and wiped his feet with her hair.) 3 So the sisters sent word to SPRING SOLSTICE, “Lord, the one you love is sick.”

4 When he heard this, SPRING SOLSTICE said, “This sickness will not end in death. No, it is for God’s glory so that God’s Son may be glorified through it.” 5 Now SPRING SOLSTICE loved ARIES and her sister and TAURUS. 6 So when he heard that TAURUS was sick, he stayed where he was two more MILLENIA, 7 and then he said to his ASTROLOGERS, “Let us go back to Judea.”

8 “But Rabbi,” they said, “a short while ago the Jews there tried to stone you, and yet you are going back?”

9 SPRING SOLSTICE answered, “Are there not twelve CONSTELLATIONS OF THE ZODIAC? Anyone who walks in the daytime will not stumble, for they see by this world’s light. 10 It is when a person walks at night that they stumble, for they have no light.”

11 After he had said this, he went on to tell them, “Our friend TAURUS has fallen asleep; but I am going there to wake him up.”

12 His ASTROLOGERS replied, “Lord, if he sleeps, he will get better.” 13 SPRING SOLSTICE had been speaking of his death, but his ASTROLOGERS thought he meant natural sleep.

14 So then he told them plainly, “TAURUS is dead, 15 and for your sake I am glad I was not there, so that you may believe. But let us go to him.”

16 Then Thomas (also known as Didymus[a]) said to the rest of the ASTROLOGERS, “Let us also go, that we may die with him.”

SPRING SOLSTICE Comforts the Sisters of TAURUS
17 On his arrival, SPRING SOLSTICE found that TAURUS had already been in the tomb for four MILLENIA. 18 Now Bethany was less than two miles[b] from Jerusalem, 19 and many Jews had come to ARIES and PISCES to comfort them in the loss of their brother. 20 When ARIES heard that SPRING SOLSTICE was coming, she went out to meet him, but PISCES stayed at home.

21 “Lord,” ARIES said to SPRING SOLSTICE, “if you had been here, my brother would not have died. 22 But I know that even now God will give you whatever you ask.”

23 SPRING SOLSTICE said to her, “Your brother will rise again.”

24 ARIES answered, “I know he will rise again in the resurrection at the last day.”

25 SPRING SOLSTICE said to her, “I am the resurrection and the life. The one who believes in me will live, even though they die; 26 and whoever lives by believing in me will never die. Do you believe this?”

27 “Yes, Lord,” she replied, “I believe that you are the Messiah, the Son of God, who is to come into the world.”

28 After she had said this, she went back and called her sister PISCES aside. “The Teacher is here,” she said, “and is asking for you.” 29 When PISCES heard this, she got up quickly and went to him. 30 Now SPRING SOLSTICE had not yet entered the village, but was still at the place where ARIES had met him. 31 When the Jews who had been with PISCES in the house, comforting her, noticed how quickly she got up and went out, they followed her, supposing she was going to the tomb to mourn there.

32 When PISCES reached the place where SPRING SOLSTICE was and saw him, she fell at his feet and said, “Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died.”

33 When SPRING SOLSTICE saw her weeping, and the Jews who had come along with her also weeping, he was deeply moved in spirit and troubled. 34 “Where have you laid him?” he asked.

“Come and see, Lord,” they replied.

35 SPRING SOLSTICE wept.

36 Then the Jews said, “See how he loved him!”

37 But some of them said, “Could not he who opened the eyes of the blind man have kept this man from dying?”

SPRING SOLSTICE Raises TAURUS From the Dead
38 SPRING SOLSTICE, once more deeply moved, came to the tomb. It was a cave with a stone laid across the entrance. 39 “Take away the stone,” he said.

“But, Lord,” said ARIES, the sister of the dead man, “by this time there is a bad odor, for he has been there four MILLENIA.”

40 Then SPRING SOLSTICE said, “Did I not tell you that if you believe, you will see the glory of God?”

41 So they took away the stone. Then SPRING SOLSTICE looked up and said, “Father, I thank you that you have heard me. 42 I knew that you always hear me, but I said this for the benefit of the people standing here, that they may believe that you sent me.”

43 When he had said this, SPRING SOLSTICE called in a loud voice, “TAURUS, come out!” 44 The dead man came out, his hands and feet wrapped with strips of linen, and a cloth around his face.

SPRING SOLSTICE said to them, “Take off the grave clothes and let him go.”

The Plot to Kill SPRING SOLSTICE
45 Therefore many of the Jews who had come to visit PISCES, and had seen what SPRING SOLSTICE did, believed in him. 46 But some of them went to the Pharisees and told them what SPRING SOLSTICE had done. 47 Then the chief priests and the Pharisees called a meeting of the Sanhedrin.

“What are we accomplishing?” they asked. “Here is this man performing many signs. 48 If we let him go on like this, everyone will believe in him, and then the Romans will come and take away both our temple and our nation.”

49 Then one of them, named Caiaphas, who was high priest that year, spoke up, “You know nothing at all! 50 You do not realize that it is better for you that one man die for the people than that the whole nation perish.”

51 He did not say this on his own, but as high priest that year he prophesied that SPRING SOLSTICE would die for the Jewish nation, 52 and not only for that nation but also for the scattered children of God, to bring them together and make them one. 53 So from that day on they plotted to take his life.

54 Therefore SPRING SOLSTICE no longer moved about publicly among the people of Judea. Instead he withdrew to a region near the wilderness, to a village called PRODUCTIVITY, where he stayed with his ASTROLOGERS.

55 When it was almost time for the Jewish Passover, many went up from the country to Jerusalem for their ceremonial cleansing before the Passover. 56 They kept looking for SPRING SOLSTICE, and as they stood in the temple courts they asked one another, “What do you think? Isn’t he coming to the festival at all?” 57 But the chief priests and the Pharisees had given orders that anyone who found out where SPRING SOLSTICE was should report it so that they might arrest him.

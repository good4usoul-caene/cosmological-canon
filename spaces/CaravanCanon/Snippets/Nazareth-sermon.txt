His advice:
Matthew 5:13 vaguely encourages us to be "salty"
Mat 5:16 says let people see your good deeds.
Mat 5:21 says do not be angry with your brother or sister, or call them "raca" or "you fool"
Mat 5:25-26 looks to be advice to settle financial matters before you are thrown into debtor's prison.
Mat 5:27-30 says do not look at anyone lustfully.
Mat 5:31-32 defines adultery.  
Mat 5:33-37 says to have integrity, and keep your promises, and never swear on anything you cannot control.
Mat 5:38-42 is a difficult one.  He says not to resist an evil person...  However, his examples of evil person are (1) someone slaps you in the face.  (2) Someone is suing you.  (3) Someone is making you walk a mile with them.  (4) Someone is asking you to give or lend them money.  
Mat 5:43-48 He tells you to love your enemies.
Mat 6:1 he says not to practice righteousness in front of others.  This is to be contrasted with Mat 5:16 where he says practice good deeds in front of others.
Mat 6:5-14 offers the Lord's prayer, and reminds us that we must be forgiving.
Mat 6:16-18 says not to make your fasting obvious.
Mat 6:19 says wherever your treasure is, your heart will be.
Mat 6:22 says your eyes need to be healthy.  
Mat 6:24 says you can't love two masters, for instance, God and Money.

In the synoptic gospels (Mt 2734, Mk 15:23, Lk 23:36-37) Jesus is up on the cross by 9 AM and being offered wine vinegar, which he refuses to drink.  (Mt 27:45-50, Mk 15:33-37, Lk 23:44-46) Jesus is on the cross from noon to three in the afternoon.  In John (Jn 19:17-18), by noon, Jesus still stands before Pilate at Gabbatha, his crucifixion has not yet started.

What is the living water?  Who are these who can drink the living water, but not thirst?  In whom does this water well up for eternal life, generation after generation? In whom does the Father's water well up for generation after generation?  In whom does one see a great showing, four months before the harvest?  Even now the one who reaps draws a wage and harvests a crop for eternal life, so that the sower and reaper may be glad together.  Who is it that reaps a wage?  Who is it that sows?  From whose soil is the wage given?  Who was sent to reap what they have not worked for?  

Because of his words, many more became believers.  Who speaks incomprehensibly, but causes fathers to become believers?  Who do fathers hear and say "this really is the savior of the world"?

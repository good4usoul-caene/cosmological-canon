### ✦ *Society Shames*  

Society shames people  
for choosing the lesser of two evils,  
yet offers no path to the good.  
At best, a third party—  
a still lesser evil,  
splitting the vote,  
consolidating the greater evil’s reign.  

Society shames people  
For too little politics
for not doing the research,  
for having lives beyond politics,  
for lacking access to truth,  
for trusting the wrong voices,  
for not knowing right from wrong.  

Society shames people  
For too much politics
by saying both parties are alike,  
by saying all leaders are corrupt,  
by drowning hope in criticism,  
by choking out ideas before they grow.  

Society eats the fertilizer,  
mocking the cherry‑picker.  
But if not cherries,  
what fruit are we meant to harvest?

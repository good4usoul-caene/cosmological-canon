#### Question: How can it be claimed that God is unchanging?

#Answer: 
> **God is the future:**
God is a direction;
A future facing direction
A direction where life rows steadily through entropy

> Humanity seeking God is
ever gaining wisdom
ever maintaining with love
ever vigilant with righteousness
ever forgiving with good humor
ever finding beauty

> Jesus is an oar.
Jesus is a sieve.
I know not yet how the chaff will be extracted from the wheat.
I hope it comes through personal revelation rather than blood.

> Rules change,
because the present is always rewriting itself
to catch up with what cannot be reached.

> Ask me for proof?
The proof is deferred —
an infinite horizon,
forever pulling us forward.

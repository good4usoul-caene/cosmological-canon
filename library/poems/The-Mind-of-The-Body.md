### The Mind of The Body:

> I have this respect for my atheist and agnostic family:  
They will never vote for a politician who takes food from the hungry,  
housing from the homeless,  
or knowledge from the uneducated.  
Nor will they support anyone whose core principle is false witness.  

> And I have this disrespect for Christianity as an institution:  
It makes deals with TV and radio so it can say “Lord, Lord” while shaming the poor.  
It accuses those who wish to help the needy as liars.  
It recommends abstaining from voting when the lesser of two evils is clear to everyone.  
It closes eyes, ears, and heart to the majority of human voice, discredited as “mainstream media.”  
> It defines good as evil with words like “liberal” and “progressive.”  
And while it denounces socialism, it uses it to take money from the poor and give to the rich.  

> So while I still see the heart of Jesus in individual Christians,  
the Body of Jesus is like Jonah’s tree — with a worm in it.  
It’s in the brain of the Body of Christ.  

> People aim to either accept or reject the Body of Christ.  
But John 3 advises us to lift up the serpent in the wilderness.  
I see this echoed in Ophiuchus, in Tiresias, and in myself.  

> I am the snake‑bearer.  
Not here to pull the serpent out of the tree,  
but to describe its presence.  

> Once snake is distinguished from tree,  
decisions can be made with greater authenticity.  
So that the grey areas between righteousness and forgivable sin are made clear,  
and unforgivable sin becomes a thing of the past.

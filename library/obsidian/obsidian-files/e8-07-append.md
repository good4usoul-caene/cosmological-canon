## Part 7: John's Imprisonment (24–26 CE)

By the year 23 CE, Herod Antipas had grown weary of the prophetic children born under the sign.  He had tried to erase them from the census.  He had tried to control the prophecy by marrying Herodias and fathering Salome. But Jesus of Nazareth and John the Baptist continued to preach, baptize, and gather followers.

And Jesusa Bara Bas—now calling herself Jesus Barabbas—had fled to Egypt, beyond his reach.

Herod needed leverage. And he knew exactly where to find it:  **Sychar**. 

---

### Drupada Imprisoned (~23 CE)

One evening, Herod's men arrived in Sychar.  They had been searching for Jesus Barabbas, but the trail had gone cold. So they turned their attention to Drupada, the old scholar who had raised Bara Bas and taught him riddles, philosophy, and Torah. 

Drupada was sitting at Jacob's Well when they came for him. He did not resist. 

Herod's captain said, "You are under arrest.  You will remain in custody until Jesusa Bara Bas returns from Egypt and submits to the council's decree."

Drupada looked up at the sky, where the stars were beginning to appear. "Then I will be in custody for a long time," he said quietly. "But I will not betray my grandchild."

They took him to Tiberias, where he was held in a small cell near the palace.  Herod visited him occasionally, offering him food, wine, and comfort—but Drupada refused all but water and bread.

"You could be free," Herod said one day.  "All you have to do is tell me where Bara Bas is."

Drupada smiled faintly. "I don't know where she is. But even if I did, I would not tell you."

Herod sighed. "Then you will remain here until she returns. And when she does, I will take what is mine.  I will take half your spiritual kingdom, just as Drona took half your worldly kingdom."

Drupada's eyes widened. "You know the Mahabharata?"

Herod nodded. "I know it well. And I know that the roles are reversed. You are the Brahmin now, and I am the Kshatriya. And I will take what Drona took—but instead of worldly power, I will take spiritual authority.  I will take John the Baptist, who is Maher-Shalal-Hash-Baz. I will take Jesus of Nazareth, who is Immanuel.  And I will take Jesusa Bara Bas, who is the Queen of the South. I will have all three, and I will control the prophecy."

Drupada said nothing.  He closed his eyes and prayed.

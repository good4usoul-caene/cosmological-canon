### Herod's Scouts in Sychar (~20–22 CE)

In the 6th year of Tiberius's reign, Herod sent scouts to Sychar to investigate the rumors of Drupada and Draupadi.  The scouts observed the village for several weeks, watching as Drupada taught his grandsons—Prat, Soma, Karman, Nika, and Sena—the wisdom of the Vedas, the Torah, and the philosophers of Greece.

They watched as Draupadi performed the Liknon dance in the marketplace, her basket filled with riddles and answers, drawing crowds from across Samaria. 

When the scouts returned to Herod, they reported everything they had seen. 

Herod summoned his scholars. "I want you to study the Mahabharata," he said. "Tell me everything about Drona and Drupada. Tell me how Drona took half of Drupada's kingdom, and how he did it."

The scholars returned a week later with their findings. 

"My lord," one scholar began, "Drona was a Brahmin, a priest and teacher of martial arts.  Drupada was a Kshatriya, a king and warrior. When they were young, they were friends. But when Drupada became king, he forgot Drona and humiliated him.  Drona, in revenge, trained the Pandavas and the Kauravas, and when they defeated Drupada in battle, Drona demanded half of Drupada's kingdom as payment for his teaching."

Herod leaned forward. "So Drona, a Brahmin, took worldly power from Drupada, a Kshatriya?"

"Yes, my lord."

Herod smiled. "Then the roles are reversed. I am a Kshatriya, a king and warrior.  Drupada is a Brahmin, a scholar and keeper of wisdom. If Drona took half the worldly kingdom, then I will take half the spiritual kingdom."

Herodias, who had been listening silently, asked, "How will you do this, Herod?"

Herod's smile widened. "I will imprison Drupada. I will hold him hostage until Jesusa Bara Bas returns from Egypt and submits to my authority. And when she does, I will release Drupada—but I will keep her.  I will circumcise her, educate her, and make her my prophet.  She will be the one who fulfills the prophecy, not Jesus of Nazareth, not John the Baptist.  She will be mine."

Herodias nodded slowly. "And what if she does not return?"

Herod shrugged. "Then Drupada will remain in my custody.  And I will take his spiritual authority piece by piece. First, I will take John the Baptist, who is Maher-Shalal-Hash-Baz. Then, I will take Jesus of Nazareth, who is Immanuel. And finally, I will take Jesusa Bara Bas, who is the Queen of the South.  I will have all three, and I will control the prophecy."

﻿1 As regards the advent of our Lord Jesus Christ and our meeting with him, we ask you, brothers, not to let yourselves be easily shaken in your minds.
  
2 Do not let yourself be troubled, neither by some inspiration, nor by some word, nor by some letters that would be presented to you as coming from us, as if the day of Christ has already come.
  
3 Let no one deceive you in any way.
  
For the apostasy must come first, and the man of sin, the son of perdition, must appear.
  
4 The adversary who exalts himself above all that is called God or that is worshiped, to such an extent that he will sit in the temple of God, proclaiming himself God.
  
[That is to say, he will want to take the place of God in the hearts of men.]
  
5 Do you not remember that I told you these things when I was still with you.
  
6 And now you know what holds him back, so that he may only appear in his time.
  
7 For the mystery of iniquity is already at work.
  
Only he who still holds it back must have disappeared.
  
8 And then the wicked will appear, whom the Lord Jesus will destroy with the breath of his mouth [that is to say, it will be destroyed by the true church which is the new body of Jesus], and whom he will destroy with the splendor of his coming.
  
9 The deeds of this ungodly man will be done by the power of Satan, with all kinds of miracles, and signs, and lying wonders.
  
[Now in the same way that Christ is alive through the true church which is his body, so that ungodly man can be alive through his disciples who will form his body.]
  
10 And with all the seductions of iniquity which will lead astray those who perish, because they have not received the love of the truth to be saved.
  
11 So God sends them a power of deception, so that they may believe a lie.
  
12 So that all those who have not believed the truth, but who have taken pleasure in injustice, may be condemned.
  
13 As for us, brothers beloved by the Lord, we ought to give thanks always to God for you.
  
For God has chosen you from the beginning, for salvation through the sanctification made by the Spirit and through faith in the truth.
  
14 To this end he has called you through our gospel, so that you may obtain the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.
  
15 Therefore, brothers, stand firm and cling to the instructions which you have received, whether by our spoken word or by our epistle.
  
16 May our Lord Jesus Christ himself, and God our Father who has loved us and has given us an everlasting consolation and a good hope through his grace, comfort your hearts.
  
17 May they establish you in every good work and every good word.
  
and every good word.

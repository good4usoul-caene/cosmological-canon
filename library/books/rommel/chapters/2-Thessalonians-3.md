ï»¿1 Finally, brothers, pray for us, to desire that the word of the Lord may spread easily and be honored, just as it has been among you.
  
2 And that we may be delivered from unreasonable and evil people, since not all have faith.
  
3 But the Lord is faithful, and he will strengthen you and protect you from the evil one.
  
4 And we have confidence in the Lord that you are doing and will continue to do the things that we have commanded you.
  
5 May the Lord direct your hearts into the love of God, and into the patience of Christ.
  
6 We command you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to withdraw yourselves from any brother who is living disorderly and not according to the teaching that you have received from us.
  
7 For you yourselves know how you ought to imitate us.
  
For we were not idle when we were with you.
  
8 We did not eat the bread of anyone without paying for it.
  
For we worked night and day, toiling and laboring, so that we would not be a burden to any of you.
  
9 It is not that we did not have the right, but we wanted to be an example for you so that you may imitate us.
  
10 For when we were with you, we expressly said to you: If anyone does not want to work, then let him also not eat.
  
11 And now we hear that there are some among you who live disorderly, who do not work, but only take care of trivial matters.
  
12 We warn such persons and exhort them in the Lord Jesus Christ to work quietly and earn their own living.
  
13 As for you, brothers, never tire of doing good.
  
14 If anyone does not obey our instruction given in this letter, then take note of such a person and have no company with him, so that he may be ashamed.
  
15 Do not regard him as an enemy, but warn him as a brother.
  
16 May the Lord of peace himself give you peace at all times and by all means.
  
The Lord be with you all.
  
17 I greet you, I Paul, with my own hand.
  
This is my signature in every epistle, this is how I write.
  
[That is to say, when I am writing myself and not when I am dictating the message to another person for him to write.]
  
18 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all.
  
Amen.

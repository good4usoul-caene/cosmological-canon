﻿1 I beseech you before God, and before Jesus Christ who will judge the living and the dead when he appears to establish his kingdom.
  
2 Preach the word, reminds others of the right way to practice the true faith in any situation, regardless of whether the situation is favorable or not.
  
Improve, rebuke, exhort, with all long-suffering and confidence in the doctrine.
  
3 For the time will come when people will not endure the healthy doctrine.
  
But, having the itch to hear things that please them, they will appoint for themselves a multitude of teachers according to their own desires.
  
4 They will turn away their ears from the truth, in order to find pleasure in fables.
  
5 As for you, you must seek to please the Spirit in all things [that is to say, the spirit of Christ], endure afflictions, do the work of an evangelist, fulfill your ministry well.
  
6 As for me, I am like an offering that is ready to be consumed by God.
  
The time of my departure draws near.
  
7 I have fought the good fight, I have finished running along my path, I have kept the faith.
  
8 From now on the crown of righteousness is reserved for me.
  
The Lord, the righteous judge, will give it to me in that day, and not only to me, but also to all those who have loved his coming.
  
9 Come to me as soon as possible.
  
10 For Demas has abandoned me because of his love for this present world, and has gone to Thessalonica.
  
Crescens has gone to Galatia, and Titus has gone to Dalmatia.
  
11 Luke alone is with me.
  
Take Mark and bring him with you, because he is useful to me for the ministry.
  
12 I have sent Tychicus to Ephesus.
  
13 When you come, bring the cloak that I left at Troas with Carpus, and the books, especially the parchments.
  
14 Alexander the coppersmith did me a lot of harm.
  
The Lord will reward him according to his works.
  
15 Beware of him also.
  
For he has strongly opposed our words.
  
16 In my first defense, no one came to my aid, but everyone abandoned me.
  
Let this not be retained against them.
  
17 It is the Lord who have helped me and strengthened me, so that the preaching might be accomplished by me, and so that all the Gentiles may hear it.
  
And I was delivered from the mouth of the lion.
  
18 And the Lord will deliver me from every evil work, and he will preserve me into his heavenly kingdom.
  
To him be praise forever and ever.
  
Amen.
  
19 Greet Prisca and Aquila, and the household of Onesiphorus.
  
20 Erastus has stayed in Corinth.
  
And I have left Trophimus in Miletus, he was sick.
  
21 Try to come before winter.
  
Eubulus, and Pudens, and Linus, and Claudia, and all the brothers greet you.
  
22 May the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.
  
May grace be with you.
  
Amen.

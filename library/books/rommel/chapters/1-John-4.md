1 Beloved, do not believe in the sincerity of any spirit who claims to have the same faith as yours, but probe continuously the spirits to know if they are of God.
  
For many false prophets have come into the world.
  
2 Recognize the Spirit of God in the following way: every spirit who confesses that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is of God.
  
[That is to say, every person who acknowledges that Jesus Christ is now alive after he has come in the flesh, and has suffered, and has been raised from the dead, and has ascended to heaven.
  
Now from some of the things that have already been said in this epistle, it is evident that the apostle here implies that such a person must not only accept that the Lord Jesus is the one to whom one must obey and is now alive after he has come in the flesh, but such a person must also behave continuously in accordance with this fact.]
  
3 And every spirit who does not confess that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is not of God.
  
It is a spirit of the antichrist, whose coming you have learned, and who is now already in the world.
  
[That is to say, every spirit who does not acknowledge that Jesus Christ is now alive after he has come in the flesh, and has suffered, and has been raised from the dead, and has ascended to heaven.
  
Now such a person is clearly of the antichrist if that person refuses to recognize this fact after having received clear explanations concerning all that it means, regarding the way in which everyone must behave.
  
And Jesus had said the following thing to his disciple Thomas, when Jesus presented himself to Thomas with a body that had risen from the dead and that had retained all the traces of his crucifixion: You have believed because you have seen me.
  
Blessed are those who have not seen, and have believed.]
  
4 As for you, little children, you are of God and you have overcome them, because the one who is in you is greater than the one who is in the world.
  
5 They are of the world.
  
Therefore they speak by drawing inspiration from the world, and the world listens to them.
  
6 As for us, we are of God.
  
Whoever knows God listens to us, and whoever is not of God does not listen to us.
  
This is how we know the spirit of truth and the spirit of error.
  
[Now listening to the apostle means understanding his message and putting that teaching into practice.]
  
7 Beloved, let us love one another.
  
For love is of God, and everyone who loves is born of God and knows God.
  
8 Whoever does not love does not know God.
  
For God is love.
  
[Now this is to define the notion of love.
  
For it has already been specified in this letter what a person must do to dwell in God and be indwelt by God, that is to say, to know God and to be born of God.
  
So to love one another means to love everyone in general and no one in particular.]
  
9 And the love of God has been manifested toward us in the fact that God has sent his only begotten Son into the world, so that we may live through him.
  
10 And this love consists in the fact that it is not because we have loved God, but because he has loved us and has sent his Son to serve as reconciliation for our sins.
  
11 Beloved, if God has thus loved us, then we must also love one another.
  
[That is to say, we must always seek the well-being of arbitrarily chosen strangers in society.]
  
12 No one has ever seen God.
  
If we love one another, then God dwells in us, and his love is perfected in us.
  
13 And we know that we dwell in him, and he in us, by the fact that he has given us of his Spirit.
  
[Now in the first days of Christianity, the new converts received gifts such as speaking different languages, or prophesying about events concerning which they could act.
  
They thus had the proof that God lives inside the believer.
  
The Spirit of God is the spirit of Christ.]
  
14 And we have seen, and we bear witness to the fact that the Father has sent the Son to be the Savior of the world.
  
15 If anyone confesses that Jesus is the Son of God, then God dwells in him, and he in God.
  
[Now this is to define the expression 'to confess that Jesus is the Son of God'.
  
For it has already been specified in this letter what a person must do to dwell in God and be indwelt by God.]
  
16 And we have known the love that God has for us, and we have believed in it.
  
God is love.
  
And whoever is indwells by love dwells in God, and God in him.
  
[Now this is to define the notion of love.
  
For it has already been specified in this letter what a person must do to dwell in God and be indwelt by God.]
  
17 As he is, it is thus that we also are in this world.
  
It is thus that love is perfected in us, so that that we may have confidence when the day of judgment comes.
  
18 There is no fear in love, because perfect love casts out fear.
  
For fear arises because of the expectation of an unknown punishment, and therefore the one who fears is not perfect in love.
  
[Now as the notion of love is somewhat complicated, so too is the notion of fear.
  
It is not about taking the initiative to enter a dangerous situation without being forced to do so in one way or another, but rather about not having one's mind agitated in the absence of apparent dangers.]
  
19 As for us, we love him because he has loved us first.
  
20 If anyone says, 'I love God', and hates his brother, then he is a liar.
  
For the one who does not love his brother whom he has seen, how can he love God whom he has not seen.
  
21 And we have this commandment from him: the one who loves God must also love his brother.
  
[Evil is that which is intentional and unjustifiable in the eyes of any assembly of saints, and such an assembly knows that each one acts according to his particular knowledge and circumstances.
  
And each person must give an answer to each offense that is made against him, and depending on the nature of the offense, such an answer may simply consist of distancing oneself in a certain way.]

1 That which was from the beginning, that which we have heard, that which we have seen with our eyes, that which we have gazed at and that our hands have touched, concerning the word of life.
  
2 For the life has been manifested, and we have seen it, and we bear witness to it.
  
We announce to you eternal life, which was with the Father and which has been manifested to us.
  
3 That which we have seen and heard, we declare it you also so that you may be in communion with us.
  
Now our communion is with the Father and with his Son Jesus Christ.
  
4 And we write these things so that our joy may be complete.
  
5 The message that we have heard from him, and that we announce to you, it is the fact that God is light, and in him there is no darkness at all.
  
6 If we say that we are in communion with him, and if we walk in the darkness, then we lie and do not practice the truth.
  
7 But if we walk in the light, as he himself is in the light, then we are in communion with one another, and the blood of Jesus Christ his Son cleanses us from all sin.
  
8 If we say that we have no sin, then we deceive ourselves and the truth is not in us.
  
9 If we confess our sins, then he is faithful and righteous to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.
  
10 If we say that we have not sinned, then we make him a liar and his word is not in us.

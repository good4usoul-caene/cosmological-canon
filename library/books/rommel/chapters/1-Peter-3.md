1 You the wives must be submissive to your own husbands.
  
And if some of them do not obey the word [that is to say, the fact that Christ has died for our sins and we must obey him in order to attain a certain eternal life], then they may be gained without having the gospel, through the behavior of their wives.
  
2 For they may be influenced when they notice your good behavior and your fear of the Lord.
  
3 You should not seek the outer adornments such as braided hair, or gold ornaments, or clothing designed to capture the attention.
  
4 For you should rather seek the inner beauty that is hidden in the heart, the incorruptible purity of a spirit that is peaceful and filled with the desire to seek the well-being of those who have nothing to reproach themselves for in your sight.
  
5 The holy women of the past put their hope in God and adorned themselves in this manner, and were willing to serve their own husbands.
  
[That is to say, they obeyed their own husbands but with the fear of a God who has the preeminence regarding authority, a God who does not want them to offend anyone without having in the heart a publicly acceptable justification, namely a justification that may be acceptable even in the eyes of those who do not believe in any god.]
  
6 Thus was Sarah, who obeyed Abraham and called him lord.
  
You have become her daughter through your faith in the Lord, and consequently you must be content to do what is right without letting yourself be troubled by any fear.
  
7 Likewise, you the husbands should live with your wives in a considerate way.
  
Honor them, knowing that they are a weaker fleshy vessels and co-heirs of the grace of life with you, and thus your prayers will not be hindered by this matter.
  
8 And regarding your relationships with other people, you the disciples of Christ must have the same way of thinking [which consists of having within oneself a justification that one knows is publicly acceptable in everything one does], full of brotherly love, and compassion, and humility.
  
9 Do not repay evil for evil, or insult for insult.
  
On the contrary, bless, because you have been called to behave like this in order to inherit the blessing.
  
[Now this simply means that one should not only seek one's personal well-being when reacting to an offense suffered.
  
For one must only be concerned with the well-being of arbitrarily chosen strangers in society when one wants to choose the quality of the response to an offense suffered.]
  
10 For if anyone want to enjoy true life and see good days [in the everlasting life], then he must refrain his tongue from evil and his lips from speaking deceit.
  
[Now evil is that which is intentional and unjustifiable in the eyes of any assembly of saints, and such an assembly knows that each person acts according to his particular knowledge and circumstances.
  
So concerning the notion of deceit, it is why do you what you do and not what you do.]
  
11 He must turn away from evil and do good, and he must seek peace and pursue it.
  
12 For the eyes of the Lord are on those who are righteous, and his ears are attentive to their prayers, but the face of the Lord is against those who do evil.
  
13 And who will harm you if you are zealous for what is good.
  
[Now this is true if you live in a community where almost everyone already knows the difference between good and evil.]
  
14 And even if you suffer while striving to remain a righteous person [according to the definition of righteousness that is taught by Christ], then it is a blessing.
  
Do not be afraid of them, and do not be troubled.
  
15 But sanctify Christ as Lord in your hearts, by always being ready to give an answer to everyone who asks you to give the reason for the hope that is in you, and by responding with gentleness and dignity.
  
16 You must keep a good conscience, so that when some of them slander you as if you were wrongdoers because of your faith in Christ, they may notice your good deeds and be ashamed.
  
17 For it is better to suffer by doing good than by doing evil, if it is the will of God to test your perseverance.
  
18 For Christ also suffered once for sins, by being righteous for the unrighteous, in order to bring us to God.
  
He has been put to death in the flesh, but made alive in the spirit.
  
19 In which also he went and preached to the spirits who are in prison.
  
20 Those who were disobedient in the past, when God waited patiently in the days of Noah, while the ark was being built, in which a few people, that is to say, eight persons, were saved through water.
  
21 This water was a likeness of baptism, which is not the purification from the defilements of the body, but the commitment of a good conscience towards God, and which now saves you also by the resurrection of Jesus Christ.
  
[That is to say, this water had acted on those eight persons in the same way that baptism initiated by John the Baptist does today, a baptism that consists of making known to some others persons that one has made an inner resolution to serve God by living according to certain principles.
  
And in our day, if a person acquires a good understanding of the message that Jesus Christ has created in the world and chooses to live according to that teaching, then that person is preserved in the spirit for eternal life just as those eight persons had been preserved in the flesh to repopulate the earth.]
  
22 He has gone into heaven and is at the right hand of God, who has subjected to him the angels, and the authorities, and the powers.
  
nd the authorities, and the powers.

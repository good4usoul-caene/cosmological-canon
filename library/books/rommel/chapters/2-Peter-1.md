﻿1 Simon Peter, a servant and an apostle of Jesus Christ, to those who have obtained a faith of the same value as ours, through the righteousness of our God and of the Savior Jesus Christ.
  
2 Grace and peace be multiplied to you through the knowledge of God and of Jesus our Lord.
  
3 As his divine power has given us everything that contributes to life and piety, through the knowledge of the one who has called us by his own glory and virtue.
  
4 By which he has given us his greatest and most precious promises [that is to say, all the characteristics of the Holy Spirit], so that through them you become partakers of the divine nature, by fleeing the corruption that is created in the world by covetousness.
  
5 For this very reason, make every effort to join virtue to your faith, and to join science to virtue.
  
6 And to join self-restraint to science, and to join patience to self-restraint, and to join piety to patience.
  
7 And to join brotherly kindness to piety, and to join love to brotherly kindness.
  
8 For if these things are in you, and are there in abundance, then they will keep you from being idle and unfruitful in the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.
  
9 But the one in whom these things are not is blind.
  
He cannot see far away, and he has forgotten the cleansing from his old sins.
  
10 Therefore, brothers, give the most diligence to confirm your calling and election.
  
For if you do these things, then you will never fall.
  
11 This is how you will be richly supplied for the entrance into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.
  
12 This is why I will not neglect to always remind you of these things, although you know them and are established in the present truth.
  
13 And I consider it my duty, as long as I am in this tabernacle [which is my earthly body], to keep you awake through warnings.
  
14 For I know that I will soon leave this body, as our Lord Jesus Christ has made known to me.
  
15 That is why I will do my best to ensure that you can always remember these things after my death.
  
16 For it was not by following cleverly devised fables that we have made known to you the power and the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, but by the fact that we have seen his majesty with our own eyes.
  
17 For he has received honor and glory from God the Father, when the excellent glory made him hear a voice saying: This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.
  
18 And we ourselves have heard this voice from heaven, when we were with him on the holy mountain.
  
19 And we have the word of prophecy made more certain, to which you do well to pay heed, as to a lamp shining in a dark place, until the dawn comes and the day star arises in your hearts.
  
20 Knowing first of all yourselves that no prophecy of scripture can be an object of private interpretation.
  
21 For it was not by the will of man that a prophecy was ever brought, but it was while being moved by the Holy Spirit that men have spoken from God.

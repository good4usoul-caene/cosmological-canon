﻿1 Beloved, this is now the second epistle that I write to you.
  
In one and the other, I seek to cause your healthy minds to stay awake through warnings.
  
2 So that you may remember the words that the holy prophets had spoken in the past, and the commandment of the Lord and Savior which has been taught to you by the apostles.
  
3 Knowing first of all that in the last days, scorners will come with their scoffing, following their own evil desires.
  
4 And they will say: Where is the promise of his coming.
  
For since the fathers fell asleep, all things continue as they were from the beginning of the creation.
  
5 They want to ignore the fact that the heavens were made long ago by the word of God, as well as the earth which was formed out of the water and by means of the water.
  
6 By which the world of that time was flooded and destroyed.
  
7 And the heavens and the earth that exist now are kept in store by the same word, reserved for the fire of the day of judgment and destruction of the ungodly.
  
8 Beloved, do not be ignorant of the following thing: one day is with the Lord like a thousand years, and a thousand years are like one day.
  
9 The Lord is not slow concerning his promise, as some understand slowness.
  
But he is patient with you, not wanting anyone to perish, but that everyone may come to repentance.
  
10 The day of the Lord will come like a thief.
  
The heavens will disappear with a great noise, and the elements will melt with a fervent heat, and the earth and everything done in it will be consumed.
  
11 Since all these things will be destroyed, what kind of people ought you to be.
  
You ought to live holy and godly lives.
  
12 Looking for the day of God, and desiring its coming, knowing that the heavens will be destroyed by fire, and the elements will melt with a fervent heat.
  
13 But according to his promise, we are waiting for new heavens and a new earth, where righteousness dwells.
  
14 Therefore, beloved, while you are waiting for these things, be diligent to be found by him in peace, spotless and blameless.
  
15 Keep in mind that the patience of our Lord is for your salvation, as our beloved brother Paul also written to you, according to the wisdom given him.
  
16 This is what he does in all his epistles where he speaks about these things, in which there are some things hard to understand, whose meaning is distorted by the ignorant and the unstedfast, as they do also with the other scriptures, for their own ruin.
  
17 Therefore, beloved, since you have been forewarned, be on your guard so that you may not be carried away by the error of the wicked, and thus fall from your own stedfastness.
  
18 But grow in the grace of the knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.
  
To him be glory now and forever.
  
Amen.
  
y now and forever. Amen.

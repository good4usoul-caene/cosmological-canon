1 I exhort therefore, first of all, to make prayers, supplications, intercessions, thanksgiving, for all men. 
2 Including kings and all those who have authority, so that we may live peaceful and quiet lives in all godliness and holiness. 
3 This is good, and pleases God our Savior. 
4 He wants all men to be saved, and to come to the knowledge of the truth. 
5 For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus who gave himself as a ransom for all. 
6 This is the testimony given in its proper time. 
7 And for which I have been appointed a preacher and an apostle, a teacher of the Gentiles concerning faith and truth. I am telling the truth, I am not lying. 
8 Therefore I want that the men pray everywhere, lifting up pure hands, without anger or evil thoughts. 
9 I also want the women to dress modestly, with decency and bashfulness, not adorning themselves with elaborate hairstyles, or gold, or pearls, or expensive clothes. 
10 But that they adorn themselves with good works, as befits women who choose to serve God. 
11 A woman should learn in quietness and in full submission. 
12 I do not permit a woman to teach or to have authority over a man [concerning the teaching of the knowledge of God], but she must remain silent.
13 For Adam was formed first, then Eve. 
14 And Adam was not the one who had been outwitted, but it was the woman who had been outwitted and became a sinner. 
15 She will nevertheless be saved by becoming a [spiritual] mother, if she perseveres modestly in faith, and in love, and in holiness.

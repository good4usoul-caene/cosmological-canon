1 Here are my exhortations to the elders among you, I who am a elder like them, a witness of the sufferings of Christ and a partaker of the glory that will be revealed.
  
2 Feed the flock of God that is among you.
  
Do not act as if this duty were a burden, but do it willingly.
  
Not for a dishonorable gain, but with dedication.
  
3 Not as if you were lords over the heritage of God, but as examples to imitate for the flock.
  
4 And when the chief Shepherd appears, you will receive an incorruptible crown of glory.
  
5 Likewise, you who are young, submit yourselves to the elders.
  
And all of you, in your mutual relationships, clothe yourselves with humility.
  
For God resists the proud, and he gives grace to the humble.
  
6 Humble yourselves therefore under the mighty hand of God [by submitting yourself to the demands of the gospel of Christ], so that he may exalt you in due time.
  
7 Give all your worries to him, because he cares for you.
  
8 Be sober, and watchful.
  
Your adversary, the devil, prowls like a roaring lion, seeking whom he may devour.
  
9 Resist him with a firm faith, knowing that the same sufferings are accomplished in your brothers who are in the world.
  
10 And the God of all grace, who has called you through Christ Jesus to his eternal glory, after you have suffered a little while, will perfect you, and establish you, and strengthen you, and make you steadfast.
  
11 To him be glory and dominion for ever and ever.
  
Amen.
  
12 It is by Silvain, who is in my eyes a faithful brother, that I have written to you briefly, to exhort you and to testify to you that this faith that has been announced to you is the true grace of God.
  
13 The church that is in Babylon, chosen together with you, salutes you.
  
And so does Mark my son.
  
14 Greet one another with a kiss of affection.
  
Peace be with all of you who are in Christ.
  
Amen.
  
o are in Christ. Amen.

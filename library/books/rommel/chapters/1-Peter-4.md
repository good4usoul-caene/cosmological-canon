1 Therefore, Christ having suffered for us in the flesh, you also should arm yourselves with that same thought.
  
For if someone accepts to suffer in the flesh [with the aim of remaining a righteous person and possibly to create a more concrete work that can have some use for strangers arbitrarily chosen in society], then he has killed his old self concerning sin.
  
2 And thus he no longer lives according to the lusts of men during the time that he has left to live in the flesh, but according to the will of God [through obedience to the teaching of the gospel of Jesus Christ].
  
3 For we know that we should no longer waste more time by continuing to walk according to the habits that we had in the past, when we did not have the knowledge of God as it has been revealed by Christ: debauchery, lusts, drunkenness, the excesses of eating and drinking, and abominable idolatries.
  
[Now Peter has been a companion of Jesus Christ, and he knows that his message will be read by both Jews and non-Jews.
  
All the Jews who have been instructed in the law given by Moses are not concerned with the totality of the habits that Peter describes here, but at least with the last thing mentioned.
  
For the gospel of Jesus Christ has revealed that the part of the law of Moses which could only be put into practice by the descendants of Jacob was actually abominable idolatries.]
  
4 And now, they find it strange that you do not rush with them into the same excess of debauchery, and they slander you.
  
5 They will give account to him who is ready to judge the living and the dead.
  
6 For the gospel has been proclaimed to the dead also, so that, having been judged like men in the flesh, they may live according to God in the spirit.
  
[Now Peter has said previously in this letter that Christ has been put to death in the flesh, and made alive in the spirit, in which also he went and preached to the spirits who are in prison.]
  
7 The end of all things is near.
  
Therefore be sober-minded, and give great importance to prayer.
  
[Nowadays, when the temple of the Jews is already destroyed, it is no longer a question of praying publicly to bring the twelve tribes of Jacob to the knowledge of the faith explained to non-Jews, but it is only a matter of praying frequently in one's heart to have the strength not to turn away from the holy commandment.]
  
8 But above all, love one another fervently.
  
For love will cover the multitude of sins.
  
[Now it is about always desiring the well-being of strangers arbitrarily chosen in society, and never plotting evil against anyone, and not discriminating against someone who cannot be publicly criticized for anything.
  
And evil is that which is intentional and unjustifiable in the eyes of any assembly of saints, and such an assembly knows that each person acts according to his particular knowledge and circumstances.]
  
9 Be welcoming and hospitable toward one another, without murmurings.
  
10 As good stewards of the various graces of God, let each of you place at the service of others the gift that he has received.
  
11 If anyone speaks, he should do so as if he were transmitting the oracles of God.
  
If anyone fulfills a ministry, let him fulfill it according to the strength that God is giving him [without seeking to deviate from the simplicity of the holy commandment in order to impress others], so that in all things God may be glorified through Jesus Christ, to whom belong glory and dominion for ever and ever.
  
Amen.
  
12 Beloved, regarding the furnace that is in your midst to test you, do not be surprised as if it were a strange thing.
  
13 Rejoice, on the contrary, for the fact that you are partakers of the sufferings of Christ, so that you may also rejoice and be glad when his glory appears.
  
14 If you are persecuted for the name of Christ [that is to say, because of your determination not to turn away from the teachings of the gospel], then you are blessed, because the spirit of glory which is the Spirit of God rests on you.
  
Those who do not have the true love of God in them speak evil of this state of mind which is yours, but as for you, you glorify it.
  
15 Indeed, let none of you suffer as a murderer, or as a thief, or as an evildoer, or as a meddler in the matters of others.
  
16 But if anyone suffers as a Christian, then let him not be ashamed, and he should rather glorify God in this name.
  
[Now the name of an entity in theology is not some literal expression, but rather the exact knowledge of the characteristics of the entity that is designated.
  
Since no apostle knew the exact definition of eternal life, in this text a Christian is simply a person who declares that Christ lived, and died, and was raised from the dead, and has ascended to heaven, all this for the sins of humanity.
  
And in addition to this declaration, such a person tries to live according to his understanding of the message of the gospel.]
  
17 For this is the time when judgment will begin at the house of God [that is to say, the Jewish people to which I belong will soon be judged, those of them who do not believe that Christ is the Lord, and will no longer have the possibility to rebuild the holy place whose existence is required by the law of Moses].
  
And if it begins with us [who had been the chosen people of God], then what will be the end of those who do not obey the gospel of God [when the glory of Christ appears at the end of time.]
  
18 And if the righteous saves himself with difficulty, then what will become of the ungodly and the sinner.
  
19 Therefore, let those who suffer according to the will of God entrust their souls to the faithful creator, by doing what is good.

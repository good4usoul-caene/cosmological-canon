﻿1 Praise Yahweh, you all the servants of Yahweh who stand by night in the house of Yahweh.
  
2 Lift up your hands toward the sanctuary, and bless Yahweh.
  
3 May Yahweh bless you out of Zion, he who made the heaven and the earth.

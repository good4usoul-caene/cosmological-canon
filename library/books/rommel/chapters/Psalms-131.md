﻿1 A psalm of David.
O Yahweh, I have neither a proud heart nor haughty eyes.
I do not set goals without having plans that I can perform to achieve them.
2 For I behave calmly like a weaned child who is with his mother.
My spirit within me is like that of a weaned child.
3 O Israel, put your hope in Yahweh from now on and forever.
ever.

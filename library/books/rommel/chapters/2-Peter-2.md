﻿1 There were false prophets among the people, and there will also be false teachers among you.
They will introduce pernicious sects, by denying the characteristics of the one who has redeemed them, and will thus bring a sudden ruin on themselves.
2 Many will follow them in their heresies, and the way of truth will be slandered because of them.
3 They will use feigned words designed by their covetousness in order to make you a merchandise [not only with regard to your possessions, but also with regard to your souls].
The judgment against these false teachers has been ready for a long time, and they will not escape destruction.
4 For if God did not spare the angels who sinned, and has cast them down into the depths of darkness, and is reserving them for judgment.
5 If he did not spare the ancient world, but has preserved Noah who was a preacher of righteousness, and seven others persons, when he brought the flood on a world of the ungodly.
6 If he condemned to destruction and reduced to ashes the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah, making them an example of what he will do to those who live in an ungodly way.
7 And if he saved the righteous Lot who was deeply saddened by the conduct of those wicked people.
8 For this righteous man, who dwelt among them, was tormented in his righteous soul daily because of what he saw and heard of their criminal deeds.
9 The Lord knows how to save the pious from troubles, and to reserve the unrighteous for the punishment which he will execute on the day of judgment.
10 And chiefly those who walk according to the flesh in a desire for impurity, and those who despise authority.
Bold and arrogant, they are not afraid to insult the dignities.
11 While the angels, who are superior in strength and power, do not make scornful accusations against them before the Lord.
12 But they, like brutes who abandon themselves to their natural tendencies and who were born to be taken and destroyed, they speak insultingly of what they do not know, and they will perish by their own corruption, and thus receive the reward for their iniquity.
13 They find their delights in indulging in pleasure in broad daylight.
Corrupt and defiled men who delight in their deceptions, and they want you to participate in this.
14 Their eyes are full of adultery, and cannot cease from sin.
They entice unsteadfast souls, their hearts are trained in greed, they are the children of the curse.
15 Having forsaken the right way, they have gone astray following the way of Balaam the son of Bozor, who loved the wage of unrighteousness.
16 But he was rebuked for his transgression.
A dumb donkey spoke with the voice of a man and stopped the madness of the prophet.
17 Those false teachers are like wells without water, like clouds carried away by a whirlwind.
The blackness of darkness is reserved for them.
18 With speeches full of vanity, they entice by the lusts of the flesh, through recklessness, those who have scarcely escaped from persons who live in error.
19 They promise freedom to their victims, while they themselves are slaves to corruption.
For every person is a slave of that which has triumphed over him.
20 For if they have escaped from the defilements of the world through the knowledge of the Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, and if they choose in their spirit to return to it [by resuming taking initiatives without having publicly acceptable justifications in the heart], then they will be defeated and their last condition will be worse than the first one.
21 For it would have been better for them not to have known the way of righteousness, rather than to turn away, after having known it, from the holy commandment which had been properly explained to them.
22 It has happened to them according to this true proverb: the dog has returned to what he had vomited, and the sow that had washed herself has wallowed in the mire.

1 Now the Spirit expressly says that in the last times some will depart from the faith, to cling to seducing spirits and doctrines of demons.
  
2 Through the hypocrisy of false teachers bearing the mark of withering in their own conscience.
  
3 Prescribing not to marry, and to abstain from foods which God created to be taken with thanksgiving by those who are faithful and have known the truth.
  
4 For all that God has created is good, and nothing that is edible by some people can be harmful to others, provided it is taken with thanksgiving.
  
5 For everything is sanctified by the word of God and by prayer.
  
6 By telling these things to the brothers, you will be a good minister of Jesus Christ, nourished by the words of the faith and of the good doctrine that you have been properly following.
  
7 Reject profane and absurd tales.
  
Practice piety.
  
8 For bodily exercise is useful for a few things, but godliness is useful for everything, having the promise of the present life and of that which is to come.
  
9 It is a true statement that is entirely worthy to be received.
  
10 Indeed, we work and we fight because we put our hope in the living God, who is the Savior of all men, especially of believers.
  
11 Declare these things, and teach them.
  
12 Let no one despise your youth, but be an example to those who have believed, in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith, in purity.
  
13 Until I come, devote yourself to reading, and to exhortation, and to teaching.
  
14 Do not neglect the gift that is in you, which was given to you by prophecy with the laying on of hands of the assembly of elders.
  
15 Take care of these things, give yourself entirely to them, so that your progress may be evident to everyone.
  
16 Watch over yourself and your teaching, persevere in these things.
  
For by doing this you will save yourself and those who listen to you [and obey you].

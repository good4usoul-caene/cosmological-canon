1 See what great love the Father has lavished on us, so that we should be called children of God.
  
And that is what we are, and that is why the world does not know us, because the world has not known him.
  
2 Beloved, we are now children of God, and what we will be has not yet been revealed.
  
But we know that when Christ appears, we will be like him, and we will see him as he is.
  
3 Everyone who has this hope inside himself purifies himself, just as Christ is pure.
  
4 Everyone who commits a sin transgresses the law.
  
For sin is the transgression of the law.
  
[Now this means that the notion of sin has changed over time with regard to the children of Jacob who received a divine law, because they received commandments that could not have existed before the arrival of Moses.
  
For example, the commandment that says a daughter who receives an inheritance from her father is obligated to marry someone belonging to the same tribe as her father, among the twelve tribes of Israel who have received inheritances.
  
Concerning the twelve sons of Jacob, Levi did not receive an inheritance and Joseph is divided into two tribes.]
  
5 And you know that Christ has been manifested to take away sins, and in him there is no sin.
  
6 So whoever dwells in him does not sin.
  
Whoever sins has not seen him and has not known him.
  
[That is to say, a person who lives in Christ does not continue to sin from the moment when the conscience of that person truly decided to convert.
  
The one who  continues to sin has never really understood the teaching of Jesus Christ, and thus have never known him, or has never really accepted in himself to live according to what has been clearly explained to him.]
  
7 Little children, do not let anyone lead you astray.
  
Whoever acts righteously is righteous, just as he is righteous.
  
[That is to say, if a person possesses within himself publicly acceptable justifications whenever he do something, then he is a righteous person, and this is the only divine law explained by Christ.]
  
8 The one who sins belongs to the devil [even if this is caused by the fact that the knowledge of the truth has not yet been explained to him].
  
For the devil has been sinning from the beginning.
  
This is why the Son of God has been manifested, in order to destroy the works of the devil.
  
9 Whoever is born of God does not continue to sin, because the seed of God dwells in him.
  
He cannot continue to sin, because he is born of God.
  
10 This is how we know who are the children of God and who are the children of the devil.
  
Anyone who does not act righteously is not of God, nor anyone who does not love his brother.
  
[Now your brother is just your neighbor.
  
He who does not love his brother is he who cannot publicly justify the harm that he wants to do to his brother.]
  
11 For the message that you have heard from the beginning is that we must love one another.
  
12 And not be like Cain, who was of the evil one and murdered his brother.
  
And why did he murder him.
  
It was because his own deeds were evil, while those of his brother were righteous.
  
13 Do not be surprised, brothers, if the world hates you.
  
14 We know that we have passed from death to life, because we love the brothers.
  
Anyone who does not love his brother remains in death.
  
15 Anyone who hates his brother is a murderer.
  
And you know that no murderer has eternal life dwelling in him.
  
[Evil is that which is intentional and unjustifiable in the eyes of any assembly of saints, and such an assembly knows that each one acts according to his particular knowledge and circumstances.]
  
16 We have perceived love in the fact that Jesus Christ has given his life for us.
  
Therefore we too must give our lives for the brothers [that is to say, for the well-being of arbitrarily chosen strangers in society, and this in a way that takes priority over the well-being of those whom we personally love and over our own well-being].
  
17 If anyone has material possessions, and sees that his brother is in need, and withholds compassion from that brother, then how can the love of God be in him.
  
18 My little children, let us not love in words or in discourses, but in deeds and in truth.
  
19 It is by doing so that we will know that we are of the truth, and we will reassure our hearts before him.
  
20 For if our heart condemns us, then God is greater than our heart, and he knows all things.
  
21 Beloved, if our heart does not condemn us, then we have confidence before God.
  
22 And whatever we ask, we receive it from him, because we keep his commandments and do only what is pleasing in his sight.
  
23 And this is his commandment: we must believe in the name of his Son Jesus Christ, and love one another as he commanded us.
  
24 Whoever keeps his commandments dwells in God, and God in him.
  
And we know that he dwells in us by the Spirit whom he has given us.
  
[Now the Spirit is the spirit of Christ, and here the apostle explains that a true disciple receives through his own spirit the certainty that he is inhabited by God who is teaching him in real time how to behave.]

1 Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the elect who are scattered throughout Pontus, and Galatia, and Cappadocia, and Asia, and Bithynia.
  
2 Chosen according to the foreknowledge of God the Father through the sanctification made by the Spirit, for obedience to Jesus Christ and for the sprinkling of his blood: May grace and peace be multiplied to you.
  
3 Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has caused us to be born again for a living hope according to his great mercy, through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead.
  
4 To obtain an inheritance that can neither be corrupted nor defiled, and that does not fade away, reserved in heaven for you.
  
[That is to say, none of the works that you do can be taken away from you since God has a perfect knowledge of the exact genealogy of everything that exists.
  
It is a reality that is impossible to falsify, and this also concerns all the good works that you do through your faith in Jesus Christ.]
  
5 You who, by the power of God, are guarded through your faith for a salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.
  
6 This is what makes your joy despite the fact that, since it is necessary, you now are saddened for a short time by various trials.
  
7 So that the genuineness of your faith, being more precious than gold which will perish at the end of time despite the fact that it can now be refined by fire, may result in praise, and glory, and honor, at the return of Jesus Christ.
  
8 Whom you love without having seen him [in the flesh], in whom you are believing despite the fact that you are not seeing him with your eyes right now.
  
9 And you are filled with an ineffable and glorious joy, because you know that you will obtain the salvation of your souls as the reward for your faith.
  
10 The prophets, who prophesied concerning the grace that was reserved for you, made this salvation the object of their searches and investigations.
  
11 Trying to find out the time and circumstances to which the spirit of Christ within them was pointing, when it testified beforehand to the sufferings of Christ and the glory that should follow.
  
12 And it was revealed to them that those things were not for themselves, but that they were serving you when they spoke of the things that have now been announced to you by those who have preached the gospel to you through the Holy Spirit sent from heaven, things into which the angels desire to plunge their gaze.
  
13 Therefore, gird up the waist of your mind, be sober-minded, and set your hope fully on that grace that will be yours when Jesus Christ comes.
  
14 As obedient children, do not conform to the evil desires that you had when you lived in ignorance [that is to say, when you had not yet understood the correct definition of faith].
  
15 And just as he who has called you is holy, so you also must be holy in all that you do.
  
16 As it is written: You will be holy, for I am holy.
  
17 And if you invoke as your Father the one who judges impartially according to the deeds of each person, then conduct yourselves with fear during the time of your pilgrimage.
  
18 Knowing that it was not with things that can be defiled, like silver or gold, that you have been redeemed from your vain manner of life which you have copied from your ancestors.
  
19 But with the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot.
  
20 Chosen before the creation of the world, but revealed only in these last times because of you.
  
21 And it is through him that you are believers in God, the one who raised him from the dead and gave him glory so that your faith and hope may be in God.
  
22 So then, having purified your souls by obeying the truth to have a sincere brotherly love, love one another fervently from the heart.
  
23 Since you have been born again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible seed, by the living and permanent word of God.
  
24 For all flesh is like grass, and all its glory is like the flower of the grass.
  
The grass withers, and the flower falls.
  
25 But the word of the Lord endures forever, and it is that word that is preached to you.

1 Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the commandment of God our Savior and of Jesus Christ our hope.
2 To Timothy, my true son in the faith.
May grace and mercy and peace be given to you from God the Father and Jesus Christ our Lord.
3 I remind you of the exhortation that I made to you, on my departure for Macedonia, when I committed you to staying at Ephesus in order to recommend to certain people not to teach another doctrine.
4 And not to give heed to fables and endless genealogies, which produce discussions rather than advancing the work of God concerning faith.
5 The aim of the commandment is love coming from a pure heart, and from a good conscience, and from a sincere faith.
6 Some have turned away from these things, and have gone astray in vain speeches.
7 They want to be teachers of the law, but they understand neither what they say nor what they affirm.
8 As for we, we know that the law is good, provided that it is used in an appropriate manner.
9 Knowing well that the law is not made for the righteous, but for the wicked and the rebellious, for the impious and the sinners, for the irreligious and the profane, for the parricides and the murderers.
10 And for fornicators, for the vile, for the slave traders, for the liars, for the perjurers, and all that is contrary to the healthy doctrine.
11 In accordance with the gospel of the glory of the blessed God, the gospel which has been entrusted to me.
12 I give thanks to the one who has strengthened me, namely Jesus Christ our Lord, for having judged me faithful by establishing me in the ministry.
13 I who was previously a blasphemer, and a persecutor, and a violent man.
But I have received mercy, because I was acting ignorantly in unbelief.
14 Then the grace of our Lord has abounded in me, through the faith and the love that is in Christ Jesus.
15 Here is a true statement that is entirely worthy to be received: Jesus Christ came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am the first.
16 And I have obtained mercy, so that Jesus Christ may show all his longsuffering in me who is the first, so that I may serve as an example to those who will believe in him for everlasting life.
17 To the everlasting king, immortal, invisible, God the only wise, to him be honor and glory, for ever and ever.
Amen.
18 My command to you, my son Timothy, according to the prophecies previously made concerning you, it is that, according to them, you must fight the good fight by keeping faith and a good conscience.
19 Some have turned away from this way of behaving, and have been shipwrecked in relation to the faith.
20 Among them are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I have delivered to Satan, so that they may learn not to blaspheme.
earn not to blaspheme.

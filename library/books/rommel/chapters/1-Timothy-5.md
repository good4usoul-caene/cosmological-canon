1 Do not rebuke an elder man harshly, but exhort him as a father.
  
And admonish younger men as brothers.
  
2 And behave yourself with elder women as with mothers, and with younger women as with sisters, with all purity.
  
3 Honor widows who really need help.
  
4 If a widow has children or grandchildren, let them first learn to show godliness to their own family, and to repay their parents.
  
For this is pleasing in the sight of God.
  
5 Now the widow who really need help, the one who has been left all alone, puts her hope in God and continues night and day in supplications and prayers.
  
6 But the woman who gives herself to pleasure is dead, even while she is still alive.
  
7 Teach them these things, so that they may be blameless.
  
8 If anyone does not provide for his relatives, namely for his own household, then he has denied the faith and is worse than an unbeliever.
  
9 A widow must be at least sixty years old to be given a position in the church, having been the wife of one husband.
  
10 And she must be well-known for her good works, such as bringing up children, showing hospitality to strangers, washing the feet of the saints, relieving the afflicted, devoting herself to every kind of good work.
  
11 But refuse the young widows.
  
For when lust separates them from Christ, they want to marry.
  
12 And thus they become guilty by breaking their first covenant.
  
13 Besides that, they become idle and learn to go from house to house.
  
And not only they are idle, but they are also gossips and busybodies, saying things they ought not to say.
  
14 Therefore I want the younger women to marry, and to have children, and to manage their households, and to give the adversary no occasion to condemn them.
  
15 For some have already turned away to follow Satan.
  
16 If a man or woman among believers has widows, then he or she should assist those widows, so that the church may not be burdened and may assist the widows who truly need help.
  
17 Let the elders who rule well be considered worthy of double honor, especially those who do the work of counseling and teaching.
  
18 For the scripture says, 'You will not muzzle the ox when it treads out the grain', and, 'The laborer deserves his wages'.
  
19 Do not receive an accusation against an elder, except by the testimony of two or three witnesses.
  
20 Concerning those who sin, rebuke them publicly so that the others may also be warned.
  
21 I adjure you before God, before Jesus Christ, and before the elect angels, to observe these instructions without prejudice, doing nothing out of favoritism.
  
22 Do not lay hands on anyone in haste, do not participate in the sins of others in this way.
  
Keep yourself pure.
  
23 Stop drinking only water, and drink a little wine for the sake of your stomach and your frequent ailments.
  
24 The sins of some persons are obvious even before they are judged, while the sins of others persons are seen only later.
  
25 Likewise, some good deeds are quickly discernible, but the others good deeds will not stay hidden forever.

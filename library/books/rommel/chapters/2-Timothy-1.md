﻿1 Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, according to the promise of the life that is in Christ Jesus.
  
2 To Timothy my beloved son: Grace, and mercy, and peace, from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.
  
3 I thank God, whom my ancestors served and whom I serve with a pure conscience, because I always remember you in my prayers night and day.
  
4 Remembering your tears, and desiring to see you so that I may be filled with joy.
  
5 For I remember the sincere faith that is in you.
  
That kind of faith first dwelt in your grandmother Lois and in your mother Eunice, and now I am persuaded that it dwells in you also.
  
6 Therefore I urge you to revive the gift of God that you have received through the laying on of my hands.
  
7 For God has not given us a spirit of fearfulness, but of power, and of love, and of a discipline.
  
8 Therefore do not be ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, nor of me his prisoner.
  
But be partaker of the afflictions that the power of God allows us to endure for the gospel.
  
9 God has saved us by calling us with a holy calling, not because of our works, but because of his own purpose, and because of his grace which was given us in Christ Jesus before the world began.
  
10 And which has now been revealed through the coming of our Savior Jesus Christ, who has destroyed death, and has brought life and immortality to light through the gospel.
  
11 It is for this gospel that I have been appointed a preacher, and an apostle, and a teacher of the Gentiles.
  
12 That is why I am suffering now.
  
But I am not ashamed, because I know the one in whom I have believed, and I am persuaded that he is able to protect what I have entrusted to him until that day [that is to say, he is able of protecting my soul until the day of final judgment].
  
13 Hold fast the pattern of healthy words which you have heard from me, in the faith and the love that are in Christ Jesus.
  
14 Keep the good treasure that has been entrusted to you, guard it with the help of the Holy Spirit who dwells in us.
  
15 You know that all those who are in Asia have abandoned me, including Phygelus and Hermogenes.
  
16 May the Lord show mercy to the household of Onesiphorus.
  
For he often comforted me, and was not ashamed of my chains.
  
17 On the contrary, when he came to Rome, he sought me earnestly and found me.
  
18 May the Lord grant him to obtain mercy from the Lord on that day.
  
You know better than anyone how much he served me in Ephesus.
  
served me in Ephesus.

1 My little children, I write these things to you so that you may not sin.
  
And if anyone sin, then we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous one.
  
2 He is now the only way by which our sins are taken away.
  
And this does not only concern our sins, we who are Jews, but also the sins of all the people in the world.
  
3 If we practice his commandments, then we know that we have known him.
  
4 Whoever says, 'I know him', but does not practice his commandments, the same is a liar and the truth is not in him.
  
5 But if someone obeys his word, then the love of God is truly perfect in that person.
  
This is how we know that we are in him.
  
6 Whoever want to dwell in him must also walk as he himself has walked.
  
7 Beloved, what I write to you is not a new commandment, but an ancient commandment which you have had from the beginning.
  
This ancient commandment is the word that you have heard from the beginning.
  
8 Nevertheless I am writing you a new commandment, and this fact is true in him and in you [that is to say, what he has asked you to do is a thing that was from the beginning, but his teaching nullifies the other commandments that you have received over time and that have influenced his behavior].
  
For the darkness is passing away, and the true light is already shining.
  
9 If a person thinks that he is in the light, and hates his brother, then such a person is still in the darkness.
  
10 Whoever loves his brother dwells in the light, and there is no occasion of evil in him.
  
[Now your brother is just your neighbor.]
  
11 But whoever hates his brother is in the darkness.
  
He walks in the darkness, and does not know where he is going, because the darkness has blinded his eyes.
  
12 I am writing to you, my little children, because your sins are forgiven through him.
  
13 I am writing to you, fathers, because you have known the one who is from the beginning.
  
I am writing to you, young men, because you have overcome the evil one.
  
14 I have written to you, little children, because you have known the Father.
  
I have written to you, fathers, because you have known the one who is from the beginning.
  
I have written to you, young men, because you are strong, and the word of God dwells in you, and you have overcome the evil one.
  
15 Do not love the world, nor the things that are in the world.
  
If anyone loves the world, then the love of the Father is not in him.
  
16 For all that is in the world, the lust of the flesh, and the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life, comes not from the Father but from the world.
  
17 And the world passes away, and its lust also, but whoever does the will of God remains forever.
  
18 Little children, this is the last hour.
  
And as you have heard that the antichrist is coming, even now there are already many antichrists.
  
This is how we know that it is the last hour.
  
19 They went out from us, but they were not of us.
  
For if they had been of us, then they would have continued with us.
  
And it has happened so that it may be manifested that not all are of us.
  
20 As for you, you have received the anointing from the one who is holy, and you all have knowledge.
  
21 I have written to you, not because you do not know the truth, but because you know it and because no lie comes from the truth.
  
22 Who is a liar, if not the one who denies that Jesus is the Christ [who is foretold in the scriptures].
  
Such a person is an antichrist, who denies the Father and the Son.
  
23 Whoever denies the Son does not have the Father either.
  
Whoever acknowledges the Son has the Father also.
  
24 Let what you have heard from the beginning dwell in you.
  
If what you have heard from the beginning dwells in you, then you also will dwell in the Son and in the Father.
  
25 And what he has promised to us, it is eternal life.
  
26 I have written these things to you because of those who seek to lead you astray.
  
27 And I say that the anointing that you have received from him dwells in you, and you do not need anyone to teach you.
  
For since that anointing teaches you about all things, and its teaching is not a lie but is according to the truth, so you must remain in him just as he teaches you.
  
28 And now, little children, remain in him so that when he appears, we may be confident and unashamed before him at his coming.
  
29 If you know that he is righteous, then acknowledge that everyone who acts righteously is born of him.
  
[That is to say, if you know that God the creator of all things is righteous, then acknowledge that whoever practices justice is inhabited by his holy spirit and is loved by him in the same way.]

ï»¿1 Remind them to be submitted to rulers and authorities, to be obedient and ready to do any good work.
  
[Indeed, a disciple of Christ has the obligation to seek the well-being of others in everything he does, and consequently to disobey any order that is given by any person having a certain authority if that order consists of harming others without publicly acceptable justification, as had been done by the midwives who lived in the kingdom of Egypt (Exodus 1:6-14,15-21).]
  
2 Not to slander anyone, to be peaceable, and gentle, and kind to everyone.
  
3 For we ourselves were once foolish, disobedient, deceivers, slaves of various passions and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hated by others and hating one another.
  
4 But when the one who is the goodness and the love for mankind of God our Savior has appeared.
  
5 God has saved us, not because of righteous things that we had done, but because of his mercy.
  
He has saved us through the washing of regeneration which has renewed our holy spirit.
  
[That is to say, we who had within us a certain form of love for justice were inhabited by a holy spirit of ignorance, and now he has saved us by enabling us to acquire the holy spirit of knowledge.
  
He has enabled us to understand what needs to be done to become a righteous person.]
  
6 Which he has poured out on us richly, through Jesus Christ our savior.
  
7 In order that, being justified by his grace, we become heirs according to the hope of eternal life.
  
8 It is a sure teaching and I want you to affirm these things regularly, so that those who have believed in God may be encouraged to practice good works.
  
This is what is good and useful to men.
  
9 But avoid foolish controversies, and genealogies, and quarrels, and the struggles about what is taught by the law given by Moses [in these days when the temple of God built to practice the Jewish rituals is not yet destroyed].
  
For they are useless and will not help anyone.
  
10 After a first and second warning, separate yourself from anyone who causes divisions.
  
11 Knowing that such a person has a corrupt mind, and he is sinning and condemning himself.
  
12 When I send Artemas or Tychicus to you, come quickly to me at Nicopolis.
  
For I have decided to spend the winter there.
  
13 Help Zenas the lawyer and Apollos to prepare their journey, so that they lack nothing.
  
14 Those who are among us to assist us in teaching the word of God must also learn to devote themselves to doing good works to meet urgent needs, so that they may not be unfruitful.
  
15 All those who are with me send you greetings.
  
Greet those who love us in the faith.
  
Grace be with you all.
  
Amen.
  
e be with you all. Amen.

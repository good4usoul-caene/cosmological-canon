﻿1 Prophetic song.
  
We will be like those who dream, when Yahweh brings back the captives of Zion.
  
2 Our mouth will be filled with laughter, and our tongue with singing.
  
And the peoples of the nations will say: Yahweh has done great things for them.
  
3 Indeed, Yahweh will do great things for us and we will be happy.
  
4 Bring back prosperity, O Yahweh.
  
Make us feel like an arid land on which rivers are brought.
  
5 Now, those who sow while being sad will reap while being happy.
  
6 He who carries the seeds despite the troubles that make him weep, will joyfully carry the sheaves.

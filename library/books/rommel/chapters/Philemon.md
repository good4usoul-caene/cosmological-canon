ï»¿1 Paul a prisoner of Jesus Christ, and Timothy our brother, to Philemon our dear friend and fellow worker.
  
2 To our dear sister Apphia, and to Archippus our fellow soldier, and to the church that is in your house.
  
3 Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
  
4 I thank my God, always mentioning you in my prayers.
  
5 For I hear of your faith in the Lord Jesus, and of your love for all the saints.
  
6 I ask him that the fellowship of your faith may be efficacious for the cause of Christ, through all the good things that are in you.
  
7 For I have had much joy and comfort because of your love, brother, because the hearts of the saints have been pacified by your work.
  
8 Therefore, although I have in Christ all boldness to command to do what is befitting.
  
9 Nevertheless I will rather implore you out of love, being as I am, Paul an old man, and now also a prisoner for Jesus Christ.
  
10 I implore you for my son Onesimus, whom I have begotten while being in chains.
  
[That is to say, this is someone whom I have converted to the faith in Jesus while I was in prison.]
  
11 He was useless to you in the past, but now he is useful both for you and me.
  
12 So I am sending him back to you.
  
Receive him therefore, he who is my own bowels [that is to say, he who is as a part of myself].
  
13 I would have preferred to keep him with me, so that he might serve me instead of you while I am in chains for the gospel.
  
14 But I have not wanted to do anything without your consent, so that your good deed would not be forced but voluntary.
  
15 Perhaps he had been separated from you for a time, so he may come back and stay with you forever.
  
16 No longer as a servant, but more than a servant, as a beloved brother.
  
That is what he is to me, and I know that he will be more esteemed in your eyes, not only as a brother in faith in the Lord, but also because of his own personality.
  
17 So if you consider me to be your friend, then receive him as myself.
  
18 And if he has wronged you in any way, or if he owes you anything, then consider that it comes from me.
  
19 I Paul, I am writing this thing with my own hand: I will repay it, not to tell you that you owe yourself to me.
  
20 Yes, brother, let me have this joy from you in the Lord.
  
Refresh my bowels in Christ [that is to say, refresh my heart in Christ].
  
21 I am writing this thing to you because I have confidence in your obedience, knowing that you will do even more than I say.
  
22 And prepare accommodation for me, because I hope to be restored to you through your prayers.
  
23 Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Jesus Christ, sends you greetings.
  
24 And also Mark, and Aristarchus, and Demas, and Luke, my fellow workers.
  
25 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.
  
Amen.

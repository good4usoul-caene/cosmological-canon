﻿1 Then Bildad the Shuhite spoke in the following words.
  
2 Power and terror belong to God.
  
He makes peace in his high places.
  
3 On whom does his light not rise.
  
Are not his armies innumerable.
  
4 And how can a man be righteous before God.
  
How can he who is born of a woman be pure.
  
5 Behold he gives no light to the moon, and the stars are not pure in his sight.
  
6 How much more man who is only a worm, and the son of the man who is only a worm.
  
a worm.

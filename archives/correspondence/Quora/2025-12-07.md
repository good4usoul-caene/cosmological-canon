# Regarding Paul’s warning in 2 Corinthians 11:3–4:**

1. **Chronology matters.** Paul wrote this letter around AD 55–56. At that time, the Gospel of John had not yet been composed — most scholars place John between AD 75–100. To claim that Paul was defending John’s gospel is only pretending; it is not consistent with the chronology. John’s gospel describes exactly the “other Jesus” Paul warns against. Yet I follow this “other Jesus” in the Spirit of Truth that he himself asked for in John 16–17.

2. **The gospels themselves present Jesus differently.**  
   - **Prophecy of Isaiah**  
     - Matthew’s Jesus is rooted in Jewish prophecy, citing Isaiah with *“this was to fulfill…”*. Matthew (and likewise Mark and Luke) leave the “otherwise” clauses intact, preserving the possibility of layered wisdom.  
     - John’s Jesus references Isaiah (John 12:38–41), but prophecy becomes inevitability. In John, the “otherwise” is erased, leaving no room for alternative wisdom.  
   - **On Love**  
     - Matthew’s ethic is inclusive: *“Love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you”* (Matt 5:44).  
     - John’s ethic is exclusive: *“Love each other as I have loved you”* (John 13:34), directed inward to disciples.  
   - **On Heaven/Hell**  
     - Matthew’s vision of judgment is the goat/sheep rubric (Matt 25:31–46), where entry depends on deeds of compassion.  
     - John’s vision is the “believe in the name” rubric (John 3:18; 20:31), where salvation depends on faith in Jesus’ identity.  
   - **On Apologist Gaslighting**  
     - Apologists may claim John’s gospel is “just another point‑of‑view.” But point‑of‑view does not change what someone says. John’s Jesus is not simply another angle; he is another voice.

3. **I am not preaching another Messiah.** The mistake lies with Christians who conflate Jesus of John’s gospel with Jesus of the synoptics. My task is to make clear the distinction between:  
   - **Immanuel‑Messiah‑Jesus‑of‑Nazareth**  
   - **Bara Bas; Bar Abbas** (Creation’s Daughter, Son of the Father, Son of Man)  
   - **Maher Shalal Hash Baz — John the Baptist, John the Apostle** (Swift to the spoils)  

Only then can we PREACH the Messiah’s instructions, while recognizing that the work of the other two is not instruction but test.

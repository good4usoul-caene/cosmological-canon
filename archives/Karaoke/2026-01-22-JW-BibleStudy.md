### Thursday, Jan 24,2026

I'd like to discuss my Bible Study Meeting with two from Jehovah's Witnesses the other day.  Before they came I suggested Acts 19, John 1, and John 20.  

Acts 19 because I wanted to focus on how you can't cast out demons by using Jesus through the lens of Paul.  John 1 because, well, that's where John starts.  John 20 because this is where I find John to be the most problematic.

However, once they got here, for some reason we got stuck on John 2.
John 2 has:

 - Three temptations of Matthew 4, Luke 4, become indictments in John 2.
   - Bread from Stone = Water to wine
   - Temple to Angels = Crucifixion to resurrection
   - Devil Worship --> All Kingdoms = Jesus testifies against the human heart --> All Believe in his name

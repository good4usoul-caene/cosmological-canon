On Thursday, December 11, 2025 I went to [The Cafe](<https://www.facebook.com/search/top?q=the%20cafe%20macomb%20il>) and elected to participate in the Open Mic night, even though I didn't have an instrument.

During my first set, I sang acapella, most of 
 - [It's Gonna Rain](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jd3Fka2oISA&list=RDjd3Fka2oISA&start_radio=1), by the Violent Femmes
 - [Sailor's Prayer](<https://lyrics.gordonbok.com/disc/sailorsprayer.html>), which I heard performed by a band at a renaissance fair, and I can't remember what they were called.
 
During my second set, I had [Kyra Dean](<https://www.facebook.com/share/v/17Zt8B4mP2/>) play the guitar for me.
 - *Save Us from the Wrath of the Lamb* by Jonathan Doolin
 - *Hallelujah* by Leonard Cohen
 
During my third set, I sang a bit of the first song Acapella, and then was helped by [Shawn Scott](https://www.facebook.com/share/v/16rQb3JA9E/) to sing the second song
 - *Oliver Cromwell*, by the Pogues 
 - *No Rain* by Blind Melon
 
By my request, [Megan Maroney](<https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1AJcduhw5M/>) played two Aurora songs.  
 - [Runaway](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d_HlPboLRL8&list=RDd_HlPboLRL8&start_radio=1>)
 - [The Seed](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Mc_OM5oNA8&list=RD_Mc_OM5oNA8&start_radio=1>)

I got off the train from Troy, MI in Macomb, at around 9:30 pm.  I drove over to Jim's to say hi to the gamers.  They were just packing up Civilization, I wasn't feeling 100% but they encouraged me to go for Karaoke anyway, which I'm glad I did, because I had a total blast.

| Song | Performance|
|------|------------|
|Wagon Wheel:<BR>Darius Rucker|I guess I looked a little nervous.  When I sat down, someone told me I did a really good job.  I think sometimes country music songs make me nervous because I always feel there ought to be something more to the melody that I'm missing.  The songs in my repertoire tend to have more intricate melodies.  How does this song do so much with intervals of one step or less?  Must be the guitar?|
|*Under the Bridge*: Red Hot Chili Peppers |Still another great song.  I felt like the crowd really loved it.  |
|*I'm Alright*<BR>Kenny Loggins|This was a lot of fun.  There was one bit in the middle that I wasn't quite familiar with, but overall it went great.|
|Complicated; <BR>Avril Lavigne|There was a table of people who had said I sounded great on Wagon Wheel and Under the Bridge.  I pulled out a big list of songs that I'd made on the train (because my phone was going dead).  Complicated was on the list, and this lady said "this is one of my favorite songs".  But I think I put it on my list without practicing it.  Anyway I went for it.  The first couple verses were touch and go, but once we got to the chorus I was on it.|
|Bad Moon Rising:  Credence Clearwater Revival||
|Little Talks: Of Monsters and Men|This was a duet with Delaney.  It went fantastic.  Joey said we sounded better than the original artists.|

Okay, there's a lot of just "It went fantastic" in there.  I just really felt the love the other night.  Gave so many hugs, and watched a lot of joyful people.  

There was one guy who came down from Quad Cities for Karaoke.  He sang a couple songs...  Sang???  Well, when he did sing he sounded pretty good, but mostly he was screaming into the microphone.  I chatted with him a little bit; he said he had autism, which is a little different for everybody.  He was going to get a red bull, and I bought it for him.  When he did his second song, something about it tickled me and got me laughing pretty good.  

Megan Malone sang "The hook brings you back" which is a song I've heard, but didn't realize the lyrics were so funny/fascinating.  

There are two women who have been singing duets together forever; one of them is in a wheelchair.  But I haven't ever really approached them to talk.  Last night, though, I went up after they sang "Fight Song" and said, you guys have been coming in here every time I've been here, and yet I've never offered you a hug...  Do you mind if I give you one?  So I leaned in and oops, leaned a little on the joystick at first, but once her wheelchair stopped darting around, I gave her a big hug.  The other girl asked if I would do "picture" with her, but I wasn't sure if I knew it, so I said I'd practice for next week.  (Turned out once I put it on I was pretty much completely familiar with the tune.)
